# CATCHER'S UNIVERSITY BASEBALL 12U FINISHES SECOND AT THE 2018 NATIONS WORLD SERIES

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
 CATCHER'S UNIVERSITY BASEBALL 12U FINISHES SECOND AT THE 2018 NATIONS 
                              WORLD SERIES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 10, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Catcher's 
University Baseball 12U team from Katy, TX for winning second place at 
the 2018 Nations World Series.
  Catcher's University Baseball 12U team, under the coaching of head 
manager Rick Ruff, capped off a season of 34 wins, 9 losses and 2 ties 
with a second place finish at the 2018 Nations World Series. Team 
members T.K. Rollins, Blake Davila, Tanner Reese, Ty Garrett, Myles 
Jackson, Jackson Cleveland, Pablo Lazcano Jr., Jose DeJongh, Christian 
Jaquay, Kaden Reising, O.D. Denner, Charlie Radley, Aaron Brashear and 
Drew Leuders are all 11 and 12 years old. Their hard work has brought 
great pride to our Katy community and will be remembered by many for 
years to come.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Catcher's University Baseball 12U team from 
Katy, TX for winning second place at the 2018 Nations World Series. I 
look forward to seeing what y'all accomplish in the future on and off 
the field.

                          ____________________
```
