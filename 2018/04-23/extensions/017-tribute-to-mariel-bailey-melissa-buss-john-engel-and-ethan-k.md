# TRIBUTE TO MARIEL BAILEY, MELISSA BUSS, JOHN ENGEL AND ETHAN KOZAK

```
   TRIBUTE TO MARIEL BAILEY, MELISSA BUSS, JOHN ENGEL AND ETHAN KOZAK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 23, 2018

  Mr. COFFMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mariel Bailey, 
Melissa Buss, John Engel, and Ethan Kozak for their hard work

[[Page 6216]]

and dedication to the people of Colorado's Sixth District as interns in 
my Washington, D.C. office for the spring of the 115th Congress, Second 
Session.
  The work of these young professionals has been nothing short of 
exemplary. During their time in my office, Mariel, Melissa, John, and 
Ethan served as tour guides, interacted with constituents, conducted 
legislative research and learned a great deal about the United States 
Congress. I know they all have especially bright futures ahead of them 
and I look forward to seeing them build their prospective careers.
  Mariel, Melissa, John, and Ethan have made plans to continue their 
education and professional occupations in Washington, D.C. and 
throughout the United States. I am certain they will succeed in their 
new roles and wish them all the best in their future endeavors. Mr. 
Speaker, it is an honor to recognize Mariel Bailey, Melissa Buss, John 
Engel, and Ethan Kozak for their service this spring.

                          ____________________
```
