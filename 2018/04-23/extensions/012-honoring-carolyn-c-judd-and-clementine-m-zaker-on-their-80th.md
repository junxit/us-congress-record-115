# HONORING CAROLYN C. JUDD AND CLEMENTINE M. ZAKER ON THEIR 80TH BIRTHDAYS

```
    HONORING CAROLYN C. JUDD AND CLEMENTINE M. ZAKER ON THEIR 80TH 
                               BIRTHDAYS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DANIEL LIPINSKI

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 23, 2018

  Mr. LIPINSKI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Carolyn C. Judd and 
Clementine M. Zaker, twin sisters who will be celebrating their 80th 
birthdays on May 2nd.
  Carolyn and Clementine were born on May 2, 1938, to Theodore and 
Clementine Price in Chicago, Illinois, where they lived until moving a 
short distance to Burbank. From a young age, the sisters were an 
inseparable pair and their bond remains strong. Carolyn and Clementine 
have four and three children, respectively, and watching their 
grandchildren grow up has brought the sisters plenty of joy. To this 
day, the sisters live less than 15 minutes away from each other, with 
Carolyn in Hickory Hills and Clementine in Burbank.
  Chicagoland has always been home to Carolyn and Clementine, and they 
have been active in their communities throughout their lives. Carolyn 
spent her career with Nabisco before retiring from her position as a 
secretary to the general manager at the Chicago plant. She remains 
involved in the community through her work as the Secretary of the 
Justice Chamber of Commerce. Clementine is a dedicated wife and mother, 
having raised her three children with her husband, Ronald Zaker. She is 
very committed to her Catholic faith and helped bring the Eucharist to 
those in the community who were unable to attend Mass. Until recently, 
the sisters greatly enjoyed going to local craft shows where they would 
sell items including ornaments, jewelry, and embroidered pillows. They 
continue to enjoy each other's company in sharing meals and taking 
vacations together.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Carolyn and Clementine 
on this special occasion. It is not often that twin sisters are able to 
celebrate their 80th birthday together, and I am inspired learning 
about these remarkable women and their lifelong companionship. May 
Carolyn and Clementine live long and healthy lives with family and 
loved ones, while continuing to contribute to their communities.

                          ____________________
```
