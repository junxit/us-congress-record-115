communication from Brian Perkins, District

Director, the Honorable Jackie Speier, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, April 20, 2018.
     Hon. Paul D. Ryan,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.

     the State of California, County of San Mateo.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I

     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
