# RESIGNATION FROM THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

```
             RESIGNATION FROM THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
resignation from the House of Representatives:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, April 23, 2018.
     Hon. Paul D. Ryan,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: I have notified Oklahoma Governor Mary 
     Fallin that I will resign my office as the Representative of 
     Oklahoma's 1st Congressional District effective as of 10:30 
     a.m. today, Monday April 23, 2018.
       As you know, on April 19th, the Senate confirmed my 
     nomination to serve as Administrator of the National 
     Aeronautics and Space Administration. It has been a great 
     honor to represent the people of Oklahoma's 1st District in 
     the 113th, 114th, and 115th Congresses.
       NASA's leadership team and 17,000 employees comprise a 
     scientific and engineering resource unique in the world. They 
     will enable America to secure its future as the world's 
     preeminent space-faring nation, advancing a new era in space 
     exploration and expanding understanding of our home planet.
       As NASA Administrator, I will look forward to working with 
     you, and with all members of the House and the Senate, to 
     ensure that the United States realizes its full potential as 
     the world's leader in space.
           Sincerely,
     Jim Bridenstine.
                                  ____

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, April 23, 2018.
     Hon. Mary Fallin,
     Office of the Governor,
     Oklahoma City, OK.
       Dear Governor Fallin: On Thursday April 19th, 2018, the 
     United States Senate confirmed my nomination to serve as 
     Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space 
     Administration.
       Accordingly, I write to provide you with advance notice 
     that I will resign my office as United States Representative 
     for the 1st District of Oklahoma effective as of 10:30 a.m. 
     today, Monday April 23, 2018.
       It has been a great honor to represent the people of the 
     1st District in the 113th, 114th, and 115th Congresses.
       NASA's leadership team and 17,000 employees comprise a 
     scientific and engineering resource unique in the world. They 
     will enable America to secure its future as the world's 
     preeminent space-faring nation, advancing a new era in space 
     exploration and expanding understanding of our home planet.
       I look forward to the opportunity to serve the United 
     States, and Oklahoma, in a new capacity as NASA 
     Administrator.
           Sincerely,
     Jim Bridenstine.

                          ____________________
```
