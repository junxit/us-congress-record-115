# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Our Father in Heaven, You clothe Yourself with light as with a robe. 
You spread the glorious Heavens with Your mighty hand. Listen now to 
our prayers, and forgive us for our conscious and unconscious 
transgressions.
  Lord, bless our lawmakers until all they do may find the goals You 
have inspired. May they live this day with a greater dedication to 
serve You and humanity. Give them grace to fill every hour with an 
awareness of Your love, mercy, and grace.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
