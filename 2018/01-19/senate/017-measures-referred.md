# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 1660, H.R. 2954

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1660. An act to direct the Administrator of the United 
     States Agency for International Development to submit to 
     Congress a report on the development and use of global health 
     innovations in the programs, projects, and activities of the 
     Agency; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
       H.R. 2954. An act to amend the Home Mortgage Disclosure Act 
     of 1975 to specify which depository institutions are subject 
     to the maintenance of records and disclosure requirements of 
     such Act, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.
```
