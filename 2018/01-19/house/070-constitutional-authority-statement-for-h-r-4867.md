# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4867

**Measures:** H.R. 4867

```
            By Mr. RUSH:
        H.R. 4867.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 1: ``Full Faith and Credit shall be 
     given in each State to the public Acts, Records, and judicial 
     Proceedings of every other State. And the Congress may by 
     general Laws prescribe the Manner in which such Acts, Records 
     and Proceedings shall be proved, and the Effect thereof.''
```
