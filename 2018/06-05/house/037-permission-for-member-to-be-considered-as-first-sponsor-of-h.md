# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2846

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2846

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2846

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 2846, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Farenthold of Texas, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Norman). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
```
