# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3663, H.R. 4910

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on June 5, 2018, she 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bills:

       H.R. 3663. To designate the medical center of the 
     Department of Veterans Affairs in Huntington, West Virginia, 
     as the Hershel ``Woody'' Williams VA Medical Center.
       H.R. 4910. To amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     provide outer burial receptacles for remains buried in 
     National Parks, and for other purposes.
```
