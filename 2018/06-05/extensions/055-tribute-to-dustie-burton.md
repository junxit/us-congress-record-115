# TRIBUTE TO DUSTIE BURTON

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO DUSTIE BURTON

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 5, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Dustie Burton from 
Greater Regional Medical Center in Creston, Iowa who was awarded the 
2018 DAISY Award For Extraordinary Nurses at a ceremony this spring.
  This award is part of the DAISY Foundation's program to recognize the 
superhuman efforts nurses perform every day. Dustie was nominated by 
patients, families, and colleagues because of her willingness to give 
her best efforts for the patients at GRMC.
  I applaud and congratulate Dustie for her award and for providing 
excellent patient care in Iowa's Third District. I am proud to 
represent her and all the employees of the Greater Regional Medical 
Center in the United States Congress. I know that my colleagues join me 
in congratulating Dustie Burton and wishing her continued success in 
the future.

                          ____________________
```
