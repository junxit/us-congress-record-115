# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, in these challenging and unpredictable times, we look to 
You for guidance. You are the source of our strength and the center of 
our joy.
  Remind our lawmakers that You are prepared to shower them with wisdom 
if they would only request it. Thank You for inviting our Senators to 
ask and receive, to seek and find, and to knock and open closed doors. 
Bless our legislators with productivity and progress for the glory of 
Your Name.
  Today and always, let Your will be done on Earth as it is done in 
Heaven.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
