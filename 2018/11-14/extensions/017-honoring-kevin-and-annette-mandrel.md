# HONORING KEVIN AND ANNETTE MANDREL

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                   HONORING KEVIN AND ANNETTE MANDREL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 14, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Kevin and Annette 
Mandrel for their contributions to our state.
  The Mandrels are fellow believers, and I have appreciated their 
prayers and encouragement over the years. They have also been community 
leaders for decades and have made a positive and lasting impact on the 
state of Indiana. Kevin sits on numerous local boards, including Bosma 
Enterprises, Rush Memorial Hospital, the Arthritis Foundation and the 
Youth Opportunity Center. Their son, Christian, also worked as an 
intern in my office in Shelbyville.
  On a personal note, the Mandrels were also among my earliest and most 
vocal supporters. Jennifer and I cherish our friendship with the 
Mandrels. We want to thank them for their friendship and support of my 
work in Congress. We wish them continued success in all that God has 
planned for their family.

                          ____________________
```
