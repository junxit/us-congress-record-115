# HONORING BRYAN FISCHER

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                         HONORING BRYAN FISCHER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 14, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Bryan Fischer for his 
contribution to our state.
  As a member of the 6th District Agriculture Advisory Committee, Bryan 
regularly met with my team to discuss emerging agriculture issues that 
are impacting Indiana's farmers. Bryan is an important business and 
civic leader in Shelby County and our state is better off today because 
of his extraordinary leadership and service.
  On a personal note, Jennifer and I cherish our friendship with Bryan 
and his wife, Celeste. Not only does Celeste make the best cookies in 
Shelby County, but she and Bryan are both passionate leaders who care 
about providing opportunity for the next generation that grows up in 
Shelby County.
  Both Bryan and Celeste were among my earliest and most vocal 
supporters. They are loyal friends and wise advisers. I want to thank 
the Fishers for their friendship and support of my work in Congress. I 
wish them continued success in all that God has planned for their 
family.

                          ____________________
```
