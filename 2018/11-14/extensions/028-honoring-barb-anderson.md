# HONORING BARB ANDERSON

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                         HONORING BARB ANDERSON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 14, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Barb Anderson for her 
contribution to our state and Shelby County.
  Barb has always put her fellow Hoosiers first and made a positive 
impact in the Shelbyville community. For decades, she led Girls Inc., 
an incredible organization that empowers girls in Shelby County. Our 
state is better off today because of Barb's extraordinary leadership 
and service.
  On a personal note, I've known Barb for most of the past two decades 
and my wife, Jennifer, worked with her as part of Girls Inc. Barb has 
been a mentor to both of my daughters. She is one of my closest friends 
in politics, and she always kept us organized in Shelby County. Without 
a doubt, Barb embodies the Girls Inc. principles of being strong, 
smart, and bold.
  I want to thank Barb for her friendship and support of my work in 
Congress. She is a loyal friend and wise adviser and my family wishes 
Barb all the best in whatever God has planned for her future.

                          ____________________
```
