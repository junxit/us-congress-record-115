# HONORING JOHN MEREDITH

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                         HONORING JOHN MEREDITH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 14, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize John Meredith for his 
contribution to our state and Wayne County.
  John is a brother in Christ, and I have appreciated his prayers and 
encouragement over the years. I always enjoy the time we get to spend 
together and count him among my close friends. John is also a community 
leader, a successful entrepreneur, and has been very active in 
promoting Republican values for years. He has had an incredible impact 
on Wayne County and on our state.
  I also want to thank John for support of my work in Congress. I wish 
him continued success in all that God has planned for him and his 
family.

                          ____________________
```
