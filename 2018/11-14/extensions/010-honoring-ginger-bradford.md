# HONORING GINGER BRADFORD

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                        HONORING GINGER BRADFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 14, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Ginger Bradford for her 
contribution to our state.
  Ginger has served Ripley County and the City of Versailles with honor 
and distinction for years. It has been a pleasure to work with her, and 
our state is better off today because of her extraordinary leadership 
and service.
  On a personal note, Ginger is among my favorite people that I met on 
this journey in Congress. She is smart, strong, honest, and loyal. With 
Ginger, you always know where you stand.
  I will be forever grateful for her friendship and support of my work 
in Congress. The entire Messer family, including my wife Jennifer and 
our kids, have thoroughly enjoyed every moment we have spent with her 
and Bill. I wish her continued success in all that God has planned for 
her family.

                          ____________________
```
