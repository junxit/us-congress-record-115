# HONORING GREG RUST

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                           HONORING GREG RUST

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 14, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Greg Rust for his 
contribution to our state and Decatur County.
  On a personal note, I have known Greg and his wife Judy since 
childhood. His family has always been kind to my family. And, Greg is a 
loyal friend and wise adviser. Whether he knows it or not, Greg's 
consistent encouragement has been an inspiration to me throughout my 
career.
  Without a doubt, Greg was among my earliest and most vocal supporters 
in Decatur County. I want to thank Greg for his incredible support of 
my work in Congress. I wish him continued success in all that God has 
planned for him and his family.

                          ____________________
```
