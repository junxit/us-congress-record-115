# HONORING CHRIS KING

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                          HONORING CHRIS KING

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 14, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Chris King for his 
contribution to our state and Shelby County.
  As a member of the 6th District Manufacturing Advisory Committee, 
Chris regularly met with my team to discuss emerging manufacturing 
issues that are impacting Indiana. The Shelbyville community is better 
off because of his extraordinary leadership and service.
  On a personal note, Chris was among my earliest and most vocal 
supporters. He is a loyal friend and wise adviser. I want to thank him 
for his incredible support of my work in Congress, and wish Chris 
continued success in all that God has planned for him and his family.

                          ____________________
```
