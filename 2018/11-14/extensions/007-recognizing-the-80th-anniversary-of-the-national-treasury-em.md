# RECOGNIZING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NATIONAL TREASURY EMPLOYEES UNION

**Speaking:** Kuster, Ann M. (K000382) [D-NH]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE NATIONAL TREASURY EMPLOYEES 
                                 UNION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ANN M. KUSTER

                            of new hampshire

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 14, 2018

  Ms. KUSTER of New Hampshire. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
the National Treasury Employees Union (NTEU) in celebration of their 
80th anniversary.
  Created in 1938, the NTEU's mission has always been to make sure 
federal employees are treated with respect and dignity. For 80 years 
the NTEU's continued advocacy has strengthened employee rights and 
direct support to more than 150,000 employees.
  The backbone of our federal government and its agencies is our 
workforce, and we must take care of these dedicated employees if we 
want our nation to thrive. We must fight for quality healthcare plans, 
retirement benefits, and livable wages for employees. I am grateful for 
all the NTEU has done in support of our federal workforce, our state 
and our country.
  On behalf of my constituents across New Hampshire's Second 
Congressional District, I want to thank the National Treasury Employees 
Union for all they have done, and will continue to do, for our federal 
workforce.

                          ____________________
```
