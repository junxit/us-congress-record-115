# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 164 for July 5, 2018

```
[[Page 11751]]

                     SENATE--Thursday, July 5, 2018

  The Senate met at 1 and 1 second p.m. and was called to order by the 
Honorable Marco Rubio, a Senator from the State of Florida.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Hatch).
  The assistant bill clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                     Washington, DC, July 5, 2018.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Marco Rubio, a Senator from the State of Florida, to perform 
     the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Orrin G. Hatch,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. RUBIO thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



           ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 9, 2018, AT 3 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until 3 p.m., Monday, July 9, 2018.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1 and 27 seconds p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, July 9, 2018, at 3 p.m.
```
