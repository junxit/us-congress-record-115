# HONORING MARK VAN EYCK

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                         HONORING MARK VAN EYCK

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 1, 2018

  Mr. GRAVES of Missouri. Mr. Speaker, I proudly pause to recognize 
Mark Van Eyck. Mark is a very special young man who has exemplified the 
finest qualities of citizenship and leadership by taking an active part 
in the Boy Scouts of America, Troop 522, and earning the most 
prestigious award of Eagle Scout.
  Mark has been very active with his troop, participating in many scout 
activities. Over the many years Mark has been involved with scouting, 
he has not only earned numerous merit badges, but also the respect of 
his family, peers, and community. Most notably, Mark has contributed to 
his community through his Eagle Scout project. Mark remodeled a youth 
area at his church, Harmony Vineyard, in Kansas City, Missouri.
  Mr. Speaker, I proudly ask you to join me in commending Mark for his 
accomplishments with the Boy Scouts of America and for his efforts put 
forth in achieving the highest distinction of Eagle Scout.

                          ____________________
```
