# CONGRATULATING HARRISON FRAN OF THE RUSSELLVILLE INDIANS

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
        CONGRATULATING HARRISON FRAN OF THE RUSSELLVILLE INDIANS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 12, 2018

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Harrison Fran of the Russellville Indians for 
his first-place finish in the 800m Run at the 2018 Missouri Class 2 
State Track and Field Championship.
   Harrison and his coach should be commended for all of their hard 
work throughout this past year and for bringing home the state 
championship to their school and community.
   I ask you to join me in recognizing Harrison Fran for a job well 
done.

                          ____________________
```
