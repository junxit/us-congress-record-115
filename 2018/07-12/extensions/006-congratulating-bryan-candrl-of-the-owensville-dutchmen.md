# CONGRATULATING BRYAN CANDRL OF THE OWENSVILLE DUTCHMEN

```
         CONGRATULATING BRYAN CANDRL OF THE OWENSVILLE DUTCHMEN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 12, 2018

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Bryan Candrl of the Owensville Dutchmen for 
his first-place finish in the Pole Vault Field Event at the 2018 
Missouri Class 3 State Track and Field Championship.
  Bryan and his coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for bringing home the state championship 
to their school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing Bryan Candrl for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
