# CONGRATULATING MIKAYLA REED OF THE WASHINGTON HIGH SCHOOL BLUE JAYS

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
  CONGRATULATING MIKAYLA REED OF THE WASHINGTON HIGH SCHOOL BLUE JAYS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 12, 2018

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Mikayla Reed of the Washington High School 
Blue Jays for her first-place finish in the 800m, 1600m, 3200m Run at 
the 2018 Missouri Class 4 State Track and Field Championship.
   Mikayla and her coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for bringing home the state championship 
to their school and community.
   I ask you to join me in recognizing Mikayla Reed for a job well 
done.
```
