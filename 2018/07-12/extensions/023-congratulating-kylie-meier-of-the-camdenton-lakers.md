# CONGRATULATING KYLIE MEIER OF THE CAMDENTON LAKERS

```
           CONGRATULATING KYLIE MEIER OF THE CAMDENTON LAKERS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 12, 2018

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Kylie Meier of the Camdenton Lakers for her 
first-place finish in the 100m and 300m Hurdles at the 2018 Missouri 
Class 4 State Track and Field Championship.
  Kylie and her coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for bringing home the state championship 
to their school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing Kylie Meier for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
