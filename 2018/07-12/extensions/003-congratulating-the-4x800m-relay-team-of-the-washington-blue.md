# CONGRATULATING THE 4X800M RELAY TEAM OF THE WASHINGTON BLUE JAYS

```
              CONGRATULATING THE 4X800M RELAY TEAM OF THE 
                          WASHINGTON BLUE JAYS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 12, 2018

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the 4x800m Relay Team of the Washington Blue 
Jays for their first-place finish at the 2018 Missouri Class 4 State 
Track and Field Championship. This 4x800m relay team includes Mikayla 
Reed, Mia Reed, Morgan Holdmeyer, and Claire Ayers.
  The 4x800m Relay Team and their coach should be commended for all of 
their hard work throughout this past year and for bringing home the 
state championship to their school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing the 4x800m Relay Team for a job 
well done.

                          ____________________
```
