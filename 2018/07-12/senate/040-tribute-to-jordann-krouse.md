# TRIBUTE TO JORDANN KROUSE

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JORDANN KROUSE

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Jordann Krouse, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Jordann is a graduate of Harrisburg High School in Harrisburg, SD. 
Currently, she is attending Boston University in Boston, MA, where she 
is pursuing degrees in international relations and environmental 
analysis. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the 
most out of her internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jordann for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
