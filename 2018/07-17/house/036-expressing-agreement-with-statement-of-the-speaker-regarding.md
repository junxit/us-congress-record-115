# EXPRESSING AGREEMENT WITH STATEMENT OF THE SPEAKER REGARDING RUSSIAN INTERFERENCE IN THE 2016 ELECTIONS

```
 EXPRESSING AGREEMENT WITH STATEMENT OF THE SPEAKER REGARDING RUSSIAN 
                   INTERFERENCE IN THE 2016 ELECTIONS

  (Mr. McGOVERN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, H. Res. 999 was introduced by our 
colleague Eliot Engel from New York. Basically, what it says is that 
the House of Representatives expresses its agreement with the 
statements of the Speaker of the House of Representatives made on July 
16, 2018, regarding the Russian Federation's interference in the 2016 
United States elections and related matters.
  It basically, again, is an endorsement word for word of what the 
Speaker of the House said, a very strong statement, basically making it 
clear that there was no question that Russia interfered in our election 
and continues to attempt to undermine our democracy here and around the 
world.
  It is not just the finding of the American intelligence community but 
also of the House Intelligence Committee.

                          ____________________
```
