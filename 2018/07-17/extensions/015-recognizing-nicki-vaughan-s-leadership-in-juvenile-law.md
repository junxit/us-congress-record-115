# RECOGNIZING NICKI VAUGHAN'S LEADERSHIP IN JUVENILE LAW

**Speaking:** Collins, Doug (C001093) [R-GA]

```
         RECOGNIZING NICKI VAUGHAN'S LEADERSHIP IN JUVENILE LAW

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DOUG COLLINS

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 17, 2018

  Mr. COLLINS of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of 
Hall County Chief Assistant Public Defender Nicki Vaughan for her work 
in the field of juvenile law. Recently, the State Bar of Georgia 
honored the Georgia Bar's Child Protection and Advocacy Section--
chaired by Ms. Vaughan--with the 2017-2018 Section Award of 
Achievement.
  Under Ms. Vaughan's leadership, the Child Protection and Advocacy 
Section stood out among the state bar's 48 other Sections for its 
dedication and service to local families.
  As chair of the Child Protection and Advocacy Section, Nicki 
volunteers her time to mentoring young people. Moreover, she educates 
other legal professionals on current legal issues affecting children 
and government policies designed to assist children and families.
  As a fellow attorney, I am proud to honor Nicki Vaughan for her 
exceptional leadership and congratulate her section on earning this 
achievement award.

                          ____________________
```
