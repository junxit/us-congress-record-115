                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______

2018 through July 13, 2018 due to the passing of my father, Leonard

Reclamation Title Transfer and Non-Federal Infrastructure 
Incentivization Act, I would have voted ``NAY.''
  Had I been present for roll call No. 326 on passage of H.R. 6237, the 
Matthew Young Pollard Intelligence Authorization Act for Fiscal Years 
2018 and 2019, I would have voted ``YEA.''
  Had I been present for roll call No. 328 on passage of H.R. 50, the 
Unfunded Mandates Information and Transparency Act, I would have voted 
``NAY.''
