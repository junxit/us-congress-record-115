# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5558

**Measures:** H.R. 5558

```
           By Mr. BUCHANAN:
       H.R. 5558.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress's specified powers are primarily, but not 
     exclusively, found in Section 8 of Article I of the 
     Constitution. This section contains 18 clauses, 17 of which 
     enumerate relatively specific powers granted to the Congress. 
     Among the powers enumerated are Congress's powers to regulate 
     commerce.
```
