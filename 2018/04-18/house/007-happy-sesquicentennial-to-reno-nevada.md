# HAPPY SESQUICENTENNIAL TO RENO, NEVADA

**Speaking:** Amodei, Mark E. (A000369) [R-NV]

```
                 HAPPY SESQUICENTENNIAL TO RENO, NEVADA

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair recognizes the gentleman from 
Nevada (Mr. Amodei) for 5 minutes.
  Mr. AMODEI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with birthday wishes for the 
premier municipality in the Nation--not the Big Apple, not the Windy 
City, not the City by the Bay, or the Big Easy.
  I am talking about the Biggest Little City in the World, Reno, 
Nevada. Reno turns 150, celebrating its sesquicentennial.
  Congratulations to you Mayor Hillary Schieve and the city council.
  Happy birthday, Reno, Nevada.

                          ____________________
```
