# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 1512, H.R. 4403, H.R. 5192

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 1512. An act to amend title II of the Social Security 
     Act to provide for the reissuance of Social Security account 
     numbers to young children in cases where confidentiality has 
     been compromised; to the Committee on Finance.
       H.R. 4403. An act to amend the Tariff Act of 1930 to 
     protect personally identifiable information, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
       H.R. 5192. An act to authorize the Commissioner of Social 
     Security to provide confirmation of fraud protection data to 
     certain permitted entities, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Finance.

                          ____________________
```
