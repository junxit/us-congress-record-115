# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Measures:** S.Res. 474

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, and pursuant to the 
provisions of S. Res. 474, the Senate stands adjourned until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, April 19, and does so as a further mark of respect for the 
late John Melcher, former Senator from Montana.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 7:35 p.m., adjourned until Thursday, April 
19, 2018, at 10 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
