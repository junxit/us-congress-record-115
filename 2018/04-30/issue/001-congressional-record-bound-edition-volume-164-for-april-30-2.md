# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 164 for April 30, 2018

```
[[Page 6718]]

                     SENATE--Monday, April 30, 2018

  The Senate met at 2:30 p.m. and was called to order by the Honorable 
Johnny Isakson, a Senator from the State of Georgia.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Hatch).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                   Washington, DC, April 30, 2018.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Johnny Isakson, a Senator from the State of Georgia, to 
     perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Orrin G. Hatch,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. ISAKSON thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________



         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL THURSDAY, MAY 3, 2018, AT 10:30 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until 10:30 a.m. on Thursday, May 3, 2018.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 2:30 and 34 seconds p.m., adjourned until 
Thursday, May 3, 2018, at 10:30 a.m.
```
