# RECOGNIZING CHLOE LINDEMAN FOR HER 1A STATE TITLE IN SHOTPUT

**Speaking:** Bustos, Cheri (B001286) [D-IL]

```
      RECOGNIZING CHLOE LINDEMAN FOR HER 1A STATE TITLE IN SHOTPUT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHERI BUSTOS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 14, 2018

  Mrs. BUSTOS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Chloe 
Lindeman, a junior from Fulton High School, for earning the Class 1A 
title for the Illinois State Championship in shot put for track and 
field.
  Chloe Lindeman threw the shot put 47 feet to claim her title as state 
champion, and I

[[Page E842]]

would like to recognize Chloe for her tremendous accomplishment. 
Chloe's dedication and passion for her sport meant that she was 
determined to win gold. As a former athlete, I understand the amount of 
hard work and commitment to be awarded such a title. Chloe is an 
example of the importance of dedication and a strong work ethic. I am 
proud there is such young talent in our community, and to see her 
represent Fulton throughout the state.
  Mr. Speaker, I would like to again formally congratulate Chloe 
Lindeman on her title, and I join the rest of the community in wishing 
her every success in the future.

                          ____________________
```
