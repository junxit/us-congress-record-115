# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 2758

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Lasky, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 2758. An act to amend title 36, United States Code, to 
     provide for the display of the National League of Families 
     POW/MIA flag at the World War I Memorials.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Committee will resume its sitting.
```
