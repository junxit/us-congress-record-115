# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 43, S.Con.Res. 44

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:48 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. McLaughlin, one of its journal clerks, announced that the House 
agrees to the following concurrent resolutions, without amendment:

       S. Con. Res. 43. Concurrent resolution providing for the 
     use of the catafalque situated in the Exhibition Hall of the 
     Capitol Visitor

[[Page S5991]]

     Center in connection with memorial services to be conducted 
     in the rotunda of the Capitol for the Honorable John Sidney 
     McCain, III, late a Senator from the State of Arizona.
       S. Con. Res. 44. Concurrent resolution authorizing the use 
     of the rotunda of the Capitol for the lying in state of the 
     remains of the late Honorable John Sidney McCain, III.

  The message also announced that pursuant to section 1 of the Library 
of Congress Trust Fund Board Act (2 U.S.C. 154), and the order of the 
House of January 3, 2017, the Speaker appoints the following individual 
on the part of the House of Representatives to the Library of Congress 
Trust Fund Board for a 5-year term: Ms. Elizabeth Beretta-Perik of 
Jamestown, Rhode Island.

                          ____________________
```
