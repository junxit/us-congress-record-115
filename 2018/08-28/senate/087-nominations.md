       THE FOLLOWING NAMED OFFICERS HAVE BEEN ASSIGNED BY THE 
     SECRETARY OF DEFENSE UNDER TITLE 10 U.S.C. SECTION 950F(B)(2) 
     TO SERVE AS APPELLATE MILITARY JUDGES ON THE UNITED STATES 
     COURT OF MILITARY REVIEW. THE FOLLOWING NAMED OFFICERS FOR 
     APPOINTMENT IN THE GRADE INDICATED IN THE UNITED STATES AIR 
     FORCE OR THE UNITED STATES NAVY AND AS APPELLATE MILITARY 
     JUDGES ON THE UNITED STATES COURT OF MILITARY COMMISSION 
     REVIEW. IN ACCORDANCE WITH THEIR CONTINUED STATUS AS 
     APPELLATE MILITARY JUDGES PURSUANT TO THEIR ASSIGNMENT BY THE 
     SECRETARY OF DEFENSE AND UNDER 10 U.S.C. SECTION 950F(B)(2), 
     WHILE SERVING ON THE UNITED STATES COURT OF MILITARY 
     COMMISSION REVIEW, ALL UNLAWFUL INFLUENCE PROHIBITIONS REMAIN 
     UNDER 10 U.S.C. SECTION 949B(B) AND SUCH APPELLATE MILITARY 
     JUDGES SHALL ONLY BE ELIGIBLE TO SERVE AS JUDGES ON THE 
     UNITED STATES COURT OF MILITARY COMMISSION REVIEW SO LONG AS 
     THEY REMAIN ON ACTIVE DUTY STATUS.
