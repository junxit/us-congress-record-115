# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Elad L. Roisman, of Maine, to be a Member of the 
     Securities and Exchange Commission for a term expiring June 
     5, 2023.
         Mitch McConnell, John Cornyn, Michael B. Enzi, Roy Blunt, 
           Thom Tillis, Mike Rounds, Johnny Isakson, Roger F. 
           Wicker, Mike Crapo, Richard C. Shelby, Steve Daines, 
           John Kennedy, John Boozman, David Perdue, John Thune, 
           Shelley Moore Capito, Pat Roberts.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
mandatory quorum call for the cloture motion be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
