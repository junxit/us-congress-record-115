# EPIPHANY OF THE LORD CATHOLIC SCHOOL OPENS IN KATY, TEXAS

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
       EPIPHANY OF THE LORD CATHOLIC SCHOOL OPENS IN KATY, TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 28, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Epiphany of the 
Lord Catholic School in Katy, TX on opening their doors.
  Epiphany of the Lord Catholic School opened to students on August 16, 
after four years of hard work, fundraising, and careful planning. Denny 
Dillinger, the development board co-chair has stated the project began 
four years ago, with 12-18 months of periodic fundraisers. Currently, 
there are just over 90 students, but 100 are expected on their first 
day. The faculty consists of 14 teachers, two in prekindergarten, two 
in kindergarten and one teacher per classes 1-4. The plan is to add at 
least one grade each year over the next three to four years until they 
get to eighth grade. The Epiphany School will boast a library, music 
room, STEM lab and science lab. The church hopes to provide a faith-
based alternative to public schools, with a dedication to faith, and 
knowledge and compassion being at the forefront of their curriculum.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to the Epiphany Church for opening the Epiphany 
of the Lord Catholic School.
```
