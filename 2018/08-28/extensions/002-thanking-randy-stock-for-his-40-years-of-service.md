# THANKING RANDY STOCK FOR HIS 40 YEARS OF SERVICE

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
            THANKING RANDY STOCK FOR HIS 40 YEARS OF SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 28, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Randy Stock for his 
40 years of service to the city of Sugar Land, TX.
  Randy, a Public Utilities Operator, was born and raised in Sugar Land 
and began working for the town's water division when he was 17 years 
old. When he started, there was one water plant for the whole city--the 
city now operates 12 groundwater plants and five elevated tanks. Over 
the years, he has played a role in ensuring Sugar Land has safe water 
and helped during the numerous annexations that occurred during his 
tenure. Randy was also on the front lines during Hurricane Harvey and 
previous hurricanes, making sure that our water supply remained safe. 
Each and every day he goes above and beyond to serve our community.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Randy Stock on his completion of 40 years of 
service to the city of Sugar Land, TX. I thank him for his work to 
ensure Sugar Land has safe, quality water. His efforts help make Sugar 
Land a wonderful place to live, work and raise a family.

                          ____________________
```
