# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  We are one Nation under God. And You, O God, call us to be united in 
each other and one in You. Hatred, violence, discord--these all break 
down the God-image in us and destroy our Nation. Help us to work 
together for unity; unite us as brothers and sisters. Let Your peace 
reign in this place and flow throughout this Nation until we are all 
truly and completely one Nation under God.
  We make this prayer in Your holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
