# TRIBUTE TO NORMA JEAN AND BOB LINDEMAN

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                 TRIBUTE TO NORMA JEAN AND BOB LINDEMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, January 29, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Norma Jean and Bob Lindeman of Atlantic, Iowa on the very 
special occasion of their 70th wedding anniversary. They were married 
on November 30, 1947 at the Lyman Gospel Hall in Lyman, Iowa.
  Norma Jean and Bob's lifelong commitment to each other and their 
family truly embodies Iowa values. As they reflect on their 70th 
anniversary may their commitment grow even stronger as they continue to 
love, cherish, and honor one another for many years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend this great couple on their 70th year together 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
