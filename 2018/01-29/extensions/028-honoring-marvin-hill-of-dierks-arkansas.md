# HONORING MARVIN HILL OF DIERKS, ARKANSAS

```
                HONORING MARVIN HILL OF DIERKS, ARKANSAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BRUCE WESTERMAN

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, January 29, 2018

  Mr. WESTERMAN. Mr. Speaker, I stand today to recognize a true 
American hero. Marvin Hill of Dierks, Arkansas, is a 103-year-old 
veteran of World War II. On Tuesday, January 30, the Central Arkansas 
Veterans Healthcare System will recognize Mr. Dierks with a 
commemorative coin in honor of his service and sacrifice on behalf of 
the United States.
  I ask the people of Arkansas to join the VA and myself in honoring 
Mr. Dierks and thanking him for fighting in defense of freedom.
```
