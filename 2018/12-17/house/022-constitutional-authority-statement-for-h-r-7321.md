# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7321

**Measures:** H.R. 7321

```
           By Mr. GALLAGHER:
       H.R. 7321.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution, specifically, the 
     power to ``To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.''
```
