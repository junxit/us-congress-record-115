# TRIBUTE TO SEAN ZIMNY

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO SEAN ZIMNY

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Sean Zimny, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Sean is a graduate of Milbank High School in Milbank, SD. Currently, 
he is attending Concordia College in Moorhead, MN, where he is pursuing 
degrees in sociology and social activism. He is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Sean for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
