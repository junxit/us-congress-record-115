# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Keating, William R. (K000375) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. WILLIAM R. KEATING

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 17, 2018

  Mr. KEATING. Mr. Speaker, on December 12, 2018, I was recovering from 
an eye surgery and was therefore unable to be present for the votes on 
the motion on ordering the previous question on the Rule providing for 
consideration of the Conference Report to Accompany H.R. 2, the 
Agriculture and Nutrition Act of 2018, the rule providing for 
consideration of the Conference Report to Accompany H.R. 2, the 
Agriculture and Nutrition Act of 2018, and agreeing to the Conference 
Report to Accompany H.R. 2, the Agriculture and Nutrition Act of 2018.
  Had I been present for Roll Call No. 431, on the motion on ordering 
the previous question on the Rule providing for consideration of the 
Conference Report to Accompany H.R. 2, the Agriculture and Nutrition 
Act of 2018, I would have voted NO.
  Had I been present for Roll Call No. 432, on the rule providing for 
consideration of the Conference Report to Accompany H.R. 2, the 
Agriculture and Nutrition Act of 2018, I would have voted NO.
  Had I been present for Roll Call No. 434, on agreeing to the 
Conference Report to Accompany H.R. 2, the Agriculture and Nutrition 
Act of 2018, I would have voted YEA.
```
