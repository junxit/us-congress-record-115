# HONORING ALEX STIRN AND TYLER WALTERMAN

```
                HONORING ALEX STIRN AND TYLER WALTERMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 17, 2018

  Mr. MESSER, Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District

[[Page E1682]]

of Indiana, to recognize Alex Stirn and Tyler Walterman for being named 
to the Class A All-State team.
  Alex Stirn started as quarterback for the North Decatur Chargers, as 
a junior. He passed for 1,433 yards and threw 12 touchdown passes. He 
also rushed for 327 yards and for nine touchdowns. He was named to the 
Class A junior All-State team.
  Tyler Walterman started as tight end for the Chargers, as a senior. 
He had 10 receptions for 135 yards and two touchdowns. He was named to 
the Class A senior All-State team.
  I want to congratulate them both on an outstanding season and for 
representing themselves with class throughout the season. I ask the 
entire 6th Congressional District to join me in congratulating Tyler 
and Alex for their dedication, execution, and excellence this season.

                          ____________________
```
