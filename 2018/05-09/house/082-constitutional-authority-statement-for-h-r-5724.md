# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5724

**Measures:** H.R. 5724

```
           By Mr. ROHRABACHER:
        H.R. 5724.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to amend laws it has previously 
     enacted. This legislation would amend the Fair Housing Act 
     and Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act of 2010 to 
     restore the power to regulate or prohibit substance abuse 
     recovery facilities in residentially zoned areas to state and 
     local governments. This is consistent with the Tenth 
     Amendment to the Constitution, which reads: ``The powers not 
     delegated to the United States by the Constitution, nor 
     prohibited by it to the States, are reserved to the States 
     respectively, or to the people.''
```
