# POINT OF ORDER

**Speaking:** Massie, Thomas (M001184) [R-KY]

```
                             POINT OF ORDER

  Mr. MASSIE. Mr. Speaker, point of order.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The gentleman will state his point of order.
  Mr. MASSIE. When a quorum is not present, under the rule, the yeas 
and nays are automatic. Will there be a vote of the yeas and nays?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. When business resumes, pursuant to clause 8 
of rule XX, a demand for the yeas and nays will be in order.
```
