# JAMES WILLIAM ROBINSON JR. MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 6167

```
        JAMES WILLIAM ROBINSON JR. MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6167) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 5707 South Cass Avenue in Westmont, Illinois, 
as the ``James William Robinson Jr. Memorial Post Office Building,'' 
was ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
