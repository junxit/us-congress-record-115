# Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
            Unanimous Consent Agreement--Executive Calendar

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that following 
the remarks of the senior Senator from Texas, all postcloture time be 
considered expired and the Senate vote on the Maguire nomination; that 
if confirmed, the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table; and that the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
