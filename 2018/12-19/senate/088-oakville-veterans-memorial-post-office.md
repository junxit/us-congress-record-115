# OAKVILLE VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 6335

```
                 OAKVILLE VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 6335) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 322 Main Street in Oakville, Connecticut, as 
the ``Oakville Veterans Memorial Post Office,'' was ordered to a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
