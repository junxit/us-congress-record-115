# TRIBUTE TO CADE DEVOS

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO CADE DEVOS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DAVID YOUNG-

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 27, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Cade DeVos for winning the Class 3A, 145 pound bracket at 
the Iowa High School State Wrestling tournament earlier this year.
  Iowa has a long and proud history of strong wrestling programs, 
producing college and Olympic champions for years. Cade is a junior at 
Southeast Polk High School in Pleasant Hill. His coaches are Jason 
Christenson and Jake Agnitsch.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to represent Cade and his family in the 
United States Congress and it is with great pride that I recognize him 
today. I ask that my colleagues in the United States House of 
Representatives join me in congratulating Cade on competing in this 
rigorous competition and in wishing him nothing but continued success 
in his education and wrestling career.

                          ____________________
```
