# HONORING ESTHER MESTRE

```
                         HONORING ESTHER MESTRE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. NYDIA M. VELAZQUEZ

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 27, 2018

  Ms. VELAZQUEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the upcoming 
94th birthday of an honorable New Yorker, Esther Mestre. At just twenty 
years old, Esther moved to New York City from her native Puerto Rico. 
While the fast-paced city proved to be a dramatics transition from her 
tranquil life in Puerto Rico, Esther never lost sight of pursing a 
happy and productive life on the Mainland.
  From her humble roots as one of eighteen children in Santurce, Puerto 
Rico, Esther has raised seven wonderful children of her own. As leaders 
in their own communities, many of her children have become successful 
small business owners.
  A beloved friend and dedicated community servant, Esther exemplifies 
the spirit of the greater Puerto Rican family of New York.
  As we approach her 94th birthday celebration, I would like to send my 
deepest gratitude to Esther for her friendship, service and commitment 
to our community. My best wishes for a successful and joyful birthday 
celebration for a cherished leader and a dear friend.

                          ____________________
```
