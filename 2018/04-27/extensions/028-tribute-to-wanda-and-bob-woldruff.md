# TRIBUTE TO WANDA AND BOB WOLDRUFF

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                   TRIBUTE TO WANDA AND BOB WOLDRUFF

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 27, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Wanda and Bob Woldruff on the very special occasion of 
their 60th wedding anniversary.
  Wanda and Bob's lifelong commitment to each other and their family 
truly embodies Iowa's values. As the years pass, may their love 
continue to grow and may they continue to cherish and honor one another 
for many more years to come.

[[Page E564]]

  Mr. Speaker, I commend this great couple on their 60 years together 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
