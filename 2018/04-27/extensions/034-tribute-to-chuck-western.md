# TRIBUTE TO CHUCK WESTERN

```
                        TRIBUTE TO CHUCK WESTERN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 27, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Chuck Western who was inducted into the Union County 
Cattlemen's Hall of Fame at their annual banquet earlier this year.
  Chuck has been a long time livestock producer and row crop farmer. He 
was an original board member of the Union County Cattlemen and also 
served on the state board for Cattlemen.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize Chuck for receiving this 
outstanding award. I am proud to represent him and the Union County 
Cattlemen in the United States Congress. I ask that my colleagues in 
the United States House of Representatives join me in congratulating 
Chuck and in wishing him nothing but continued success.

                          ____________________
```
