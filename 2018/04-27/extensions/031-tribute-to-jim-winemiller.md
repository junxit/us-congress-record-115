# TRIBUTE TO JIM WINEMILLER

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JIM WINEMILLER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 27, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Jim Winemiller. Jim was awarded the Taylor County 
Conservation Leadership Award.
  The Leadership Award is a joint effort of the Taylor County Soil and 
Water Conservation District, Taylor County Board of Supervisors, and 
the Taylor County Conservation Board to recognize the exemplary efforts 
of Taylor County's conservation leaders, committed to improving 
wildlife habitat, water quality, and conservation in Taylor County. 
Since 1990, Jim has worked as the Conservation Board Director and 
enjoyed creating and bettering outdoor recreation opportunities for the 
community. One of his greatest achievements was the improvement of Sand 
Timber Lake and watershed near Blockton.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize Jim for this award. I am proud 
to represent him and Taylor County Conservation in the United States 
Congress. I ask that my colleagues in the United States House of 
Representatives join me in congratulating Jim and in wishing him 
nothing but continued success.

                          ____________________
```
