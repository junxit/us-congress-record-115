# TRIBUTE TO DARLENE AND MYRON LINTNER

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                  TRIBUTE TO DARLENE AND MYRON LINTNER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 27, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Darlene and Myron Lintner of Sidney, Iowa on the very 
special occasion of their 65th wedding anniversary.
  Darlene and Myron's lifelong commitment to each other and their 
family truly embodies Iowa values. As they reflect on their 65th 
anniversary, may their commitment grow even stronger, as they continue 
to love, cherish, and honor one another for many years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend this great couple on their 65th year together 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
