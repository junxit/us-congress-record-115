# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7255

**Measures:** H.R. 7255

```
            By Mr. GALLAGHER:
        H.R. 7255.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution, specifically ``To 
     regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the several 
     States, and with the Indian Tribes.''
```
