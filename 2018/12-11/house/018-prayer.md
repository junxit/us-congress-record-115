# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Loving and gracious God, we give You thanks for giving us another 
day.
  We ask today that You bless the Members of the people's House to be 
the best and most faithful servants of the people they serve.
  May they be filled with gratitude at the opportunity they have to 
serve in this place. We thank You for the abilities they have been 
given to do their work to contribute to the common good.
  As this second session of the 115th Congress draws near its end and 
legislative business once again weighs heavily on this Hill, withhold 
not Your spirit of wisdom and truth from this assembly. Give each 
Member clarity of thought and purity of motive so that they may render 
their service as their best selves.
  May all that is done this day in the people's House be for Your 
greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
