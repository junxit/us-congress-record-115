# HONORING EMILY DANIELS SPAULDING

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                    HONORING EMILY DANIELS SPAULDING

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 11, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize a former member of my 
staff, Emily Daniels Spaulding, for her work in my office and her 
service to our state.
  Emily worked in my Indiana office for nearly a year, serving as one 
of my most senior staffers. Emily has an incredible work ethic and was 
a tremendous asset to our organization. She brought a wealth of 
experience and knowledge to our team and was someone I could rely on 
for on the ground political advice.
  On a personal note, I admire Emily's commitment to her faith and 
family. Emily works very hard and delivers great results. But, you 
always have the sense that Emily's priorities are in the right order.
  I want to thank Emily for her friendship and her hard work in our 
office. I wish her and Brian continued success in all that God has 
planned for her family.

                          ____________________
```
