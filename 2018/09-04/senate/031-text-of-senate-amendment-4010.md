# Text of Senate Amendment 4010

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2553

```
  SA 4010. Mr. McCONNELL (for Ms. Stabenow) proposed an amendment to

[[Page S6031]]

the bill S. 2553, to amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to 
prohibit Medicare part D plans from restricting pharmacies from 
informing individuals regarding the prices for certain drugs and 
biologicals; as follows:

       Amend the title so as to read: ``A bill to amend title 
     XVIII of the Social Security Act to prohibit Medicare part D 
     plans from restricting pharmacies from informing individuals 
     regarding the prices for certain drugs and biologicals.''.
```
