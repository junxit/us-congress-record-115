# RECOGNIZING ORAN YEARGAN

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING ORAN YEARGAN

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Oran 
Yeargan, a student at Glynn County Middle School in Brunswick, Georgia.
  A student with a disability, Oran has been the water boy for the 
Hurricane's football team for the past 3 years. This October, the coach 
called him off the bench and Oran ran the ball for the last touchdown 
of the game against Needwood Middle School.
  He stood by the team with utmost dedication all year, and this 
touchdown was not only really deserved, but an important lesson to us 
all about commitment and overcoming life's obstacles.
  Oran, we are proud of you. Congratulations.

                          ____________________
```
