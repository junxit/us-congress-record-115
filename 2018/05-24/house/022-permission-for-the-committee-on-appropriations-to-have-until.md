# PERMISSION FOR THE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL 5 P.M. ON THURSDAY, MAY 24, 2018, TO FILE A PRIVILEGED REPORT

**Speaking:** Culberson, John Abney (C001048) [R-TX]

```
PERMISSION FOR THE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL 5 P.M. ON 
          THURSDAY, MAY 24, 2018, TO FILE A PRIVILEGED REPORT

  Mr. CULBERSON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Appropriations have until 5 p.m. on Thursday, May 24, 
2018, to file a privileged report to accompany measures making 
appropriations for the Agriculture, Rural Development, Food and Drug 
Administration, and Related Agencies for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2019, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
