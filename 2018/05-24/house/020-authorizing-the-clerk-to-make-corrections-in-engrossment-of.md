**Speaking:** Thornberry, Mac (T000238) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 5515

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 5515, 
        NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2019

  Mr. THORNBERRY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Clerk 
be authorized to make technical corrections in the engrossment of H.R.

contents and short titles, and the insertion of appropriate headings.
```
