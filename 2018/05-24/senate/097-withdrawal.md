# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on May 
24, 2018 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       Edward Masso, of Virginia, to be Ambassador Extraordinary 
     and Plenipotentiary of the United States of America to the 
     Republic of Estonia, which was sent to the Senate on 
     September 5, 2017.
```
