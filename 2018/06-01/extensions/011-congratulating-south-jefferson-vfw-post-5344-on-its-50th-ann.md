# CONGRATULATING SOUTH JEFFERSON VFW POST 5344 ON ITS 50TH ANNIVERSARY

```
  CONGRATULATING SOUTH JEFFERSON VFW POST 5344 ON ITS 50TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, June 1, 2018

  Ms. STEFANIK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and recognize South 
Jefferson VFW Post 5344 on its 50th Anniversary.
  For the past 50 years, members of South Jefferson VFW Post 5344 have 
successfully advocated on behalf of fellow veterans and their families. 
The imperative work this post carries out in support of our military 
families is incredible. VFW Post 5344 has hosted barbecues and 
fundraisers to benefit community members, and has hosted wreath laying 
events and ceremonies in honor of Memorial Day and Veterans Day. For 
its 50 years of dedication to veterans and to the community, Post 5344 
will proudly receive its Golden Anniversary Award Citation.
  On behalf of New York's 21st District, I would like to thank all of 
the members of South Jefferson VFW Post 5344 for their selfless service 
to our Nation and to the local community, and wish this Post all the 
best in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
