# RECOGNIZING THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY OF SUMMERCREST ASSISTED LIVING

```
    RECOGNIZING THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY OF SUMMERCREST ASSISTED LIVING

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ANN M. KUSTER

                            of new hampshire

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, June 1, 2018

  Ms. KUSTER of New Hampshire. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to offer my 
sincere congratulations to Summercrest Assisted Living in Newport, New 
Hampshire on its 20th anniversary.
  Summercrest provides vital programs to seniors across the Lake 
Sunapee Region who need care services but are limited by income. I am 
so thankful to the healthcare professionals, caregivers, leadership and 
staff at Summercrest for their essential role in improving the lives of 
Granite Staters every day. I remain committed to doing all that I can 
to guarantee retirement security, healthy aging and long-term services 
for our elder population.
  On behalf of my constituents across New Hampshire's Second 
Congressional District, I thank Summercrest Assisted Living for the 
great service it provides to the Granite State and congratulate the 
facility again on 20 years of operation. I look forward to our 
continued work together in making New Hampshire an even better place to 
live, work, and raise a family.

                          ____________________
```
