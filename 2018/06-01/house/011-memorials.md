# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S. 13, S. 41, S.Res. 143, S.Res. 219, H.J.Res. 1043

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       200. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of Louisiana, relative to Senate Resolution No. 143, 
     designating Tuesday, April 24, 2018, as the fourth annual Oil 
     and Natural Gas Industry Day at the state capitol; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       201. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to Senate Resolution No. 219, 
     memorializing Congress to encourage that the design and 
     construction of the Vito development in the Gulf of Mexico 
     occur in Louisiana; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       202. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Oklahoma, relative to House Joint Resolution No. 
     1043, requesting the Congress of the United States call a 
     convention of the states to propose amendments to the 
     Constitution of the United States; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
       203. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 41, 
     memorializing the Congress of the Untied States to allow 
     variances on certain projects regulated by the Clean Water 
     Act and the Rivers and Harbors Act; to the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure.
       204. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 13, 
     memorializing the Congress of the United States to take such 
     actions as are necessary to pass the Disability Integration 
     Act of 2017; jointly to the Committees on Energy and Commerce 
     and the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
