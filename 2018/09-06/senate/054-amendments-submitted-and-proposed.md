# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

**Measures:** H.R. 6, H.R. 302, S. 1417, S. 2554

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 4011. Mr. LEE submitted an amendment intended to be 
     proposed by him to the bill S. 2554, to ensure that health 
     insurance issuers and group health plans do not prohibit 
     pharmacy providers from providing certain information to 
     enrollees; which was ordered to lie on the table .
       SA 4012. Mr. McCONNELL (for Mr. Hatch (for himself and Mr. 
     Heinrich)) proposed an amendment to the bill S. 1417, to 
     require the Secretary of the Interior to develop a 
     categorical exclusion for covered vegetative management 
     activities carried out to establish or improve habitat for 
     greater sage-grouse and mule deer, and for other purposes.
       SA 4013. Mr. ALEXANDER submitted an amendment intended to 
     be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 6, to provide for opioid 
     use disorder prevention, recovery, and treatment, and for 
     other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table.
       SA 4014. Mr. McCONNELL (for Mr. Alexander) proposed an 
     amendment to the bill H.R. 302, to provide protections for 
     certain sports medicine professionals who provide certain 
     medical services in a secondary State.
```
