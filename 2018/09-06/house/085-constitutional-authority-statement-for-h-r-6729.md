# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6729

**Measures:** H.R. 6729

```
           By Mrs. WAGNER:
       H.R. 6729.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8, clause 1 (relating to the general 
     Welfare of the United States); Article I, section 8, clause 3 
     (relating to regulating Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes); and 
     Amendment XIII (relating to slavery and involuntary 
     servitude, which authorizes Congress to make laws enforcing 
     the extension of civil rights and universal freedom to 
     victims of slavery)
```
