# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       121. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Gregory D. Watson, 
     a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to urging Congress to cease 
     the appropriation of United States Federal Government funds 
     for financial aid to foreign nations; which was referred to 
     the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
```
