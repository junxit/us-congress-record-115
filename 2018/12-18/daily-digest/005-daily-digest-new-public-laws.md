# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 315, H.R. 1417, H.R. 1861, H.R. 3398, H.R. 6330

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

     (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D1288)
  H.R. 315, to amend the Public Health Service Act to distribute 
maternity care health professionals to health professional shortage 
areas identified as in need of maternity care health services. Signed 
on December 17, 2018. (Public Law 115-320)
  H.R. 1417, to amend the National Law Enforcement Museum Act to allow 
the Museum to acquire, receive, possess, collect, ship, transport, 
import, and display firearms. Signed on December 17, 2018. (Public Law 
115-321)
  H.R. 1861, to award a Congressional Gold Medal in honor of Lawrence 
Eugene ``Larry'' Doby in recognition of his achievements and 
contributions to American major league athletics, civil rights, and the 
Armed Forces during World War II. Signed on December 17, 2018. (Public 
Law 115-322)
  H.R. 3398, to amend the Real ID Act of 2005 to permit Freely 
Associated States to meet identification requirements under such Act. 
Signed on December 17, 2018. (Public Law 115-323)
  H.R. 6330, to amend the Small Business Act to modify the method for 
prescribing size standards for business concerns. Signed on December 
17, 2018. (Public Law 115-324)
```
