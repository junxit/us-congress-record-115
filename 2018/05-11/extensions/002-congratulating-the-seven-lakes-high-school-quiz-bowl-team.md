# CONGRATULATING THE SEVEN LAKES HIGH SCHOOL QUIZ BOWL TEAM

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
       CONGRATULATING THE SEVEN LAKES HIGH SCHOOL QUIZ BOWL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 11, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Seven Lakes 
High School Quiz Bowl team for being ranked in the top 100 quiz bowl 
teams in the world by the High School Quiz Bowl Rankings.
  Quiz Bowl is an academic competition where school teams compete by 
answering questions about a wide range of academic subjects including 
science, literature, history, mathematics and social science. The Seven 
Lakes team of Zach Whang, Son Jadhav, George Li and Mohammad Dhillon 
was ranked 67th in the midseason rankings announced in January. They 
are coached by Kirsten Hampshire.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to the Seven Lakes High School Quiz Bowl team. We 
look forward to their continued successes. Go Spartans

                          ____________________
```
