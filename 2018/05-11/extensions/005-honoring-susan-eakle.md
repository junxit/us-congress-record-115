# HONORING SUSAN EAKLE

```
                          HONORING SUSAN EAKLE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 11, 2018

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Susan 
Eakle and her commitment to public school education. Ms. Eakle is 
retiring after 42 years of service in public education at Pope Valley 
Union Elementary School in Pope Valley, California.
  Ms. Eakle was born in San Francisco, California and grew up in Mill 
Valley and St. Helena, California. She earned a Bachelor of Arts in 
Spanish and a multiple-subject teaching credential with a 
specialization in bilingual

[[Page 7099]]

education at the University of California, Davis. She also received a 
Master of Arts in Educational Administration and an administration 
services credential from Sonoma State University. After graduating from 
college she lived in Yountville, California until she moved to Pope 
Valley in 1983.
  At Pope Valley Union Elementary School, Ms. Eakle has taught 
combinations of kindergarten and first and second grades and has been 
the Principal and Superintendent. She currently teaches transitional 
kindergarten and first and second grades. Ms. Eakle is a natural 
educator who encourages her students to continue learning after they 
leave her classroom and inspires them with her love of reading and 
appreciation for knowledge. She is also active in the Pope Valley 
Scholarship Fund and has been a member of the California Teachers 
Association and National Education Association.
  In addition to her work in education, Ms. Eakle is active in our 
community in other ways. She was a Board Member of the Northern 
California Coastal Region of the American Red Cross and served on their 
Disaster Action Team. She has been a project leader in 4-H and a 
volunteer for liturgies at St. Helena Catholic Church.
  Mr. Speaker, Ms. Eakle is a passionate educator who is dearly loved 
by her students, her colleagues and our community. It is therefore 
fitting and proper that we honor Susan Eakle here today.

                          ____________________
```
