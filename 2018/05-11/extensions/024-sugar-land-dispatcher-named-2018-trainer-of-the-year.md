# SUGAR LAND DISPATCHER NAMED 2018 TRAINER OF THE YEAR

```
          SUGAR LAND DISPATCHER NAMED 2018 TRAINER OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 11, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Amanda Davis for 
being named the 2018 Texas Association of Public Safety Communication 
Officials Trainer of the Year.
  Davis has served as a front-line dispatcher for over nine years and 
also trains new recruits. As part of this effort, she has developed a 
curriculum to inform dispatchers on how to respond to frantic callers 
and ensures that they are prepared to help citizens in need at any 
point in time. Thanks in part to her efforts, Sugar Land is a safe 
place to raise a family, with the lowest crime rates in 20 years, 
faster ambulances, and improved public dispatch. Those who work 
alongside Amanda say that she goes above and beyond to make her 
trainees feel welcome and never fails to demonstrate absolute 
professionalism.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Amanda Davis for this wonderful honor. I thank 
her for everything she does to help keep our Sugar Land community safe.
```
