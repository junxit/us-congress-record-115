# ALVIN HIGH SCHOOL COLOR GUARD WINS STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
         ALVIN HIGH SCHOOL COLOR GUARD WINS STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 11, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Alvin High 
School Color Guard on winning their state championship.
  In April, the Alvin High School Color Guard, coached by Maurice 
Wright traveled to College Station to compete in the state finals and 
came home with their first state championship. The 25 member team works 
year round to perfect their routines. Their success has made Alvin 
proud.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to the Alvin High School Color Guard. We look 
forward to their continued successes. Go Yellowjackets.

                          ____________________
```
