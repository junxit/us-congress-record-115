# FRESHMAN KENDALL JACKSON QUALIFIES FOR STATE GOLF TOURNAMENT

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
      FRESHMAN KENDALL JACKSON QUALIFIES FOR STATE GOLF TOURNAMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 11, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Pearland High 
School freshman Kendall Jackson on qualifying for the Class 6A state 
girls golf tournament.
  In two rounds, Kendall shot an impressive 76 and 77 and set a new 
school record. She is the first Lady Oiler golfer to ever qualify for 
the state tournament. We know she will represent Pearland well at the 
state level.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Kendall Jackson. We look forward to her 
continued successes. Go Oilers.

                          ____________________
```
