# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Hatch).
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                               Washington, DC, September 18, 2018.

     To the Senate.
  Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the Standing Rules of 
the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Cindy Hyde-Smith, a Senator 
from the State of Mississippi, to perform the duties of the Chair.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S6197, September 18, 2018, in the second column, the 
following appears: Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of 
the Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
Cindy Hyde-Smith, a Senator from the State of Alabama, to perform 
the duties of the Chair.
  
  The online Record has been corrected to read: Under the 
provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Cindy Hyde-Smith, a Senator 
from the State of Mississippi, to perform the duties of the Chair.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 

                                                   Orrin G. Hatch,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mrs. HYDE-SMITH thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________
```
