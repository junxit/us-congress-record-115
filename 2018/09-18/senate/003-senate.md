# Senate

```
                                 Senate

Tuesday, September 18, 2018
  The Senate met at 10 a.m. and was called to order by the Honorable 
Cindy Hyde-Smith, a Senator from the State of Mississippi.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S6197, September 18, 2018, in the first column, the 
following appears: The Senate met at 10 a.m. and was called to 
order by the Honorable Cindy Hyde-Smith, a Senator from the State 
of Alabama.
  
  The online Record has been corrected to read: The Senate met at 
10 a.m. and was called to order by the Honorable Cindy Hyde-Smith, 
a Senator from the State of Mississippi.
```
