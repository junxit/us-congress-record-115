# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, whose mercy is great unto the Heavens, help us to do 
what is right. May we not forget that You are the judge of the Earth 
and that we are accountable to You. Protect us from our enemies, for we 
place our trust in You.
  Lord, sustain our Senators. Surround them with the shield of Your 
favor as they seek to live for Your glory. Hasten the day when the just 
rule of Your Kingdom shall fill the Earth with health and life and 
peace. Be exulted, O God, above the Heavens. Let Your glory be over the 
Earth.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
