# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. McCONNELL. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Peter A. Feldman, of the District of Columbia, to be a 
     Commissioner of the Consumer Product Safety Commission for a 
     term of seven years from October 27, 2019. (Reappointment)
         Mitch McConnell, Richard C. Shelby, Todd Young, Pat 
           Roberts, Thom Tillis, Cory Gardner, Roger F. Wicker, 
           Mike Rounds, David Perdue, John Boozman, Roy Blunt, 
           Jerry Moran, Lamar Alexander, John Thune, Tim Scott, 
           John Barrasso, Steve Daines.

  Mr. McCONNELL. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
