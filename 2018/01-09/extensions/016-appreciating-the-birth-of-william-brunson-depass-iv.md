# APPRECIATING THE BIRTH OF WILLIAM BRUNSON DePASS IV

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
          APPRECIATING THE BIRTH OF WILLIAM BRUNSON DePASS IV

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, January 9, 2018

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, congratulations to W. 
Brunson DePass, III, and Emily Barnhill DePass on the birth of their 
son William Brunson DePass, IV, born at 4:47 p.m., Saturday, January 6, 
2018, at Baptist Medical Center of Columbia, South Carolina, weighing 
six pounds and fourteen ounces.
  He is welcomed by his grateful grandparents William (Rusty) B. 
DePass, Jr. and Susan Parker DePass along with Jane Arnold Barnhill and 
Edward (Butch) D. Barnhill, Jr., Esq.

                          ____________________
```
