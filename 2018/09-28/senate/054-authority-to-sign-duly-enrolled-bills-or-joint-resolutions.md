# AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
       AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
majority leader and the senior Senator from Arkansas be authorized to 
sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions during the upcoming 
recess of the Senate.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
