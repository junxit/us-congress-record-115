# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Infinite Spirit, Creator of Heaven and Earth, the sea, and all that 
lives in it, thank You for the gift of another day. We praise You that 
You are the same yesterday, today, and forever. Remind us of the 
foolishness of seeking security apart from You.
  Bless our lawmakers. Protect them in their work as You give them Your 
peace. Be for them a light in the darkness and a shelter from life's 
storms. Lord, give them the wisdom to make decisions that will bring 
glory and honor to Your Name.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
