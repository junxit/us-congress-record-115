# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 1, 2018

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 1, 2018

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until 3 p.m., Monday, 
October 1; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date and the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
