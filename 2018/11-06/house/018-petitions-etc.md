# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       124. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Gregory D. Watson, 
     a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to requesting the enactment 
     of legislation to amend Section 31 of U.S.C. 5103 so as to 
     universally require the acceptance of government-produced 
     United States currency throughout the United States as legal 
     tender for all debts public and private and stipulating that 
     a refusal to accept cash -- not exceeding the amount actually 
     due and not involving more than 50 coins -- as payment shall 
     extinguish the debt.; which was referred to the Committee on 
     Financial Services.
```
