# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.Res. 421

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       267. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, relative to Senate Resolution 
     No. 421, urging the Congress of the United States to pass the 
     language from the Hemp Farming Act of 2018, as contained in 
     the Farm Bill of 2018, removing industrial hemp from the 
     Schedule I Controlled Substance List and legalizing 
     commercial industrial hemp production in the United States; 
     which was referred to the Committee on Agriculture.
```
