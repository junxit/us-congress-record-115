# CONGRATULATING HENRY TYSON FOR RECEIVING THE 2018 GREATER FAYETTEVILLE CHAMBER YOUNG PROFESSIONAL OF THE YEAR AWARD

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
CONGRATULATING HENRY TYSON FOR RECEIVING THE 2018 GREATER FAYETTEVILLE 
              CHAMBER YOUNG PROFESSIONAL OF THE YEAR AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 23, 2018

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Henry Tyson as the 
recipient of the 2018 Greater Fayetteville Chamber Young Professional 
of the Year Award.
  Born and raised in Fayetteville, Henry has a passion for government 
and has remained committed to making our community a better place. 
After receiving his degree in Government from Campbell University, 
Henry took the opportunity to return home to Fayetteville and join the 
family business. Today he is a successful real estate broker and co-
owns Tyson Commercial Properties in downtown Fayetteville with his 
mother, Kirby Tyson.
  In addition to improving Fayetteville through his involvement in the 
Chamber of Commerce, Henry has volunteered himself during times of 
need, including helping clean up and rebuild after the devastation 
caused by Hurricane Matthew. I've known the Tyson family for decades, 
and I can tell you that no one is more deserving than Henry as the 
Chamber's Young Professional of the Year.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in celebrating Henry Tyson on being 
named the 2018 Greater Fayetteville Chamber Young Professional of the 
Year.

                          ____________________
```
