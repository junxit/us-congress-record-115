# CONGRATULATING MAC HEALY ON RECEIVING THE 2018 GREATER FAYETTEVILLE CHAMBER REALTOR CUP AWARD

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
  CONGRATULATING MAC HEALY ON RECEIVING THE 2018 GREATER FAYETTEVILLE 
                       CHAMBER REALTOR CUP AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 23, 2018

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mac Healy as the 
recipient of the 2018 Greater Fayetteville Chamber Realtor Cup Award.
  The Realtor Cup Award is given to the person who has contributed to 
the civic, economic and cultural growth of Fayetteville and Cumberland 
County. During his career, my good friend Mac has exhibited the 
qualities which define excellence in both business and community 
service. Mac is the President of Healy Wholesale Company, Incorporated 
and has lived in Fayetteville since he graduated from Wake Forest 
University.
  Over the years, Mac has contributed to many local Fayetteville 
organizations. Currently, he serves as President of the N.C. Civil

[[Page E211]]

War History Center Foundation, in addition to being Chairman of the 
Board of Trustees for Methodist University. He also serves on the 
Boards of the Cumberland County Foundation, American Heart Association, 
Downtown Development Corporation, YMCA, Dogwood Festival, March of 
Dimes, Fayetteville Symphony and many more.
  Mac looks for every way to better our community, and I am truly 
thankful for his service, friendship and counsel over the years. He is 
deserving of this award, and I wish him continued success as he 
continues to be a great asset for Cumberland County.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in celebrating Mac Healy on winning 
the 2018 Greater Fayetteville Chamber Realtor Cup Award.

                          ____________________
```
