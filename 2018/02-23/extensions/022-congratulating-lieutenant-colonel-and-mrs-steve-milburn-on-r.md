# CONGRATULATING LIEUTENANT COLONEL AND MRS. STEVE MILBURN ON RECEIVING THE 2018 GREATER FAYETTEVILLE CHAMBER BUSINESS OF THE YEAR AWARD

```
 CONGRATULATING LIEUTENANT COLONEL AND MRS. STEVE MILBURN ON RECEIVING 
    THE 2018 GREATER FAYETTEVILLE CHAMBER BUSINESS OF THE YEAR AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 23, 2018

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Lieutenant Colonel 
Steve Milburn and his wife, Debbie, as the recipients of the 2018 
Greater Fayetteville Chamber Business of the Year Award.
  The Milburns own and operate two UPS Stores in the Westwood Shopping 
Center and at Fort Bragg that they opened after LTC Milburn retired 
from the Army in 1996. These UPS Stores have offered unparalleled 
service to our community through their experienced staff and ownership. 
LTC Milburn is not only a sharp businessman, but a bright student as 
well, graduating from the U.S. Military Academy at West Point in 1976 
before serving as an Army Aviator. His hard work and determination 
allowed him to reach the rank of Lieutenant Colonel in the United 
States Army. LTC Milburn, I want to take this opportunity to thank him 
for his service to our country. It is because of men and women like you 
we are able to enjoy the freedoms that we have.
  Over the years, LTC Milburn has dedicated his life to serving our 
community and country. He currently serves as President of the Board of 
Directors for the CARE Clinic, Head of the Finance Council at St. 
Patrick's Catholic Church, former President of the Board of the Armed 
Services YMCA at Fort Bragg, Outstanding Lieutenant Governor of the 
Carolinas Kiwanis District, and a member of the Kiwanis Club of Cape 
Fear. The Milburns look to better our community at every turn, and I am 
truly thankful for their service.
  The Milburns are deserving of this award and I wish them continued 
success. They truly are a wonderful asset for Cumberland County.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in celebrating LTC and Mrs. Steve 
Milburn on winning the 2018 Greater Fayetteville Chamber Business of 
the Year Award.
```
