# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on May

nomination:

       HARRY B. HARRIS, JR., OF FLORIDA, TO BE AMBASSADOR 
     EXTRAORDINARY AND PLENIPOTENTIARY OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
     AMERICA TO THE COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA, WHICH WAS SENT TO 
     THE SENATE ON FEBRUARY 13, 2018.
```
