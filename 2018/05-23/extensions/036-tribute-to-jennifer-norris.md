# TRIBUTE TO JENNIFER NORRIS

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JENNIFER NORRIS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 23, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Jennifer Norris. She was presented with the Appreciation 
Award from the Salvation Army at their annual dinner on May 9, 2018.
  Jennifer is the Director of Recreational Ministries and oversees the 
Basketball and Day Camp activities at Citadel. She will soon be an 
officer in the Salvation Army.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend Jennifer Norris for receiving this outstanding 
award and for her continued commitment to making her community better. 
I am proud to represent her, and Iowans like her, in the United States 
Congress. I ask that my colleagues in the United States House of 
Representatives join me in congratulating Jennifer Norris and in 
wishing her nothing but continued success.

                          ____________________
```
