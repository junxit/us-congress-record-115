PERMISSION TO MODIFY AMENDMENTS EN BLOC NO. 4 OFFERED BY MR. THORNBERRY 
               OF TEXAS PURSUANT TO HOUSE RESOLUTION 905

  Mr. THORNBERRY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during 
further consideration of H.R. 5515 in the Committee of the Whole
