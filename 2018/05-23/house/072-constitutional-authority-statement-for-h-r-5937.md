# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5937

**Measures:** H.R. 5937

```
           By Miss GONZALEZ-COLON of Puerto Rico:
       H.R. 5937.

[[Page H4694]]

       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 1 of the U.S. Constitution
       ``All legislative Powers herein granted shall be vested in 
     a Congress of the United States, which shall consist of a 
     Senate and House of Representatives.''
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the U.S. Constitution
       Congress shall have the power . . . ``To establish Post 
     Offices and post Roads.''
```
