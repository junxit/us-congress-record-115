# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 5895

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Lasky, one of the clerks, announced 
that the Senate insists upon its amendment to the bill (H.R. 5895) ``An 
Act making appropriations for energy and water development and related 
agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, and for other 
purposes.'', disagreed to by the House and agrees to the conference 
asked by the House on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses thereon, 
and appoints Mr. Shelby, Mr. Alexander, Mr. Boozman, Mr. Daines, Mr. 
Lankford, Mr. Leahy, Mrs. Feinstein, Mr. Schatz, and Mr. Murphy, be the 
conferees on the part of the Senate, with instructions.
```
