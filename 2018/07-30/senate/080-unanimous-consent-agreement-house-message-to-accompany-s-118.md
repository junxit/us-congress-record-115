# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--HOUSE MESSAGE TO ACCOMPANY S. 1182

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 1182

```
    UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--HOUSE MESSAGE TO ACCOMPANY S. 1182

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding rule XXII, the cloture motion with respect to the House 
message to accompany S. 1182 be withdrawn, and the motion to refer and 
the motion to concur with amendment be withdrawn, and the Senate vote 
on the motion to concur without further intervening action or debate at 
12:15 p.m., Tuesday, July 31.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
