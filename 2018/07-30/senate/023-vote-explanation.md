# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Warner, Mark R. (W000805) [D-VA]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, family obligations unfortunately have kept 
me from being present for the cloture vote today on the nomination of 
Britt Grant, of Georgia, to be a U.S. circuit judge for the Eleventh 
Circuit. I have grave concerns about Justice Grant's qualifications and 
her record, and, had I been present, I would have voted against cloture 
on her nomination.
```
