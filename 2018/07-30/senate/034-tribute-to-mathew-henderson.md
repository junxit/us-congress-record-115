# TRIBUTE TO MATHEW HENDERSON

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO MATHEW HENDERSON

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Mathew Henderson, 
a 2018 summer intern in my Orlando office, for all of the hard work he 
has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of Florida.
  Mathew is a student at Florida State University. He is a dedicated 
and diligent worker who has been devoted to getting the most out of his 
internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Mathew 
Henderson for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued 
success in the years to come.●
```
