# TRIBUTE TO MARK WHITTEN

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MARK WHITTEN

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Mark Whitten, a 
2018 summer intern in my Orlando office, for all of the hard work he 
has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of Florida.
  Mark is a graduate of Liberty University. He is a dedicated and 
diligent worker who has been devoted to getting the most out of his 
internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Mark 
Whitten for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued 
success in the years to come.●
```
