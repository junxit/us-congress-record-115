# TRIBUTE TO MARCO USECHE

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MARCO USECHE

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Marco Useche, a 
2018 summer intern in my Orlando office, for all of the hard work he 
has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of Florida.
  Marco is an incoming freshman at Loyola University. He is a dedicated 
and diligent worker who has been devoted to getting the most out of his 
internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Marco 
Useche for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success 
in the years to come.●
```
