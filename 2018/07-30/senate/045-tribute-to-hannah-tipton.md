# TRIBUTE TO HANNAH TIPTON

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO HANNAH TIPTON

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Hannah Tipton, a 
2018 summer intern in my Orlando office, for all of the hard work she 
has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of Florida.
  Hannah is a student at Charleston School of Law, where she is 
pursuing her juris doctorate. She is a dedicated and diligent worker 
who has been devoted to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Hannah 
Tipton for all the fine work she has done and wish her continued 
success in the years to come.●
```
