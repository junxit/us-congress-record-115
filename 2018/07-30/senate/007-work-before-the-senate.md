# WORK BEFORE THE SENATE

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                         WORK BEFORE THE SENATE

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, we will finish up the set of 
appropriations measures we have been considering for several days and 
take four more big steps toward our goal of completing a regular 
appropriations process and funding the government in a timely and 
orderly manner.
  We will authorize vital resources for our Nation's Armed Forces. We 
will attend to several other priorities as well, including an extension 
of flood insurance and sending the bipartisan farm bill to a conference 
with the House.
```
