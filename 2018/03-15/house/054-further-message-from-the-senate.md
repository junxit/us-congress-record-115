# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Lasky, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed with an amendment in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 3210. An act to require the Director of the National 
     Background Investigations Bureau to submit a report on the 
     backlog of personnel security clearance investigations, and 
     for other purposes.


[[Page 3797]]


  A message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1869. An act to reauthorize and rename the position of 
     Whistleblower Ombudsman to be the Whistleblower Protection 
     Coordinator.
```
