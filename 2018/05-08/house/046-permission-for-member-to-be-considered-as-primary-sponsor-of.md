# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 1587 AND H.R. 4199

```
PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 1587 
                             AND H.R. 4199

  Ms. DeLAURO. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the primary sponsor of both H.R. 1587 and 
H.R. 4199, bills originally introduced by Representative Slaughter of 
New York, for the purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting 
reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Budd). Is there objection to the request 
of the gentlewoman from Connecticut?
  There was no objection.
```
