# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       99. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislature of 
     Rockland County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 153 of 
     2018, urging the United States House of Representatives to 
     Pass H.R. 4663 -- the ``Know Your Vets Act''; to the 
     Committee on Armed Services.
       100. Also, a petition of the Legislature of Rockland 
     County, New York, relative to Resolution No. 151 of 2018, 
     urging the United States Senate to Pass S. 994 -- the 
     ``Protecting Religiously Affiliated Institutions Act of 
     2017''; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
