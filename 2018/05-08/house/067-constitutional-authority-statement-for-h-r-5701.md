# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5701

**Measures:** H.R. 5701

```
           By Mr. GRAVES ofMissouri:
       H.R. 5701.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 3.
       ``The Congress shall have power to regulate Commerce . . 
     .''
       Aviation maintenance is a vital component of our nation's 
     aviation system. Without a healthy workforce of aviation 
     technicians and mechanics, the interstate commerce conducted 
     through our national airspace would be endangered. This 
     legislation aims to promote greater study and promotion of 
     this field as a career option for men and women weighing 
     various career options.
```
