# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, unto whom all hearts are open, all desires known, and 
from whom no secrets are hidden, continue to be our refuge and 
strength. Guide our Senators. Let Your peace rule in their hearts. May 
Your Spirit dwell in them richly, imparting Heaven's wisdom. Lord, give 
them steadfast hearts, which no unworthy faults can drag downward.
  Lord, bless America. Make her a channel of justice, peace, and 
goodness to our world.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
