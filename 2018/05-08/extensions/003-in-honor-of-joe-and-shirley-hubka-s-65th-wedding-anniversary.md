# IN HONOR OF JOE AND SHIRLEY HUBKA'S 65TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

```
      IN HONOR OF JOE AND SHIRLEY HUBKA'S 65TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LIZ CHENEY

                               of wyoming

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 8, 2018

  Ms. CHENEY. Mr. Speaker. I rise today to extend my congratulations to 
Joe and Shirley Hubka on the celebration of their 65th Wedding 
Anniversary.
  This significant benchmark is a symbol of their commitment to each 
other and to their family. I am happy to join their friends and family 
in extending my best to them on this special occasion.
  Again, Mr. Speaker, I would like to extend my congratulations to Joe 
and Shirley on the celebration of their 65th Wedding Anniversary. I 
wish them the best today and for many more blessed years to come.
```
