# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 26, 2018

```
                          ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, 
                             JUNE 26, 2018

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., Tuesday, 
June 26; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed. I further ask that following 
leader remarks, the Senate resume consideration of the motion to 
proceed to H.R. 2. Further, I ask that the Senate recess from 12:30 
p.m. until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly conference meetings. 
Finally, I ask that all time during recess, adjournment, morning 
business, and leader remarks count postcloture on the motion to proceed 
to H.R. 2.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
