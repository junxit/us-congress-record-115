# FOSTER HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS VARSITY SWIM TEAM EARNS 5A SILVER MEDAL

```
    FOSTER HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS VARSITY SWIM TEAM EARNS 5A SILVER MEDAL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 27, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Foster High 
School Girls Varsity Swim Team for earning a silver medal in the 200-
yard freestyle.
  Kalissa Niles, Maddie Jacks, Sophie Gibson and Meagan Clark finished 
in second place with an impressive time of 1 minute and 39.47 seconds. 
Their accomplishments in the pool have made their families and the 
Richmond community proud.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations to the ladies of the Foster High School Girls Varsity 
Swim Team. Keep up the great work.

                          ____________________
```
