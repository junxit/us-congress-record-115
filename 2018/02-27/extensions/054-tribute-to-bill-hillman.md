# TRIBUTE TO BILL HILLMAN

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO BILL HILLMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 27, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Bill Hillman of Shenandoah, Iowa on his selection as the 
2017 Outstanding Individual Leader, awarded by the Iowa Tourism Office 
and Travel Federation of Iowa. Bill received this honor for his work 
with the Everly Brothers Childhood Home, Wabash Trace Nature Trail and 
many other community activities.
  Bill has worked tirelessly in promoting economic development and 
tourism opportunities in Shenandoah and the surrounding communities. 
Through Bill's determination and dedication, and with the use of his 
time and resources, he has had a direct impact on the economic future 
of Shenandoah.
  Mr. Speaker, Bill has made a difference by helping and serving his 
community. It is with great honor that I recognize him today. I ask 
that my colleagues in the United States House of Representatives join 
me in honoring his accomplishments and in wishing him nothing but 
continued success.

                          ____________________
```
