# JACK ARMSTRONG BREAKS CLASS 5A SWIMMING RECORD

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
             JACK ARMSTRONG BREAKS CLASS 5A SWIMMING RECORD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 27, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Jack Armstrong, 
of Manvel High School, for earning a pair of gold medals at the Class 
5A Swimming and Diving meet in Austin.
  Armstrong broke the 5A record with a time of 44.57 seconds in the 100 
free in the preliminary round on Friday. On Saturday, he won both the 
50 and 100 free events. His accomplishments in the pool have made his 
family and our Manvel community proud.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Jack Armstrong. We look forward to seeing what 
he will accomplish in the future. Hoka Hey.

                          ____________________
```
