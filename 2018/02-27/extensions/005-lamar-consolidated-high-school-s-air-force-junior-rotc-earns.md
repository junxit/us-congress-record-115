# LAMAR CONSOLIDATED HIGH SCHOOL'S AIR FORCE JUNIOR ROTC EARNS HIGHEST ASSESSMENT SCORE

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
 LAMAR CONSOLIDATED HIGH SCHOOL'S AIR FORCE JUNIOR ROTC EARNS HIGHEST 
                            ASSESSMENT SCORE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 27, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the instructors and 
cadets of Lamar Consolidated High School's Air Force Junior ROTC for 
receiving the assessment score of ``Exceeds Standards.''
  The JROTC program received the ``Exceeds Standards'' designation, the 
highest rating attainable by a JROTC program, based on a formal Unit 
Evaluation on December 4. Major Jeffrey M. Shelton and Senior Master 
Sergeant Jeffrey T. Moffet have created a learning environment where 
cadets take great pride in accomplishing their goals and making a 
positive impact on their community. They are outstanding leaders and 
role models for their cadets, Maeseille Calonia, Jesus Lopez, 
Christopher Coats, Manuel Arriaga, Carolina Garcia, Adrianna Rios, 
Matthew Alfaro, Azahel Vazquez, Julian Vinsonhaler and Isabel Arviso, 
who performed exceptionally well during their evaluation. Thanks to 
this JROTC program, they are paving the way to make outstanding 
military leaders.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second District of Texas, congratulations 
again to Lamar Consolidated High School's Air Force JROTC for receiving 
this high assessment score. Keep up the fantastic work.

                          ____________________
```
