# TRIBUTE TO JACOB BAUDLER

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JACOB BAUDLER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 27, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Jacob Baudler, of Fontanelle, Iowa for earning the 
American FFA Degree. Jacob was recently awarded this degree at the 
National FFA Convention in Indianapolis, Indiana. He was a member of 
the Nodaway Valley FFA Organization and is the son of John and Darla 
Baudler.
  The American FFA Degree is awarded to members who have demonstrated 
the highest level of commitment to FFA and made significant 
accomplishments in their supervised agricultural experience. Jacob had 
to meet certain requirements, such as studying agriculture for three 
years in high school, earning money in an agriculture field and 
investing that money into their business, participation in community 
service and having a record of outstanding leadership ability and 
community involvement. The work toward this degree involved many years 
of hard work and dedication in agricultural pursuits.

[[Page 2882]]

  Mr. Speaker, it is a profound honor to represent leaders like Jacob 
in the United States Congress and it is with great pride that I 
recognize and applaud him for utilizing his talents to reach his goals. 
I ask that my colleagues in the United States House of Representatives 
join me in congratulating Jacob on receiving this esteemed designation 
and in wishing him the best of luck in all his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
