# MO'S PLACE CELEBRATES 30 YEARS

```
                         MO'S PLACE CELEBRATES
                                30 YEARS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 27, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Mo's Place, of 
Katy, TX, on its 30th Anniversary.
  Mo Jeloudarzadeh came to America with only his suitcase in hand, and 
worked his way through college as a dishwasher. He was later promoted 
to bartender and it was during this time that Mo fell in love with 
country music. In March 1988, he opened Mo's Place in a small strip 
center in the Katy area. Mo's passion for serving others and his 
welcoming hospitality have been the key ingredients to his success. 
Last year, Mo was awarded the Pioneer Award from the Texas Country 
Music Hall of Fame for his continued support of Texas Country music 
artists.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Mo Jeloudarzadeh for celebrating 30 years of 
success at Mo's Place. We're excited to see what the next 30 years 
bring.

                          ____________________
```
