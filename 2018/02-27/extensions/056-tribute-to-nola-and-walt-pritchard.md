# TRIBUTE TO NOLA AND WALT PRITCHARD

```
                   TRIBUTE TO NOLA AND WALT PRITCHARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 27, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Nola and Walt Pritchard of Clarinda, Iowa on the very 
special occasion of their 65th wedding anniversary. They celebrated 
their anniversary on December 23, 2017.
  Nola and Walt's lifelong commitment to each other and their family 
truly embodies Iowa values. As they reflect on their 65th anniversary, 
may their commitment grow even stronger, as they continue to love, 
cherish, and honor one another for many years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend this great couple on their 65th year together 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion and in wishing them nothing but the best.

                          ____________________
```
