# IN HONOR OF EUGENE AND RUTH ANN YINGER'S 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

```
   IN HONOR OF EUGENE AND RUTH ANN YINGER'S 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LIZ CHENEY

                               of wyoming

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 10, 2018

  Ms. CHENEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my congratulations to 
Eugene and Ruth Ann Yinger on their 50th Wedding Anniversary.
  This significant benchmark is a symbol of their commitment to each 
other and to their family. I am happy to join their friends and family 
in extending my best to them on this special occasion.
  Again, Mr. Speaker, I would like to extend my congratulations to 
Eugene and Ruth Ann on the celebration of their 50th Wedding 
Anniversary. I wish them the best today and for many more blessed years 
to come.

                          ____________________
```
