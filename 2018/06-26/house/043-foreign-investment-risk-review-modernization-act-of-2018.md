appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2019, and for other purposes; and the bill H.R. 2083, the 
Endangered Salmon and Fisheries Predation Prevention Act, I would have 
voted ``no.''
  On rollcall No. 292, On Agreeing to the Resolution, Providing for 
consideration of the bill H.R. 6157, making appropriations for the 
Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, 
and for other purposes; and the bill H.R. 2083, the Endangered Salmon 
and Fisheries Predation Prevention Act, I would have voted ``no.''
  On rollcall No. 293, On Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass, as

of 2017, I would have voted ``yes.''

voted ``no.''
