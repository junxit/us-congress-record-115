# PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

```
                        PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

  The following petition or memorial was laid before the Senate and was 
referred or ordered to lie on the table as indicated:

       POM-251. A resolution adopted by the Jackson County Chamber 
     of Commerce, Jackson County, Mississippi memorializing its 
     support for continued and increased exploration and 
     production of the Gulf of Mexico energy resources and urging 
     the United States Congress to keep its commitment under the 
     Gulf of Mexico Energy Security Act to share Outer Continental 
     Shelf (OCS) revenues with Gulf producing states and their 
     coastal political subdivisions; to the Committee on Energy 
     and Natural Resources.
```
