# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 25, 2018

```
                          ORDERS FOR MONDAY, 
                             JUNE 25, 2018

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
June 25; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed. I further ask that following 
leader remarks, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 5895 under the 
previous order. Finally, I ask that the cloture motion filed during 
today's session ripen following disposition of H.R. 5895.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
