**Measures:** H.R. 6, H.Res. 949

 PERMISSION TO MODIFY CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6, SUBSTANCE USE-DISORDER 
PREVENTION THAT PROMOTES OPIOID RECOVERY AND TREATMENT FOR PATIENTS AND
