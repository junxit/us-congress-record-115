# COMMUNICATION FROM DEPUTY CHIEF OF STAFF, DISTRICT DIRECTOR, THE HONORABLE MATTHEW A. CARTWRIGHT, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM DEPUTY CHIEF OF STAFF, DISTRICT DIRECTOR, THE 
          HONORABLE MATTHEW A. CARTWRIGHT, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Bob Morgan, Deputy Chief of Staff, District 
Director, the Honorable Matthew A. Cartwright, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, December 27, 2017.
     Hon. Paul D. Ryan,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to notify you formally pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives 
     that I have been served with a subpoena for documents and 
     testimony, issued by the Court of Common Pleas for Lackawanna 
     County, Pennsylvania.
       After consulting with the Office of General Counsel, I will 
     make the determinations required by Rule VIII.
           Sincerely,
                                                       Bob Morgan,
     Deputy Chief of Staff, District Director.
```
