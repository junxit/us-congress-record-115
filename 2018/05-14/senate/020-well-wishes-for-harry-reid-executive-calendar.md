# Well Wishes for Harry Reid (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                       Well Wishes for Harry Reid

  Mr. President, on one final subject, the Senate's thoughts this 
afternoon are with our former colleague, the previous Democratic 
leader, Senator Reid, who underwent surgery today for a pancreatic 
tumor. According to his family's statement, the procedure went well, 
and chemotherapy will follow in the days ahead.
  So, Harry, let me say that we are all in your corner, and we wish you 
the very best in this fight.

                          ____________________
```
