# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:04 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3053. An act to amend the Nuclear Waste Policy Act of 
     1982, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to section 201(b) of the 
International Religious Freedom Act of 1998 (22 U.S.C. 6431) and the 
order of the House of January 3, 2017, the Speaker reappoints the 
following individual on the part of the House of Representatives to the 
Commission on International Religious Freedom for a term effective May 
14, 2018, and ending May 14, 2020: Dr. Tenzin Dorjee of Fullerton, 
California.

                          ____________________
```
