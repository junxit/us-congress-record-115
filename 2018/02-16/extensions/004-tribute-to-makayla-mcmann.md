# TRIBUTE TO MAKAYLA McMANN

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO MAKAYLA McMANN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 16, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Makayla McMann. Makayla was recently named a winner of the 
Uncommon Students Award. The award is given through the Hoover 
Presidential Library-Museum in West Branch, Iowa.
  The Uncommon Student Award program annually identifies and honors up 
to 15 Iowa high school juniors who propose and accomplish a project of 
their own design. Makayla created and implemented a day camp in the 
small town of Villisca, Iowa. The camp ran for five weeks and served 
students in kindergarten through sixth grade.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize Makayla for this award and I 
am proud to represent her in the United States Congress. I ask that my 
colleagues in the United States House of Representatives join me in 
congratulating Makayla for receiving this outstanding award and in 
wishing her nothing but continued success.
```
