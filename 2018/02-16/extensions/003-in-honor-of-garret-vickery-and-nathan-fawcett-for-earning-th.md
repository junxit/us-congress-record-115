# IN HONOR OF GARRET VICKERY AND NATHAN FAWCETT FOR EARNING THE EAGLE SCOUT AWARD

**Speaking:** Cheney, Liz (C001109) [R-WY]

```
  IN HONOR OF GARRET VICKERY AND NATHAN FAWCETT FOR EARNING THE EAGLE 
                              SCOUT AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LIZ CHENEY

                               of wyoming

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 16, 2018

  Ms. CHENEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my congratulations to 
Garret Vickery and Nathan Fawcett on earning the Eagle Scout Award.
  Earning the rank of Eagle Scout requires fulfilling rigorous 
requirements and a dedication to hard work. Throughout their time as 
part of Boy Scouts of America Wyoming Troop 145, Garret and Nathan have 
exhibited outstanding leadership abilities, service to their community, 
and outdoor skills that have made it apparent that they are deserving 
of this award. I commend their commitment to scouting, excellence, and 
higher education. I have no doubt the lessons they have learned 
throughout their time in the Boy Scouts will benefit them for many 
years to come.
  Again, Mr. Speaker, I would like to extend my congratulations to 
Garret Vickery and Nathan Fawcett for earning the Eagle Scout Award. 
Both them and their families deserve to be very proud of this 
significant honor and achievement.

                          ____________________
```
