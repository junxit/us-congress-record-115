# TRIBUTE TO JOHN REASONER

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JOHN REASONER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 16, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate John Reasoner of Creston, Iowa for being named Creston 
Youth of the Year for 2017. John is the son of Mike and Margaret 
Reasoner.
  John has participated in numerous activities during his time at 
Creston Community High School. He has been active in choir, band, 
winter drumline, speech and musicals. He has also spent three years on 
the student body government and this year as the student body 
President. He credits his parents, family and some great teachers for 
his success as a student.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to represent community leaders like John in 
the United States Congress and it is with great pride that I recognize 
him today. I ask that my colleagues in the United States House of 
Representatives join me in congratulating John for this outstanding 
recognition and in wishing him nothing but continued success.

                          ____________________
```
