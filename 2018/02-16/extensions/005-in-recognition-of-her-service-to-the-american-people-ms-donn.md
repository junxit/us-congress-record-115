# IN RECOGNITION OF HER SERVICE TO THE AMERICAN PEOPLE: MS. DONNA JIMENEZ

**Speaking:** Lowenthal, Alan S. (L000579) [D-CA]

```
IN RECOGNITION OF HER SERVICE TO THE AMERICAN PEOPLE: MS. DONNA JIMENEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ALAN S. LOWENTHAL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 16, 2018

  Mr. LOWENTHAL. Mr. Speaker, after twenty years of dedicated service 
to the American people as a staff member with the United States House 
of Representatives, Ms. Donna Jimenez will officially retire on 
February 28, 2018.
  Ms. Jimenez served with honor and distinction as the longtime 
District Office Manager for Congressman David Dreier from 1997 to 2012, 
and as a Senior Constituent Services Representative for my office and 
the residents of California's 47th District from 2013 until her 
retirement.
  During her time in the Legislative Branch, Ms. Jimenez served 
thousands of constituents of both Congressional districts, helping 
solve challenging cases with numerous federal departments and agencies. 
She demonstrated leadership and dedication in helping people navigate 
the federal bureaucracy, and derived great joy and satisfaction in 
serving those in most need of advocacy and assistance, among them 
including our nation's veterans, seniors, the disabled, and those 
immigrants seeking a brighter future in America.
  In addition to her dedicated service to the American people, Ms. 
Jimenez further distinguished herself by earning her Master of Public 
Administration degree with an emphasis in Immigration, from the 
University of LaVerne in 2002.
  It is with great pride and gratitude that I extend my best wishes to 
Ms. Donna Jimenez upon her retirement from the United States House of 
Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
