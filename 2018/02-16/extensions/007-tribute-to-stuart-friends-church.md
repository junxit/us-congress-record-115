# TRIBUTE TO STUART FRIENDS CHURCH

```
                    TRIBUTE TO STUART FRIENDS CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 16, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate a special Iowa church congregation from Stuart, Iowa. 
Stuart Friends Church recently celebrated the 140th anniversary of 
their congregation.
  In 1855, pioneers founded the first church in the Stuart community. A 
new building was built in 1877, named Stuart Meeting of Friends and is 
still in use today. The congregation supports missions locally and 
abroad, and welcomes all people.
  Mr. Speaker, I applaud and congratulate the members of Stuart Friends 
Church for their many years of faithful attendance and service to their 
community. I am proud to represent them in the United States Congress 
and I ask that my colleagues join me in congratulating Stuart Friends 
Church and wishing them all nothing but the best in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
