# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 12, 2018

**Speaking:** Corker, Bob (C001071) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 5515

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 12, 2018

  Mr. CORKER. Mr. President, I don't see anybody else on the floor who 
wishes to speak. So I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., Tuesday, June 
12; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and morning business be closed. I further ask that following leader 
remarks, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 5515. Finally, I ask 
that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the 
weekly conference meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
