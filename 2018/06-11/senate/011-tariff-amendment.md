# TARIFF AMENDMENT

**Speaking:** Corker, Bob (C001071) [R-TN]

```
                            TARIFF AMENDMENT

  Mr. CORKER. Mr. President, I had a conversation with Senator Inhofe 
and Senator Reed earlier. I talked a little bit about resolving a blue-
slip issue. I think many Members here are aware that I want to offer an 
amendment that deals with our ability to weigh in on the tariffs that 
were put in place under section 232 of the trade act--national security 
issues.
  I think we may have found a resolution to that, but I don't want to 
offer it right now. I know that Senator Inhofe, not knowing whether it 
works yet for him and the committee, would have to object, and I don't 
want that to be the case. I want us to continue to work on this. So I 
am not going to offer an amendment and statement thereof relative to 
this.

                          ____________________
```
