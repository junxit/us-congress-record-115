# AUTHORIZATION FOR DOCUMENTARY PRODUCTION BY SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
    AUTHORIZATION FOR DOCUMENTARY PRODUCTION BY SELECT COMMITTEE ON 
                              INTELLIGENCE

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, the Select Committee on Intelligence 
has received a request from the Department of Justice for records 
pertinent to a pending investigation arising out of allegations of the 
unauthorized disclosure of information by a former employee of the 
Committee.
  This resolution would authorize the Chairman and Vice Chairman of the 
Select Committee on Intelligence, acting jointly, to provide records in 
response to this request from the Department of Justice.

                          ____________________
```
