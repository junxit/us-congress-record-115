# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6020

**Measures:** H.R. 6020

```
           By Mr. BISHOP of Michigan:
       H.R. 6020.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, which provides that Congress shall 
     have the authority to ``establish post offices and post 
     roads.''
```
