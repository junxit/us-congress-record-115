# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 589, H.R. 1109, H.R. 5895

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on September 18, 
2018, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills:

       H.R. 1109. To amend section 203 of the Federal Power Act.
       H.R. 5895. Making consolidated appropriation for Energy and 
     Water Development, the Legislative Branch, Military 
     Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies for the 
     fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 589. To establish Department of Energy policy science 
     and energy research and development programs, and reform 
     National Laboratory management and technology transfer 
     programs, and for other purposes.
```
