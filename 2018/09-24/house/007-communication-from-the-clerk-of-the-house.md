# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 46, S.Con.Res. 47, H.R. 698, H.R. 1551, S. 3479

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                               Washington, DC, September 20, 2018.
     Hon. Paul D. Ryan,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate on September 20, 2018, at 
     2:21 p.m.:
       That the Senate passed S. 3479.
       That the Senate agreed to S. Con Res. 47.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 698.
       That the Senate passed with an amendment H.R. 46.
       That the Senate passed with an amendment H.R. 1551.
       Appointment:
       Public Safety Officer Medal of Valor Review Board
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
     Karen L. Haas.

                          ____________________
```
