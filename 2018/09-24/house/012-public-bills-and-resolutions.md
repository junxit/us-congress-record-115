# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 6862, H.R. 6863, H.R. 6864, H.R. 6865, H.R. 6866

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. BRAT (for himself and Mr. DeSaulnier):
       H.R. 6862. A bill to reauthorize the Congressional Award 
     Act; to the Committee on Education and the Workforce.
           By Mr. PEARCE:
       H.R. 6863. A bill to name the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs community-based outpatient clinic in Las Cruces, New 
     Mexico, as the ``Las Cruces Bataan Memorial Clinic''; to the 
     Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
           By Ms. DelBENE (for herself and Mr. Jeffries):
       H.R. 6864. A bill to require the Federal Trade Commission 
     to promulgate regulations related to sensitive personal 
     information or behavioral data, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Mr. KUSTOFF of Tennessee:
       H.R. 6865. A bill to direct the Comptroller General of the 
     United States to conduct certain studies, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Financial Services.
           By Mr. LaHood (for himself and Mrs. Bustos):
       H.R. 6866. A bill to clarify exclusions from the definition 
     of a deposit broker; to the Committee on Financial Services.
```
