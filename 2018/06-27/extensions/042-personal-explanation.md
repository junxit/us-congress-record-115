**Speaking:** Graves, Tom (G000560) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 961, H.R. 4294

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TOM GRAVES

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 27, 2018

  Mr. GRAVES of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, on June 26, 2018, I missed 
several votes, as I was at the White House meeting with President Trump 
to discuss the way forward on the Appropriations process.
  I missed Roll Call Number 291, Ordering the Previous Question on H. 
Res. 961. Had I been present, I would have voted yes.
  I missed Roll Call Number 292, on agreeing to H. Res. 961. Had I been 
present, I would have voted yes.
  I missed Roll Call Number 293, on passage of H.R. 4294, the 
Prevention of Private Information Dissemination Act. Had I been 
present, I would have voted yes.
```
