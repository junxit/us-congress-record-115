# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  God of wisdom and order, who filled the universe with mysteries of 
Your power, restore us to the image of Your glory. Create in us clean 
hearts, and cleanse us from all unrighteousness. Lord, we thank You for 
Your life-giving Spirit flowing through us to reinforce our faith, 
hope, and love.
  Inspire our Senators. Give them truth in their innermost being, a 
sense of dedication in their work, and a spirit of cooperation that 
they may labor together for the good of this land we love. May they 
strive to do justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with You.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
