# Welcoming Maile Pearl Bowlsbey to the Senate Floor (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

```
           Welcoming Maile Pearl Bowlsbey to the Senate Floor

  Mr. MERKLEY. Mr. President, it was very exciting to have Maile Pearl 
with us on the floor just moments ago. It was certainly the first time 
I have seen a little baby on the floor of the Senate and may be a good 
symbol for the work the Senate will be doing in the future for the 
children of the United States of America.
  Congratulations to Senator Tammy Duckworth and her husband, Bryan 
Bowlsbey, for this miraculous addition to American citizenry. We are 
looking forward to seeing little Maile as she grows and prospers in all 
of the chapters of her life ahead.
```
