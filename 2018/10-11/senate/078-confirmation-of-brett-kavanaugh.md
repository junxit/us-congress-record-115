# CONFIRMATION OF BRETT KAVANAUGH

**Speaking:** Collins, Susan M. (C001035) [R-ME]

```
                    CONFIRMATION OF BRETT KAVANAUGH

  Ms. COLLINS. Mr. President, I rise today regarding remarks I made on 
October 5, 2018, on the nomination of Brett Kavanaugh. In reference to 
Justices O'Connor, Souter, and Kennedy, I misspoke and should have said 
that pro-choice groups opposed two of the nominations, not each.
  My remarks should have read, ``Furthermore, pro-choice groups 
vigorously opposed two of the Justices' nominations.''

                          ____________________
```
