# RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 680, S.Res. 681, S.Res. 682, S.Res. 683

```
                      RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to the en bloc consideration of the following Senate 
resolutions, which were submitted earlier today: S. Res. 680, S. Res. 
681, S. Res. 682, and S. Res. 683.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolutions en bloc.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent the resolutions be agreed to; 
that the preambles be agreed to; and that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table, all en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolutions were agreed to.
  The preambles were agreed to.
  (The resolutions, with their preambles, are printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
