# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 684

```
                       ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the provisions 
of S. Res. 684 and do so as a further mark of respect for the late 
Joseph D. ``Joe'' Tydings, former Senator for the State of Maryland.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 9:39 p.m., adjourned until 
Friday, October 12, 2018, at 12 noon.

                          ____________________
```
