# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H. CON. RES. 72

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 72

```
PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H. CON. RES. 
                                   72

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I be
```
