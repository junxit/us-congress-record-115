# ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, MAY 18, 2018, TO MONDAY, MAY 21, 2018

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
     ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, MAY 18, 2018, TO MONDAY, MAY 21, 2018

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet on Monday, May 21, 2018, 
when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour debate and 2 p.m. for 
legislative business.

request of the gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
