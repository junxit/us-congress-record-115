# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2018

**Speaking:** Gardner, Cory (G000562) [R-CO]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 54

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2018

  Mr. GARDNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, December 13; further, that following the prayer and the 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and morning business be closed; finally, that 
following leader remarks, the Senate resume consideration of S.J. Res. 
54 under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
