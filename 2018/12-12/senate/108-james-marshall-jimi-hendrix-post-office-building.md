# JAMES MARSHALL `JIMI' HENDRIX POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
           JAMES MARSHALL `JIMI' HENDRIX POST OFFICE BUILDING

Postal Service located at 4301 Northeast 4th Street in Renton, 
Washington, as the ``James Marshall `Jimi' Hendrix Post Office
```
