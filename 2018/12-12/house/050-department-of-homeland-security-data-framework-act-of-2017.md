# DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY DATA FRAMEWORK ACT OF 2017

**Measures:** H.R. 2454

```
       DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY DATA FRAMEWORK ACT OF 2017

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Weber of Texas). The unfinished business 
is the question on suspending the rules and concurring in the Senate 
amendment to the bill (H.R. 2454) to direct the Secretary of Homeland 
Security to establish a data framework to provide access for 
appropriate personnel to law enforcement and other information of the 
Department, and for other purposes.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Texas (Mr. Hurd) that the House suspend the rules and 
concur in the Senate amendment.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the Senate amendment was concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
