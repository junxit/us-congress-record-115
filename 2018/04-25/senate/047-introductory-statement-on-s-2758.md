# Introductory Statement on S. 2758

**Speaking:** McCaskill, Claire (M001170) [D-MO]

**Measures:** S. 2758

```
      By Mrs. McCASKILL (for herself and Mr. Blunt):
  S. 2758. A bill to amend title 36, United States Code, to provide for 
the display of the National League of Families POW/MIA flag at the 
World War I Memorials; considered and passed.

                                S. 2758

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. DISPLAY OF NATIONAL LEAGUE OF FAMILIES POW/MIA 
                   FLAG AT THE WORLD WAR I MEMORIALS.

       (a) In General.--Subsection (d)(3) of section 902 of title 
     36, United States Code, is amended by striking ``The World 
     War II Memorial,'' and inserting ``The World War I Memorials, 
     the World War II Memorial,''.
       (b) Days for Display.--Subsection (c)(2)(A) of such section 
     is amended by inserting ``the World War I Memorials,'' before 
     ``the World War II Memorial,''.
```
