# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 6651, H.R. 7187

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on November 30, 
2018, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills:

       H.R. 7187. To extend the National Flood Insurance Program 
     until December 7, 2018.
       H.R. 6651. To extend certain authorities relating to United 
     States efforts to combat HIV/AIDS, tuberculosis, and malaria 
     globally, and for other purposes.
```
