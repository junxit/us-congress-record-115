```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

the Clerk of the House of Representatives:
                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, November 30, 2018.
     Hon. Paul D. Ryan,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
```
