# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 4, 2018

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, DECEMBER 4, 2018

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 2:30 p.m., 
Tuesday, December 4; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators allowed to speak for up to 10 minutes each for debate only.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
