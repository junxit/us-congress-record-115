# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we trust You. Lead us in the paths that bring glory to 
Your Name. Point us to the right road, guiding us with Your truth so 
that we will honor You with our lives.
  Give our lawmakers gratitude for the work they do each day and for 
the opportunities to make a positive impact upon our Nation and world. 
Increase in their hearts the qualities of love, knowledge, and 
obedience, enabling our Senators to grow daily in Your likeness.
  And Lord, we praise You for the life and legacy of a gentle 
statesman, President George Herbert Walker Bush.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
