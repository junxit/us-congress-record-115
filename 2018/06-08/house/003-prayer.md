# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, the psalmist reminds us that ``The Lord's is 
the Earth and its fullness, the world, and those who dwell in it.'' 
Indeed, all of creation bears the image of Your beauty and Your love.
  May Your spirit guide the work of our hands to nurture in love the 
people of this Earth and the beautiful world that sustains them.
  We make this prayer in the power of Your spirit, and in Your holy 
name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
