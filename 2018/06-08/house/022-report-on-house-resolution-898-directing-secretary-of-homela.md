```
REPORT ON HOUSE RESOLUTION 898 DIRECTING SECRETARY OF HOMELAND SECURITY 
                     TO TRANSMIT CERTAIN DOCUMENTS

adverse privileged report (Rept. No. 115-

[[Page H5001]]

714) on the resolution (H. Res. 898) directing the Secretary of
```
