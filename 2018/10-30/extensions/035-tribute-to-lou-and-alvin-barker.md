# TRIBUTE TO LOU AND ALVIN BARKER

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO LOU AND ALVIN BARKER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 30, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Lou and Alvin Barker of Bedford, Iowa, on the very special 
occasion of their 70th wedding anniversary.
  Lou and Alvin's lifelong commitment to each other and their family 
truly embodies our Iowa values. As they reflect on their 70th 
anniversary, may their commitment grow even stronger, as they continue 
to love, cherish, and honor one another for many years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend this great couple on their 70th year together, 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion and in wishing them both nothing but continued 
success.

                          ____________________
```
