# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1002

**Speaking:** McKinley, David B. (M001180) [R-WV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1002

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1002

  Mr. McKINLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 1002, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Dent of Pennsylvania, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from West Virginia?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
