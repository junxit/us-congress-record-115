# TRIBUTE TO JOHN DAVID RICHARDSON

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO JOHN DAVID RICHARDSON

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to JD for his hard work as an 
intern in my Rock Springs office. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  JD is a native of Green River. He is a junior at the University of 
Wyoming, where he is studying political science and public law. He has 
demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made him an invaluable 
asset to our office. The quality of his work is reflected in his great 
efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank JD for the dedication he has shown while working for 
me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. I 
know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. I 
wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
