# TRIBUTE TO GAVIN HEADY

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO GAVIN HEADY

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to

[[Page S5358]]

express my appreciation to Gavin for his hard work as an intern in my 
Casper office. I recognize his efforts and contributions to my office, 
as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Gavin is a native of Casper. He is a sophomore at Casper College, 
where he is studying anthropology. He has demonstrated a strong work 
ethic, which has made him an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of his work is reflected in his great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I want to thank Gavin for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
