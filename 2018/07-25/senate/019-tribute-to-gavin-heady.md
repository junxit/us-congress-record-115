                         TRIBUTE TO GAVIN HEADY

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Gavin for his hard work as an 
intern in my Casper office. I recognize his efforts and contributions

for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team.
