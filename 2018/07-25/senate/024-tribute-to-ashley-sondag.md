# TRIBUTE TO ASHLEY SONDAG

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ASHLEY SONDAG

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Ashley for her hard work as 
an intern in my Casper office. I recognize her efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Ashley is a native of Casper. She is a graduate student at Idaho 
State University, where she is studying public administration and 
environmental policy. She has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which 
has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of her work 
is reflected in her great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Ashley for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
