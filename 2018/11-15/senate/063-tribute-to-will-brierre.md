# TRIBUTE TO WILL BRIERRE

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO WILL BRIERRE

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Will for his hard work as an 
intern in the Environment and Public Works Committee. I recognize his 
efforts and contributions to my office, as well as to the State of 
Wyoming.
  Will is a native of Oklahoma. He is a graduate student at the George 
Washington University, where he is studying Environmental Resource 
Policy. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made him an 
invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is reflected in 
his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Will for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
