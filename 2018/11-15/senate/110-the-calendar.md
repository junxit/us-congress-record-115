# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of the following calendar bills, 
en bloc: Calendar Nos. 588 through 607.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bills 
en bloc.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
committee-reported amendments be agreed to where applicable and that 
the bills, as amended, if amended, be considered read a third time and 
passed, and the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table, all en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
