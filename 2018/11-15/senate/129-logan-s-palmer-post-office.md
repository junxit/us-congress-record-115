# LOGAN S. PALMER POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 5935

```
                      LOGAN S. PALMER POST OFFICE

  A bill (H.R. 5935) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1355 North Meridian Road in Harristown, 
Illinois, as the ``Logan S. Palmer Post Office,'' was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
