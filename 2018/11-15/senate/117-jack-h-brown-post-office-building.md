# JACK H. BROWN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2979

```
                   JACK H. BROWN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 2979) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 390 West 5th Street in San Bernardino, 
California, as the ``Jack H. Brown Post Office Building,'' was ordered 
to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
