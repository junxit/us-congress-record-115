# REMEMBERING DANIEL STEIN

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

```
                        REMEMBERING DANIEL STEIN

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, today I wish to remember Daniel Stein. Dan 
was 1 of the 11 people who was violently killed at the Tree of Life 
Synagogue in Pittsburgh's Squirrel Hill neighborhood.
  When Fred Rogers, better known as Mister Rogers, told children to 
``look for the helpers,'' he could have been talking about Dan Stein. 
Whenever and wherever he was needed, for a task big or small, Dan was 
happy to lend a hand. A longtime member of the New Light Congregation 
and, for a time, its president, Dan was a fixture at Saturday services. 
Judaism played a significant role in his life, and he was copresident 
of the area's Hadassah chapter.
  We would all do well to remember Dan Stein's never failing desire to 
help his friends and neighbors and look for opportunities in our own 
lives to do more each day.

                          ____________________
```
