# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 6666

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:02 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 6666. An act to authorize the Secretary of the 
     Interior to grant to States and local governments easements 
     and rights-of-way over Federal land within Gateway National 
     Recreation Area for construction, operation, and maintenance 
     of projects for control and prevention of flooding and 
     shoreline erosion.
```
