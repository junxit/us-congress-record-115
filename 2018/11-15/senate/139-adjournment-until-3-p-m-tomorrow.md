# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 3 P.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 700

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 3 P.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the provisions 
of S. Res. 700 and do so as a further mark of respect for the late 
Walter ``Dee'' Huddleston, former Senator from the Commonwealth of 
Kentucky.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 5:32 p.m., adjourned until Friday, November 
16, 2018, at 3 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
