# HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE BOB CHERRY

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE BOB CHERRY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 15, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize State Representative 
Bob Cherry for his contribution to our state.
  For 20 years, Bob has represented Hancock and Madison counties with 
honor and integrity in the Indiana General Assembly. He continues to be 
a strong advocate for balanced budgets and lower taxes. I had the 
pleasure of serving alongside Bob during my time in the Indiana General 
Assembly. Our state is better off today because of his extraordinary 
leadership and service.
  On a personal note, Bob has been a loyal friend and wise adviser 
during my entire tenure in Congress. He and Deb were among my earliest 
and most vocal supporters. Jennifer and I cherish our friendship with 
Bob and Deb. We want to thank them for their prayers and support of my 
work in Congress. I wish them continued success in all that God has 
planned for their family.

                          ____________________
```
