# HONORING U.S. MARSHAL DAN McCLAIN

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                   HONORING U.S. MARSHAL DAN McCLAIN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 15, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize U.S. Marshal Dan 
McClain for his contribution to our state and Scott County.
  Throughout his career, Dan has dedicated himself to the safety of his 
fellow Hoosiers. Dan served as Scott County Sheriff before being 
nominated by President Trump to be U.S. Marshal for the Southern 
District of Indiana. Building on his success as Scott County Sheriff, 
Dan now works tirelessly to ensure Hoosier communities are safe. 
President Trump could not have picked anyone better for this role and 
our state is better off today because of his extraordinary leadership 
and service.
  On a personal note, Dan has been a loyal friend and wise adviser 
during my entire tenure in Congress. I would like to thank him for

[[Page E1528]]

his friendship and support of my work. I wish him continued success in 
all that God has planned for him and his family.

                          ____________________
```
