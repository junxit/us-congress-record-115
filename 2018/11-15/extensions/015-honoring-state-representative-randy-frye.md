# HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE RANDY FRYE

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE RANDY FRYE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 15, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize State Representative 
Randy Frye for his contribution to our state.
  Since 2010, Randy has represented Dearborn, Decatur, Jefferson, 
Jennings, Ripley, Ohio and Switzerland counties with honor and 
integrity in the Indiana General Assembly. He continues to be a strong 
advocate for Hoosier veterans at the Statehouse, and without a doubt 
Indiana is better off today because of his extraordinary leadership and 
service.
  On a personal note, I want to thank Randy for his friendship and 
support of my work in Congress. I wish him continued success in all 
that God has planned for his family.

                          ____________________
```
