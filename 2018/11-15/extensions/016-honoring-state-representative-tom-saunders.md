# HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE TOM SAUNDERS

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
               HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE TOM SAUNDERS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 15, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize State Representative 
Tom Saunders for his contribution to our state.
  For 22 years, Tom has represented East Central Indiana with honor and 
integrity in the Indiana General Assembly. He has always put the 
interests of his district first and continues to be a strong advocate 
for improving our schools, rebuilding Indiana's infrastructure and 
giving a voice to our local governments. It was an honor to serve with 
him during my time in the Indiana General Assembly.
  On a personal note, Tom has been a loyal friend and wise adviser 
during my entire tenure in Congress. I thank him for his friendship and 
support of my work and wish Tom and his wife, Sue, continued success in 
all that God has planned for their family.

                          ____________________
```
