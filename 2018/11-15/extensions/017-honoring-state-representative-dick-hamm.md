# HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE DICK HAMM

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                HONORING STATE REPRESENTATIVE DICK HAMM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 15, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize State Representative 
Dick Hamm for his contribution to our state.
  Dick represents Wayne County with honor and integrity in the Indiana 
General Assembly. Through his work at the Boys and Girls Club and other 
local organizations, Dick has made a positive impact in his community. 
He continues to be a strong advocate for Hoosier families at the 
Statehouse, and Indiana is better off today because of Dick's 
extraordinary leadership and service.
  On a personal note, I want to thank Dick for his friendship and 
support of my work in Congress. I wish him continued success in all 
that God has planned for his family.
```
