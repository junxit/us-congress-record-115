# HONORING STEVE AUSTIN

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                         HONORING STEVE AUSTIN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 15, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Steve Austin for his 
contribution to our state and country.
  I first met Steve while we were working for former Congressman David 
McIntosh in the 1990s. We've been friends ever since. He is an 
incredible leader and has gone on to have a remarkable career at Red 
Gold in Elwood. His advocacy work has made a tremendous difference, and 
our state is better off today because of his service.
  I would like to thank him for his advice and support over all these 
years. Without a doubt, Steve has been among my earliest and most vocal 
supporters in Delaware County. I wish him continued success in all that 
God has planned for his future.

                          ____________________
```
