# 55TH ANNIVERSARY OF DADE COUNTY POLICE BENEVOLENT ASSOCIATION

**Speaking:** Ros-Lehtinen, Ileana (R000435) [R-FL]

```
     55TH ANNIVERSARY OF DADE COUNTY POLICE BENEVOLENT ASSOCIATION

  (Ms. ROS-LEHTINEN asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. ROS-LEHTINEN. Mr. Speaker, I am pleased to congratulate the Dade 
County Police Benevolent Association on the celebration of its 55th 
anniversary.
  Since its founding in 1963, the Dade County PBA has worked to protect 
the rights of Miami-Dade's finest, working tirelessly to serve as a 
voice of reason, of guidance, and of professionalism. From providing 
legal assistance to members in times of need, to assisting members and 
their families who may be struggling with PTSD, this organization is 
committed to serving those who protect and serve us.
  I represent some of the finest police officers that our Nation has to 
offer, and the Dade County Police Benevolent Association will always 
have their back.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank the Dade County Police Benevolent Association 
for its extraordinary work in our community, and I wish this 
organization the utmost congratulations on this extraordinary 
milestone.
  Here is to the next 55 years, PBA.

                          ____________________
```
