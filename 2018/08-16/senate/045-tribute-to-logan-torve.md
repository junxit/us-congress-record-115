# TRIBUTE TO LOGAN TORVE

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO LOGAN TORVE

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Logan Torve, an 
intern in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Logan is a graduate of Crossroads Academy in Davidson, NC. Currently, 
he is attending Belmont Abbey College in Belmont, NC, where he is 
majoring in political philosophy. He is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Logan for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
