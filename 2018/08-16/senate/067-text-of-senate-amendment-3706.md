# Text of Senate Amendment 3706

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
  SA 3706. Mr. McCONNELL (for Mrs. Fischer (for herself, Ms. Baldwin, 
Ms. Warren, Ms. Hirono, and Mr. Schatz)) proposed an amendment to 
amendment SA 3695 proposed by Mr. Shelby to the bill H.R. 6157, making 
appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2019, and for other purposes; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in title VIII of division A, 
     insert the following:
       Sec. ___. (a) The amount appropriated by title II of this 
     division under the heading ``Operation and Maintenance, 
     Defense-Wide'' is hereby increased by $10,000,000, with the 
     amount of the increase to be available for POW/MIA 
     identification within the Defense Personnel Accounting 
     Agency.
       (b) The amount appropriated by title II of this division 
     under the heading ``Operation and Maintenance, Defense-Wide'' 
     is hereby decreased by $10,000,000.
                                 ______
```
