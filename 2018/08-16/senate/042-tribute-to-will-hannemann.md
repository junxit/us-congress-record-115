# TRIBUTE TO WILL HANNEMANN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO WILL HANNEMANN

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Will Hannemann, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Will is a graduate of Lincoln High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently,

[[Page S5692]]

he is attending Augustana University in Sioux Falls, SD, where he is 
pursuing degrees in history, political science, and secondary 
education. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the 
most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Will for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
