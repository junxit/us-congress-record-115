# TRIBUTE TO NICOLE SCHNEIDER

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO NICOLE SCHNEIDER

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Nicole Schneider, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she 
has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Nicole is a graduate of O'Gorman High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, she is attending the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, where 
she is pursuing degrees in political science, global studies, national 
security studies, and Russian. She is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Nicole for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
