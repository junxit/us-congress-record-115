# RECOGNIZING GREEN BANNER PUBLICATIONS

**Speaking:** Young, Todd (Y000064) [R-IN]

```
                 RECOGNIZING GREEN BANNER PUBLICATIONS

● Mr. YOUNG. Mr. President, I wish to congratulate Green Banner 
Publications on their long and fruitful career in the publishing world. 
As a true family-owned business, the Green family has always served 
their customers as if they were one of their own. Their network of 
local and weekly newspapers delivered current events to more than 
60,000 homes in southern Indiana.
  After 85 years of service, they have made the decision to close up 
shop and end all publications. I thank them for their longtime 
commitment, and I appreciate all of their hard work informing thousands 
of Hoosiers in southern Indiana.●

                          ____________________
```
