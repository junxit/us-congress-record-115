# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI]

```
                      UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, because the time between now and 11:30 
is to be shared between me and the ranking member on the Finance 
Committee, Senator Wyden, I ask unanimous consent that when Senator 
Wyden appears, I yield the floor to him; that at the conclusion of his 
remarks, I be recognized to conclude my remarks; and that in the 
Record, my remarks be consolidated after Senator Wyden's remarks.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
