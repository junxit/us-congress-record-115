# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** S. 534, H.R. 582, S. 1438, H.R. 4708

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D147)
  H.R. 4708, to amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to require the 
Secretary of Homeland Security to issue Department of Homeland 
Security-wide guidance and develop training programs as part of the 
Department of Homeland Security Blue Campaign. Signed on February 14, 
2018. (Public Law 115-125)
  S. 534, to prevent the sexual abuse of minors and amateur athletes by 
requiring the prompt reporting of sexual abuse to law enforcement 
authorities. Signed on February 14, 2018. (Public Law 115-126)
  H.R. 582, to amend the Communications Act of 1934 to require multi-
line telephone systems to have a configuration that permits users to 
directly initiate a call to 9-1-1 without dialing any additional digit, 
code, prefix, or post-fix. Signed on February 16, 2018. (Public Law 
115-127)
  S. 1438, to redesignate the Jefferson National Expansion Memorial in 
the State of Missouri as the ``Gateway Arch National Park''. Signed on 
February 22, 2018. (Public Law 115-128)
```
