# ANWAR SADAT CENTENNIAL CELEBRATION ACT

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 754

```
                 ANWAR SADAT CENTENNIAL CELEBRATION ACT

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Calendar No. 585, H.R. 754.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 754) to award the Congressional Gold Medal to 
     Anwar Sadat in recognition of his heroic achievements and 
     courageous contributions to peace in the Middle East.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered 
read a third time and passed and that the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 754) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.

```
