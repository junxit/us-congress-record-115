# AUTHORIZING THE PRINTING OF A COMMEMORATIVE DOCUMENT IN MEMORY OF THE LATE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES, GEORGE HERBERT WALKER BUSH

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 57

```
 AUTHORIZING THE PRINTING OF A COMMEMORATIVE DOCUMENT IN MEMORY OF THE 
    LATE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES, GEORGE HERBERT WALKER BUSH

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of S. Con. Res. 57.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 57) authorizing the 
     printing of a commemorative document in memory of the late 
     President of the United States, George Herbert Walker Bush.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent that the concurrent resolution 
be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid 
upon the table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 57) was agreed to.
  (The concurrent resolution is printed in today's Record under 
``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
