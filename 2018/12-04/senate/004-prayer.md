# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, who has given us this good land for our heritage, help 
us to be grateful for Your favor and eager to do Your will. Bless our 
citizens with honorable work, sound learning, exemplary conduct, and 
faithful service. Keep our Senators true to You. Use them to defend our 
liberties and to unify our Nation for the good of humanity.
  Lord, give our lawmakers the spirit of wisdom that they may work for 
justice, as they strive to approve what is excellent. Inspire them to 
glorify You in all they think, say, and do.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
