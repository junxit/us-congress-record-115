# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4927

**Measures:** H.R. 4927

```
            By Mr. BRAT:
        H.R. 4927.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8, Clause 4 of the U.S. Constitution 
     states that The Congress shall have Power ``To establish an 
     uniform Rule of Naturalization.''
```
