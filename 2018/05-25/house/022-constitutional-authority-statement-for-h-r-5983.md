# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5983

**Measures:** H.R. 5983

```
           By Mr. YODER:
       H.R. 5983.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 3, The Congress shal 
     have power To lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and 
     Excises, to pay the Debts and provide for the common defence 
     and the general Welfare of the United States; but all Duties, 
     Imposts and Excises shall be uniform throughout the United 
     States; To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes;
```
