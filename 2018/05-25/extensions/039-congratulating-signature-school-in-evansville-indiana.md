# CONGRATULATING SIGNATURE SCHOOL IN EVANSVILLE, INDIANA

```
         CONGRATULATING SIGNATURE SCHOOL IN EVANSVILLE, INDIANA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LARRY BUCSHON

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 25, 2018

  Mr. BUCSHON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my congratulations 
to Signature School in Evansville, Indiana for being named the ninth 
best charter school in the United States, the seventeenth best high 
school in the United States, and best high school in Indiana by U.S. 
News and World Report in its 2018 rankings.
  Founded in 1992, Signature School became Indiana's first public 
charter school in 2002. Located on Main Street in downtown Evansville, 
Signature School has offered an advanced education for students all 
over Southwest Indiana for over 25 years. The education young Hoosiers 
receive at Signature School not only helps them prepare for college, 
but also for success in life. Signature School students routinely out 
perform national SAT and ACT averages, are recognized as National Merit 
Finalists, achieve a 100 percent graduation rate, and are offered 
millions of dollars in college scholarships.
  Ensuring all Hoosiers have access to a quality education is of the 
utmost importance because today's youth are tomorrow's leaders. I am 
grateful for Signature School's commitment to educating young Hoosiers 
and congratulate them on receiving this outstanding distinction.

                          ____________________
```
