# CELEBRATING THE PLATTSBURGH STATE UNIVERSITY POLICE DEPARTMENT'S 50TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Stefanik, Elise M. (S001196) [R-NY]

```
 CELEBRATING THE PLATTSBURGH STATE UNIVERSITY POLICE DEPARTMENT'S 50TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 25, 2018

  Ms. STEFANIK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the 
Plattsburgh State University Police Department on its 50th anniversary.
  The Plattsburgh State University Police Department is responsible for 
keeping the largest university in New York's 21st district a safe place 
for students to learn. The officers are exceptionally trained to 
respond to physical and mental health crises, campus safety concerns, 
and other emergencies. They are dedicated to keeping the campus 
community and surrounding city of Plattsburgh safe and secure.
  The Department is an integral part of the SUNY Plattsburgh campus. It 
demonstrates a commitment to crime prevention and campus policing, as 
well as successfully aligns with the university's educational mission 
and prides itself on its student-centered approach.
  On behalf of New York's 21st district, I would like to congratulate 
the Plattsburgh State University Police Department on an exceptional 50 
years and thank the officers for their dedicated service and commitment 
to keeping SUNY Plattsburgh safe and secure.

                          ____________________
```
