# IN RECOGNITION OF DR. ROBERT AND MRS. RITA ROBBINS

```
           IN RECOGNITION OF DR. ROBERT AND MRS. RITA ROBBINS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 22, 2018

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr. Robert and 
Mrs. Rita Robbins, who have dedicated their lives to caring for 
Kentuckians as surgeon and surgical nurse, respectively, at Hardin 
Memorial Hospital.
  Dr. and Mrs. Robbins met when they were working together at Hardin 
Memorial Hospital, and fittingly were married in the hospital's chapel. 
They have become fixtures in the Hardin County community through their 
work at the hospital and beyond.
  Dr. and Mrs. Robbins embody the definition of servant leadership, and 
I am proud to honor them.

                          ____________________
```
