# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 1012, H.Res. 1047

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to House Resolution 1047 and 
section 2(b) of House Resolution 1012, the House stands adjourned until 
2 p.m. on Tuesday, September 4, 2018, as a further mark of respect to 
the memory of the late Honorable John Sidney McCain, III.
  Thereupon (at 9 o'clock and 33 minutes a.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until Tuesday, September 4, 2018, at 2 p.m. 
as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable John 
Sidney McCain, III.
```
