# IN MEMORIAM TOBY ROBERTA STAHL

**Speaking:** Sinema, Kyrsten (S001191) [D-AZ]

```
                     IN MEMORIAM TOBY ROBERTA STAHL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. KYRSTEN SINEMA

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 31, 2018

  Ms. SINEMA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
Toby Roberta Stahl who passed away on August 19, 2018 at the age of 80. 
Mrs. Stahl was an activist who fought for human rights and worked 
toward equality and justice for all in Arizona. Our state will miss her 
dearly.
  When Mrs. Stahl retired from her career as a computer programmer in 
2000, she dedicated her time and efforts to fighting for education, 
healthcare, protecting families and human rights. She worked tirelessly 
for the Democratic party as a volunteer, serving as Precinct 
Committeeman and member of the Arizona State Democratic Committee. Mrs. 
Stahl also served as the president of Democracy for America-Maricopa 
County for many years.
  Outside of politics, Mrs. Stahl worked to preserve Arizona's natural 
history, was a devout Diamondbacks fan, and spent time with her four 
grandsons. She also served as the auxiliary president of Pueblo Grande 
Museum in Phoenix for three years.
  Mrs. Stahl is survived by her three children, Barry, Fran, and 
Carrie, her son-in-law, Lewis Dickson, daughter-in-law, Patricia 
Angelucci, and her four grandsons, Jonas, Kai, Rei and Ari. Please join 
me in honoring her memory.

                          ____________________
```
