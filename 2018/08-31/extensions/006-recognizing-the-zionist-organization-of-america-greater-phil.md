# RECOGNIZING THE ZIONIST ORGANIZATION OF AMERICA GREATER PHILADELPHIA CHAPTER'S 2018 GALA

**Speaking:** Barletta, Lou (B001269) [R-PA]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE ZIONIST ORGANIZATION OF AMERICA GREATER PHILADELPHIA 
                          CHAPTER'S 2018 GALA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LOU BARLETTA

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 31, 2018

  Mr. BARLETTA. Mr. Speaker, it is a great honor to recognize the 
Zionist Organization of America's Greater Philadelphia Chapter as they 
celebrate their 2018 Gala. This event highlights America and Israel's 
important relationship, as well as provides an opportunity to honor 
individuals who have been especially active in their communities.
  Since its founding in 1897, the Zionist Organization of America has 
sought to raise awareness of the American-Israeli relationship and to 
educate the American public on the realities of the Israeli experience. 
The Greater Philadelphia Chapter has remained a strong source of 
Israeli activism, as it provides educational opportunities and 
resources to the citizens of my home state of Pennsylvania.
  I want to extend a special congratulations to this year's honorees at 
the 2018 Gala: Sam Rohrer, Sharon Meller, Menachem Meller, William 
Wanger, Cynthia Wanger, Sharon Resnick, and Jeff Resnick. These 
dedicated individuals are important advocates for one of the United 
States' most trusted allies, and their work remains vital to our 
nation's international strategy. Throughout their impressive careers, 
they have advanced and uplifted their communities, fostered the growth 
of young professionals, and empowered American and Israeli families. 
Such dedication to local and international service is certainly 
deserving of widespread recognition.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Zionist Organization 
of America's Greater Philadelphia Chapter as they celebrate their 2018 
Gala and honor these seven outstanding men and women.
```
