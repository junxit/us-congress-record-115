     Veterans Affairs community-based outpatient clinic in 
     Statesboro, Georgia, the Ray Hendrix Department of Veterans

     anniversary of the National Guard Youth Challenge Program.

     international financial institutions for the Government of 
     Nicaragua unless the Government of Nicaragua is taking
