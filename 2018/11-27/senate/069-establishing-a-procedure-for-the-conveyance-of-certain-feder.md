# ESTABLISHING A PROCEDURE FOR THE CONVEYANCE OF CERTAIN FEDERAL PROPERTY AROUND THE DICKINSON RESERVOIR IN THE STATE OF NORTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 440

```
ESTABLISHING A PROCEDURE FOR THE CONVEYANCE OF CERTAIN FEDERAL PROPERTY 
      AROUND THE DICKINSON RESERVOIR IN THE STATE OF NORTH DAKOTA

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask that the Chair lay before the 
Senate the message to accompany S. 440.
  The Presiding Officer laid before the Senate the following message 
from the House of Representatives:

       Resolved, That the bill from the Senate (S. 440) entitled 
     ``An Act to establish a procedure for the conveyance of 
     certain Federal property around the Dickinson Reservoir in 
     the State of North Dakota.'', do pass with an amendment.


                            Motion to Concur

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I move to concur in the House amendment 
and ask unanimous consent that the motion be agreed to and that the 
motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
