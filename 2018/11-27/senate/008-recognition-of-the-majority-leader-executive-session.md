# Recognition Of The Majority Leader (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   Recognition Of The Majority Leader

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that following 
the cloture vote on the Kelley nomination, the Senate recess until 
2:15; further, if cloture is invoked, all time during the recess counts 
postcloture on the nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
