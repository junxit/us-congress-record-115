# Text of Senate Amendment 4065

  SA 4065. Mr. McCONNELL (for Ms. Baldwin) proposed an amendment to the

Guard Youth Challenge Program; as follows:

     ``160,000'' and insert ``165,000''.

     ``110,000'' and insert ``116,000''.
