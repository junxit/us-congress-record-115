# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Hyde-Smith). The clerk will please read a 
communication to the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Hatch).
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                    Washington, DC, June 12, 2018.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Mitch McConnell, a Senator from the Commonwealth of Kentucky, 
     to perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                   Orrin G. Hatch,
     President pro tempore.

                          ____________________
```
