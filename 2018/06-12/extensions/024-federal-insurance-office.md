# FEDERAL INSURANCE OFFICE

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

```
                        FEDERAL INSURANCE OFFICE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRAD SHERMAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 12, 2018

  Mr. SHERMAN. Mr. Speaker, I will vote for the Federal Insurance 
Office Reform Act of 2017 because it prevents covered agreements from 
imposing new prudential requirements without a separate act of 
Congress. I am worried about the availability and accessibility of 
nationwide data about insurance and want assurances that this will be 
addressed before the bill reaches the House floor.
```
