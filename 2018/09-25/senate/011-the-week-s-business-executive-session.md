# The Week's Business (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                          The Week's Business

  Madam President, on an entirely different matter, as I noted 
yesterday, the Senate continues to make progress on critical national 
priorities. We are restoring the regular order appropriations process; 
we are securing commonsense reforms to infrastructure policy and the 
longest authorization of FAA in over 35 years; and, this week, we are 
confirming more of the President's well-qualified nominees. Yesterday, 
the Senate voted to confirm Jackie Wolcott as the U.S. Representative 
to the International Atomic Energy Agency and to the United Nations in 
Vienna. Today, we turn to consider the nomination of Peter A. Feldman 
to be a Commissioner of the Consumer Product Safety Commission.
  I urge each of our colleagues to join me in voting to confirm Mr. 
Feldman and in continuing to process nominees for vital roles in the 
executive branch.
```
