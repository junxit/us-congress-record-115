# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 1017

```
      PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO THE PRESIDENT

  Ms. ROS-LEHTINEN, from the Committee on Foreign Affairs, submitted an 
adverse privileged report (Rept. No. 115-978) on the resolution (H. 
Res. 1017) of inquiry requesting the President, and directing the 
Secretary of State, to transmit to the House of Representatives copies 
of all documents, records, communications, transcripts, summaries, 
notes, memoranda, and read-aheads in their possession referring or 
relating to certain communications between President Donald Trump and 
President Vladimir Putin, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
