# IN HONOR OF WILLIAM STRAHAN'S 100TH BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION

```
        IN HONOR OF WILLIAM STRAHAN'S 100TH BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LIZ CHENEY

                               of wyoming

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 20, 2018

  Ms. CHENEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my congratulations to 
William Strahan on the celebration of his 100th birthday.
  I join his friends and family in extending my best to him on this 
occasion and in celebrating his life and contributions to our great 
state and country. I hope he uses this momentous day to do the same.
  Again, Mr. Speaker, I would like to extend my congratulations to 
William Strahan on his birthday. May his year be filled with happiness 
and blessings.
```
