# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENT TO HOUSE AMENDMENT TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 1892, HONORING HOMETOWN HEROES ACT

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENT TO 
  HOUSE AMENDMENT TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 1892, HONORING HOMETOWN 
                               HEROES ACT

  Mr. SESSIONS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 115-551) on the resolution (H. Res. 734) providing 
for consideration of the Senate amendment to the House amendment to the 
Senate amendment to the bill (H.R. 1892) to amend title 4, United 
States Code, to provide for the flying of the flag at half-staff in the 
event of the death of a first responder in the line of duty, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
