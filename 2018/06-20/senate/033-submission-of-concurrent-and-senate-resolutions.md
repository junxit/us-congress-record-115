     2018 as ``National Post-Traumatic Stress Awareness Month'' 
     and June 27, 2018, as ``National Post-Traumatic Stress 
     Awareness Day''; considered and agreed to.
           By Ms. KLOBUCHAR (for herself and Mr. Flake):
       S. Res. 555. A resolution recognizing the freedom of 
     Muslims of the United States to exercise their religion and 
     participate in the civil systems of their country; considered 
     and agreed to.
