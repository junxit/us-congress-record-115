# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 548

**Speaking:** Kelly, Mike (K000376) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 548

```
 PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 548

  Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
I may hereafter be considered the primary sponsor of H.R. 548, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Dent of Pennsylvania, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Francis Rooney of Florida). Is there 
objection to the request of the gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
```
