# MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR THE VICTIMS OF SCHOOL SHOOTING IN SANTE FE, TEXAS

**Speaking:** Weber, Randy K. Sr. (W000814) [R-TX]

```
MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR THE VICTIMS OF SCHOOL SHOOTING IN SANTE FE, TEXAS

  (Mr. WEBER of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. WEBER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I would like to invite the entire 
Texas delegation to be up here with me, and if you are a Member from a 
district that has had a school shooting, would you please come stand 
with us.
  Mr. Speaker, on Friday morning, at 7:30, evil reared its ugly head in 
a little community, what I call a salt-of-the-Earth community, Santa 
Fe, Texas, and 10 people tragically lost their lives.
  I would ask all Members to please stand.
  We lost two teachers on that day: Cynthia Tisdale and Glenda Ann 
Perkins. We lost students Kimberly

[[Page H4268]]

Vaughan, Shana Fisher, Angelique Ramirez, Christian Riley Garcia, Jared 
Black, Sabika Sheikh, Christopher Jake Stone, and Aaron Kyle McLeod, 
lives that should have never been ended.
  Mr. Speaker, I would like to request a moment of silence.

                          ____________________
```
