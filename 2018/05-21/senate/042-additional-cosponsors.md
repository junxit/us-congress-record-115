                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 266

  At the request of Mr. Hatch, the name of the Senator from Ohio (Mr.

Congressional Gold Medal to Anwar Sadat in recognition of his heroic 
achievements and courageous contributions to peace in the Middle East.

children with complex medical conditions through enhanced pediatric 
health homes, and for other purposes.
