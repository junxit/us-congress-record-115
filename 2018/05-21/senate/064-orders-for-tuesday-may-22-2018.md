# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 22, 2018

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2372

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 22, 2018

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., Tuesday, 
May 22; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour 
be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and morning business be closed. I further ask that following leader 
remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session and resume 
consideration of the Baiocco nomination under the previous order. 
Finally, I ask that

[[Page S2800]]

the Senate recess following the cloture vote with respect to the House 
message to accompany S. 2372 until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly 
conference meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
