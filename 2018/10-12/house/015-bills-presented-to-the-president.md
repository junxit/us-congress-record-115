# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 302, H.R. 1551, H.R. 4921

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on October 4, 2018, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills:

       H.R. 302. To provide protections for certain sports 
     medicine professionals who provide certain medical services 
     in a secondary State.
       H.R. 4921. To require the Surface Transportation Board to 
     implement certain recommendations of the Inspector General of 
     the Department of Transportation.
       H.R. 1551. To modernize copyright law, and for other 
     purposes.

                          ____________________
```
