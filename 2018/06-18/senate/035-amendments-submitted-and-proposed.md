**Measures:** H.R. 5515, H.R. 5895

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 2909. Mr. FLAKE (for himself and Mr. McCain) submitted 
     an amendment intended to be proposed to amendment SA 2282 
     proposed by Mr. Inhofe (for himself and Mr. McCain) to the 
     bill H.R. 5515, to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 
     2019 for military activities of the Department of Defense,

     Department of Energy, to prescribe military personnel 
     strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes; which 
     was ordered to lie on the table.

     5895, making appropriations for energy and water development 
     and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
     2019, and for other purposes.
       SA 2911. Mr. ALEXANDER proposed an amendment to amendment 
     SA 2910 proposed by Mr. Shelby to the bill H.R. 5895, supra.
       SA 2912. Mr. MORAN submitted an amendment intended to be

     ordered to lie on the table.
       SA 2913. Mr. MORAN submitted an amendment intended to be
```
