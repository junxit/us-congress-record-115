# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 19, 2018

```
                          ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, 
                             JUNE 19, 2018

  Mr. LANKFORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., Tuesday, 
June 19; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed. I further ask that following 
leader remarks, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 5895. Finally, 
I ask that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. to allow 
for the weekly conference meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
