# SENATE RESOLUTION 467--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 467--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE 
      ELECTION OF A SERGEANT AT ARMS AND DOORKEEPER OF THE SENATE

  Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 467

       Notifying the House of Representatives of the election of a 
     Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.
       Resolved, That the House of Representatives be notified of 
     the election of the Honorable Michael C. Stenger as Sergeant 
     at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.
```
