# COMMUNICATION FROM CONSTITUENT SERVICES DIRECTOR, THE HONORABLE JACKIE SPEIER, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
COMMUNICATION FROM CONSTITUENT SERVICES DIRECTOR, THE HONORABLE JACKIE 
                       SPEIER, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Mark H. Nagales, Constituent Services Director, the 
Honorable Jackie Speier, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, April 11, 2018.
     Hon. Paul D. Ryan,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to notify you formally, pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives, 
     that I have been served with a subpoena for testimony as a 
     witness in a criminal trial issued by the Superior Court of 
     the State of California, County of San Mateo.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Mark H. Nagales,
                                    Constituent Services Director.

     

                          ____________________
```
