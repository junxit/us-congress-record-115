# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Pastor Eddie Bevill, Parkridge Church, Coral Springs, Florida, 
offered the following prayer:
  Heavenly Father, we come to You now in humility, acknowledging Your 
omniscience, omnipotence, and omnipresence. Please hear our prayer for 
this great Nation. The men and women in this room have been given, by 
some estimates, an impossible task, but nothing is impossible with You. 
I pray for their health, their families, their staffs, and their 
wisdom. Please give them Your grace and direction in all areas, so that 
they may be able to lead for the good of all people. Your word in Micah 
6:8 is a great reminder:
  ``He has told you, O man, what is good; and what does the Lord 
require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk 
humbly with your God?''
  May this scripture be the marching orders for those who serve in this 
important position. May their decisions promote unity and harmony in 
the U.S. and the world.
  In the name of Jesus Christ, I pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
