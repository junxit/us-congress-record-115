# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 11, 2018

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 5515

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 11, 2018

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
June 11; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed. I further ask that following 
leader remarks, the Senate resume consideration of the motion to 
proceed to H.R. 5515. Finally, I ask that notwithstanding the 
provisions of rule XXII, the vote on the motion to proceed to H.R. 5515 
occur at 5:30 p.m., Monday.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
