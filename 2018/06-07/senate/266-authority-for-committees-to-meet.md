# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Wicker, Roger F. (W000437) [R-MS]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. WICKER. Mr. President, I have 2 requests for committees to meet 
during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of the 
Majority and Minority leaders.
  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:


                       Committee on the Judiciary

  The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Thursday, June 7, 2018, at 10 a.m., to conduct 
a business meeting and hearing following nominations: nominations of 
Ryan Wesley Bounds, of Oregon, to be United States Circuit Judge for 
the Ninth Circuit, J. Campbell Barker, and Jeremy D. Kernodle, both to 
be a United States District Judge for the Eastern District of Texas, 
Susan Brnovich, to be United States District Judge for the District of 
Arizona, Chad F. Kenney, to be United States District Judge for the 
Eastern District of Pennsylvania, Maureen K. Ohlhausen, of Virginia, to 
be Judge of the United States Court of Federal Claims, Britt Cagle 
Grant, of Georgia, to be United States Circuit Judge for the Eleventh 
Circuit, Allen Cothrel Winsor, to be United States District Judge for 
the Northern District of Florida, and Patrick R. Wyrick, to be United 
States District Judge for the Western District of Oklahoma.


                    Select Committee on Intelligence

  The Select Committee on Intelligence is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Thursday, June 7, 2018, at 10 a.m. to conduct 
a closed hearing.

                          ____________________
```
