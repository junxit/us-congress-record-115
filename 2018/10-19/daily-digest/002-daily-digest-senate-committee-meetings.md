Committee on the Judiciary: On Wednesday, October 17, 2018, Committee 
concluded a hearing to examine the nominations of Allison Jones 
Rushing, of North Carolina, to be United States Circuit Judge for the 
Fourth Circuit, Thomas P. Barber, and Wendy Williams Berger, both to be 
a United States District Judge for the Middle District of Florida, 
Corey Landon Maze, to be United States District Judge for the Northern 
District of Alabama, Rodney Smith, to be United States District Judge 
for the Southern District of Florida, and T. Kent Wetherell II, to be 
United States District Judge for the Northern District of Florida, 
after the nominees testified and answered questions in their own 
behalf.
