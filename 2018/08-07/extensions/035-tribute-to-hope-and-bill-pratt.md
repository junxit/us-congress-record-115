# TRIBUTE TO HOPE AND BILL PRATT

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO HOPE AND BILL PRATT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 7, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Hope and Bill Pratt of New Market, Iowa on the very special occasion of 
their 70th wedding anniversary. They were married on June 27, 1948.
  Hope and Bill's lifelong commitment to each other and their family 
truly embodies Iowa values. As they reflect on their 70th anniversary, 
I hope it is filled with happy memories. May their commitment grow even 
stronger, as they continue to love, cherish, and honor one another for 
many years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend this great couple on their 70th year together 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
