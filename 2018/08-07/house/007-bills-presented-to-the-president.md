# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 2345, H.R. 5515, H.R. 5554, H.R. 6414

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on August 3, 2018, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills:

       H.R. 6414. To amend title 23, United States Code, to extend 
     the deadline for promulgation of regulations under the tribal 
     transportation self-governance program.
       H.R. 5554. To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic 
     Act to reauthorize user fee programs relating to new animal 
     drugs and generic new animal drugs.
       H.R. 2345. To require the Federal Communications Commission 
     to study the feasibility of designating a simple, easy-to-
     remember dialing code to be used for a national suicide 
     prevention and mental health crisis hotline system.
       H.R. 5515. To authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2019 
     for military activities of the Department of Defense, for 
     military construction, and for defense activities of the 
     Department of Energy, to prescribe military personnel 
     strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
