                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session for the consideration of Calendar Nos. 
1017 through 1029 and all nominations placed on the Secretary's desk in 
the Air Force, Army, and Navy; that the nominations be confirmed; that 
the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table 
with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in 
order; that any statements related to the nominations be printed in the 
Record; that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action, and the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
