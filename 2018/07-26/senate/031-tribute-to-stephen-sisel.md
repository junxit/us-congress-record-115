# TRIBUTE TO STEPHEN SISEL

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO STEPHEN SISEL

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Stephen Sisel, an 
intern in

[[Page S5420]]

my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work he has done on behalf 
of myself, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Currently, Stephen is attending the University of St. Andrews in St. 
Andrews, Fife, Scotland, where he studies modern history. He is a 
dedicated and diligent worker who has been devoted to getting the most 
out of his internship experience and who has been a true asset to the 
office.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Stephen for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●
```
