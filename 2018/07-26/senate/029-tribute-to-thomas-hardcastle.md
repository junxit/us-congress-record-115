# TRIBUTE TO THOMAS HARDCASTLE

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO THOMAS HARDCASTLE

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Thomas 
Hardcastle, an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard 
work he has done on behalf of myself, my staff, and the State of South 
Dakota.
  Thomas is a graduate of Northern State University in Aberdeen, SD. 
Currently, he is pursuing his masters of public administration at the 
University of Colorado, Denver. Thomas is a dedicated and diligent 
worker who has been devoted to getting the most out of his internship 
experience and who has been a true asset to the office.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Thomas for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
