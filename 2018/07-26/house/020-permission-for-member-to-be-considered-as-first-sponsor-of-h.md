# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 134

**Speaking:** Jeffries, Hakeem S. (J000294) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 134

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 134

  Mr. JEFFRIES. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 134, the Home 
Foreclosure Reduction Act, a bill originally introduced by 
Representative Conyers of Michigan, for the purposes of adding 
cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
