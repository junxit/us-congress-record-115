# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       125. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson, a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to urging Congress 
     to propose an amendment to the United States Constitution, 
     pursuant to Article V, that would reduce the length of a 
     regular term of a United States Senator from six years to 
     four years; which was referred to the Committee on the
```
