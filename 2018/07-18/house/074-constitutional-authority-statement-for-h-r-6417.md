# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6417

**Measures:** H.R. 6417

```
            By Mr. GOODLATTE:
        H.R. 6417.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to clause 4 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution of 
     the United States which gives Congress the authority to 
     establish a uniform Rule of Naturalization.
```
