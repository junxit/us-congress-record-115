# MEMBERS OF THE SENATE NATO OBSERVER GROUP

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
               MEMBERS OF THE SENATE NATO OBSERVER GROUP

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, in April, the Democratic leader and I 
announced the reestablishment of the Senate NATO Observer Group. 
Senators Tillis and Shaheen, both Members of the Senate Armed Services 
Committee, were named cochairs. We have asked for the following 
Senators to participate: Barrasso, Rubio, Gardner,  Ernst, Rounds, 
Merkley, Coons, King, Booker and Van Hollen.
```
