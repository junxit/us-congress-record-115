# VOTE EXPLANATION

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

● Ms. DUCKWORTH. Mr. President, I was necessarily absent for 
vote No. 92 on May 14 on the confirmation of Executive Calendar No. 
780, Michael Y. Scudder, of Illinois, to be United States circuit judge 
for the Seventh Circuit. On vote No. 92, had I been present, I would 
have voted yea on confirmation.
  I was also necessarily absent for vote No. 93 on the confirmation of 
Executive Calendar No. 781, Amy J. St. Eve, of Illinois, to be United 
States circuit judge for the Seventh Circuit. On vote No. 93, had I 
been present, I would have voted yea on confirmation.●
  (At the request of Mr. Durbin, the following statement was ordered to 
be printed in the Record.)
```
