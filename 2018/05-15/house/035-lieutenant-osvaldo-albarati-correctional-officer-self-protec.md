  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the vote on the 
motion to suspend the rules and pass the bill (H.R. 613) to amend title 
18, United States Code, to require that the Director of the Bureau of 
Prisons ensure that each chief executive officer of a Federal penal or 
correctional institution provides a secure storage area located outside 
of the secure perimeter of the Federal penal or correctional 
institution for firearms carried by certain employees of the Bureau of 
Prisons, and for other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were 
ordered.
