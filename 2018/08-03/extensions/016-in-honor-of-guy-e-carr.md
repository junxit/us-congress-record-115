# IN HONOR OF GUY E. CARR

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                        IN HONOR OF GUY E. CARR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 3, 2018

  Mr. HILL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Korean War Veteran Guy 
E. Carr.
  Mr. Carr served in the U.S. Air Force from December 27, 1950 until 
November 3, 1953.
  Mr. Carr was trained as a Senior Turret Systems Mechanic and Gunner, 
but upon arriving in Kunson, Korea he was retrained to be a flight 
systems engineer as they did not have a need for gunners.
  He nonchalantly likes to state ``we only crashed once''while in 
Korea.
  Mr. Carr flew 54 combat mission while serving in Korea, and earned 
the Distinguished Flying Cross, two Air Medals, the United Nations 
Korean Service Medal, and the Korean Service Medal.
  Mr. Carr truly exemplifies the Warrior Spirit that has seen our 
Nation through trying times.
  I'm honored to present Guy E. Carr replacement medals for his service 
in Korea and for his sacrifice to our nation.

                          ____________________
```
