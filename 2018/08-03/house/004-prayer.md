# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, only You can lead us into all truth. Only You 
can aid us in creating a just and compassionate society. Send forth 
Your spirit. Enlighten our minds to know what is right.
  May Your spirit also give us the strength and conviction to follow 
Your lead. And when we fail, as we so often do, forgive us, heal us, 
and help us again to stand and to follow Your way. We make this prayer 
in Thy holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
