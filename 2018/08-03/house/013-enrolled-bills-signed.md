# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 2345, H.R. 5515, H.R. 5554, H.R. 6414

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker pro tempore, Mr. Messer, on Friday, August 3, 2018.

       H.R. 2345. An act to require the Federal Communications 
     Commission to study the feasibility of designating a simple, 
     easy-to-remember dialing code to be used for a national 
     suicide prevention and mental health crisis hotline system.
       H.R. 5515. An act to authorize appropriations for fiscal 
     year 2019 for military activities of the Department of 
     Defense, for military construction, and for defense 
     activities of the Department of Energy, to prescribe military 
     personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 5554. An act to amend the Federal Food, Drug, and 
     Cosmetic Act to reauthorize user fee programs relating to new 
     animal drugs and generic new animal drugs.
       H.R. 6414. An act to amend title 23, United States Code, to 
     extend the deadline for promulgation of regulations under the 
     tribal transportation self-governance program.

                          ____________________
```
