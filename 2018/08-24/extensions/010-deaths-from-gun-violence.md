# DEATHS FROM GUN VIOLENCE

**Speaking:** Kelly, Robin L. (K000385) [D-IL]

```
                        DEATHS FROM GUN VIOLENCE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBIN L. KELLY

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 24, 2018

  Ms. KELLY of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise today because Americans 
are dying from gun violence and this Congress continues to do nothing.
  Gun violence in America has become a daily reality in our cities, in 
our suburbs and in rural communities, yet this Republican-controlled 
Congress does nothing.
  Instead, my colleagues on the other side of the aisle offer their 
`thoughts and prayers' on social media while collecting NRA checks with 
zero after zero on them.
  Let me be clear, Mr. Speaker, money has poisoned our politics with 
deadly results. No other peer nation sees the level of gun violence 
that occurs in America--perhaps because our peer nations don't have a 
gun lobby dedicated to a dangerous and misguided `gun everywhere, 
commonsense nowhere' agenda that kills Americans on a daily basis.
  Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the names of 244 Americans, from 
just three Congressional districts: Pennsylvania's 17th Congressional 
District, North Carolina's 13th Congressional District and North 
Dakota's At-Large Congressional District, who've been lost to gun 
violence in recent years.
```
