# CELEBRATING CLAUDE AND MARY RENSHAW ON THEIR 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Walorski, Jackie (W000813) [R-IN]

```
 CELEBRATING CLAUDE AND MARY RENSHAW ON THEIR 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JACKIE WALORSKI

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 24, 2018

  Mrs. WALORSKI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Claude and Mary 
Renshaw of South Bend on their 50th wedding anniversary.
  After marrying in 1968 and moving to northern Indiana in 1977, they 
built a beautiful life for their family full of love, laughter, and 
strong Hoosier values. Fifty years of marriage is a marvelous 
accomplishment that speaks volumes to the compassion and the tremendous 
foundation of respect and gratitude they share with one another.
  Their lasting commitment not only to each other but also to their 
daughters and granddaughter is a shining example of devotion and

[[Page E1175]]

faithfulness in our Hoosier community. Having dedicated themselves to 
educational and cultural enrichment, they have touched the lives of 
countless people in our community. I am grateful for all they have done 
over the years to make South Bend a better place to live.
  On behalf of 2nd District Hoosiers, it is my honor to celebrate 
Claude and Mary on their golden anniversary and to help them ring in 
this milestone. I would like to extend my sincerest congratulations to 
Claude, Mary, and their entire family. It is my hope that their love 
continues to grow stronger with each passing year.

                          ____________________
```
