# TRIBUTE TO MARY ANN AND ROBERT WEGE

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                  TRIBUTE TO MARY ANN AND ROBERT WEGE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 24, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Mary Ann and

[[Page E1174]]

Robert Wege of Council Bluffs, Iowa on the very special occasion of 
their 70th wedding anniversary. They were married on July 25, 1948 at 
Christ Lutheran Church in Norfolk, Nebraska.
  Mary Ann and Robert's lifelong commitment to each other and their 
family truly embodies Iowa values. As they reflect on their 70th 
anniversary, I hope it is filled with happy memories. May their 
commitment grow even stronger, as they continue to love, cherish, and 
honor one another for many years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend this great couple on their 70th year together 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
