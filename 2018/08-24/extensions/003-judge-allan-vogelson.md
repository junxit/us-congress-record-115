# JUDGE ALLAN VOGELSON

**Speaking:** Norcross, Donald (N000188) [D-NJ]

```
                          JUDGE ALLAN VOGELSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DONALD NORCROSS

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 24, 2018

  Mr. NORCROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of the late Honorable Judge M. Allan Vogelson of Cherry Hill, Camden 
County, located in the First Congressional District of the Great State 
of New Jersey, for his decades of public service.
  The Honorable Judge Vogelson served as the Camden County Presiding 
Judge for the Camden County Superior Court, for more than twelve years. 
For eight years, he served as Presiding Judge, for the Camden County 
Superior Court, Civil Division and subsequently appointed to preside 
over the Camden County Superior Court, Chancery Division.
  Prior to serving as Presiding Judge for the Camden County Superior 
Court, Judge Vogelson served as the Assistant Prosecutor for Camden 
County and in 1971 elected as a freeholder to the Camden County Board 
of Chosen Freeholders, with tenure as the Freeholder Director.
  In the community he both loved and called home, Judge Vogelson served 
with distinction: on the New Jersey State Legislature Reappointment 
Committee, as a member of the Camden County College Board of Trustees, 
and on the Board of Trustees of the Boys & Girls Club of Camden County, 
New Jersey.
  Judge Vogelson was a husband, father, grandfather, great-grandfather, 
forerunner, role model and exceptional public servant who was a 
tireless advocate for others, and is deeply missed. Mr. Speaker, I ask 
you to join with me in mourning the loss and honoring the legacy of the 
late Honorable Judge M. Allan Vogelson

                          ____________________
```
