# AUTHORIZING THE USE OF OFFICIAL OFFICE FUNDS TO PURCHASE THE POCKET VERSION OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

**Measures:** S.Res. 712

```
  AUTHORIZING THE USE OF OFFICIAL OFFICE FUNDS TO PURCHASE THE POCKET 
            VERSION OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to consideration of S. Res. 712, submitted earlier today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 712) authorizing the use of official 
     office funds to purchase the pocket version of the 
     Constitution of the United States.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
resolution be agreed to, the preamble be agreed to, and that the 
motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with 
no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 712) was agreed to.

[[Page S7248]]

  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
