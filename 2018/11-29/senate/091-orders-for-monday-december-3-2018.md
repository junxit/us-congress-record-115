# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 3, 2018

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 3, 2018

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 o'clock p.m., 
Monday, December 3; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and morning business be closed; further, that the Senate 
proceed to executive session and resume consideration of Executive 
Calendar No. 1153 and that notwithstanding rule XXII, the cloture vote 
on that nomination occur at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
