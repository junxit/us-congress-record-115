  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were

     Transportation.
           By Mr. BLUNT (for himself and Ms. Klobuchar):
       S. Con. Res. 54. A concurrent resolution authorizing the 
     printing of the 26th edition of the pocket version of the 
     Constitution of the United States; considered and agreed to.
