# AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

```
       AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the majority 
leader and the senior Senator from Alaska be authorized to sign duly 
enrolled bills or joint resolutions on Friday, November 30, 2018.
```
