# AUTHORIZING THE PRINTING OF THE 26TH EDITION OF THE POCKET VERSION OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 54

```
 AUTHORIZING THE PRINTING OF THE 26TH EDITION OF THE POCKET VERSION OF 
                 THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to immediate consideration of S. Con. Res. 54.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 54) authorizing the 
     printing of the 26th edition of the pocket version of the 
     Constitution of the United States.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
concurrent resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or 
debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 54) was agreed to.
  (The resolution, is printed in today's Record under ``Submitted 
Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
