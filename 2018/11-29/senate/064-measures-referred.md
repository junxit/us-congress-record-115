# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 6032, H.R. 6753

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 6032. An act to direct the Secretary of Commerce to 
     conduct a study and submit to Congress a report on the state 
     of the internet-connected devices industry in the United 
     States; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
       H.R. 6753. An act to amend title XI of the Social Security 
     Act to direct the Secretary of Health and Human Services to 
     establish a public-private partnership for purposes of 
     identifying health care waste, fraud, and abuse; to the 
     Committee on Finance.
```
