# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 440, S. 1768, S. 2074, S. 3389

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, November 29, 
2018, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bills:

       S. 440. An act to establish a procedure for the conveyance 
     of certain Federal property around the Dickinson Reservoir in 
     the State of North Dakota.
       S. 1768. An act to reauthorize and amend the National 
     Earthquake Hazards Reduction Program, and for purposes.
       S. 2074. An act to establish a procedure for the conveyance 
     of certain Federal property around the Jamestown Reservoir in 
     the State of North Dakota, and for other purposes.
       S. 3389. An act to redesignate a facility of the National 
     Aeronautics and Space Administration.

                          ____________________
```
