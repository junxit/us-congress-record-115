# SENATE RESOLUTION 712--AUTHORIZING THE USE OF OFFICIAL OFFICE FUNDS TO PURCHASE THE POCKET VERSION OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES

**Measures:** S.Res. 294, S.Res. 712

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 712--AUTHORIZING THE USE OF OFFICIAL OFFICE FUNDS TO 
  PURCHASE THE POCKET VERSION OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. BLUNT (for himself and Ms. Klobuchar) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 712

       Resolved,

     SECTION 1. POCKET VERSION OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED 
                   STATES.

       Section 2(3) of Senate Resolution 294 (96th Congress), 
     agreed to April 29, 1980, is amended--
       (1) by striking ``and copies'' and inserting ``copies''; 
     and
       (2) by inserting ``, and copies of the pocket version of 
     the Constitution of the United States published by the 
     Government Publishing Office'' after ``Historical Society''.

                          ____________________
```
