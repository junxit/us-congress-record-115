# HONORING CAMERON SILVEUS

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                        HONORING CAMERON SILVEUS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 29, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Cameron Silveus for his 
contribution to our state.
  During my entire tenure in Congress, Cameron has been a loyal friend 
and wise adviser. Cameron is also a leader in Indiana's agriculture 
industry, serving as an executive at Silveus Insurance Group. Our state 
is better off today because of his extraordinary leadership and 
service.
  On a personal note, Cameron is a brother in Christ. I have 
appreciated his prayers and encouragement over the years. I want to 
thank him for his friendship and support of my work in Congress. I wish 
him continued success in all that God has planned for his family.

                          ____________________
```
