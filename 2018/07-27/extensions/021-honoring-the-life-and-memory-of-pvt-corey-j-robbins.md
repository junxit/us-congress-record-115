# HONORING THE LIFE AND MEMORY OF PVT. COREY J. ROBBINS

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
         HONORING THE LIFE AND MEMORY OF PVT. COREY J. ROBBINS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 27, 2018

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, it is with a heavy heart that I share 
the life and memory of an individual from Bucks County, Pennsylvania, 
who passed away on July 15, 2018, at the age of 23.
  Corey Robbins, of Warrington, was a 2013 graduate of Central Bucks 
High School. An American patriot with a strong desire to serve his 
country. Corey joined the United States Army Reserves. He also worked 
as a commercial truck driver.
  Corey was an avid outdoorsman who enjoyed hunting, fishing, 
snowboarding, hiking, and boating. A dedicated community servant, Corey 
attained the rank of Eagle Scout from Troop 133 in Chalfont.
  I send my deepest condolences to Corey's parents, Judithann Liebig 
Robbins and David Robbins, along with his brother Tyler Davis Robbins. 
May they take comfort in knowing Corey's life was one that was 
inspirational and well-lived.

                          ____________________
```
