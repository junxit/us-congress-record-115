# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious God, we praise You today for Your love that sustains our 
earthly journey. When evil flourishes, we keep our eyes on You, 
trusting always in the unfolding of Your powerful providence.
  Today, inspire our lawmakers to discover the fulfillment of resting 
in the assurance of Your amazing grace. As our Senators remember how 
You have provided for this Nation in the past, may they develop a 
stronger faith in the protection You will give us in the future. Guide 
them into the future surrounded by the shield of Your favor. Lord, keep 
them close to You and to each other as they fulfill their sacred 
calling as legislators.
  We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
