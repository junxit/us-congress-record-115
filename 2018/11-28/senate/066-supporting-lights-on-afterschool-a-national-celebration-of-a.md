**Measures:** S.Res. 710

```
SUPPORTING LIGHTS ON AFTERSCHOOL, A NATIONAL CELEBRATION OF AFTERSCHOOL 
                   PROGRAMS HELD ON OCTOBER 25, 2018

  Mr. PORTMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of S. Res. 710, submitted earlier today.

       A resolution (S. Res. 710) supporting Lights On

  The resolution (S. Res. 710) was agreed to.
```
