# TRIBUTE TO MATTHEW REEVES

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO MATTHEW REEVES

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Matthew Reeves, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work he has 
done on behalf of myself, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Matthew is a graduate of the University of Arkansas in Fayetteville, 
AR. Later this year, he will complete his master of science degree in 
defense and strategic studies at Missouri State University. Matthew is 
a dedicated and diligent worker who has been devoted to getting the 
most out of his internship experience and who has been a true asset to 
the office.

[[Page S7184]]

  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Matthew for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
