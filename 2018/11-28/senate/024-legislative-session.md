# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

**Speaking:** Portman, Rob (P000449) [R-OH]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 54

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

               UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S.J. RES. 54

  Mr. PORTMAN. Mr. President, as if in legislative session, I ask 
unanimous consent that notwithstanding rule XXII, the Senate proceed to 
legislative session, and Senator Sanders, or his designee, be 
recognized to make a motion to discharge S.J. Res. 54; further, that 
there be time for debate of the motion until 4 p.m. and of that time, 
10 minutes be under control of the chairman and 10 minutes for the 
ranking member, and the remaining time be equally divided between the 
two leaders or their designees; that at 4 p.m., the Senate vote in 
relation to the motion to discharge; that following disposition of the 
motion, the Senate resume executive session and the time spent in 
legislative session count postcloture on the Farr nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Vermont.

                          ____________________
```
