# TRIBUTE TO SKYLER MANSELL

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO SKYLER MANSELL

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Skyler Mansell, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work he has 
done on behalf of myself, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Skyler is a graduate of the University of North Alabama in Florence, 
AL. Currently, he is pursuing his master of business administration 
with a concentration in finance at the University of North Alabama. 
Skyler is a dedicated and diligent worker who has been devoted to 
getting the most out of his internship and who has been a true asset to 
the office.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Skyler for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
