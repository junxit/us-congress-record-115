# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, October 12, 2018.
     Hon. Paul D. Ryan,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.

     p.m.:
       That the Senate agrees to the House amendment to the bill 
     S. 1595.
       That the Senate passed S. 2465.
       That the Senate passed S. 2765.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 1037.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 3834.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 6758.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 6870.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 6896.
       That the Senate passed with amendments H.R. 390.
       That the Senate passed with an amendment H.R. 2422.
       That the Senate passed with amendment H.R. 2615.
       That the Senate passed with amendments H.R. 3342.
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
     Karen L. Haas.
```
