# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 11, 2018

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 11, 2018

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Wednesday, July 11; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and morning business be closed. I ask that following leader 
remarks, the Senate resume consideration of the Benczkowski nomination 
and that all time during adjournment, leader remarks, and morning 
business count postcloture on the nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
