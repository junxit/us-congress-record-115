# HOUSING CHOICE VOUCHER MOBILITY DEMONSTRATION ACT OF 2018

**Measures:** H.R. 5793

```
       HOUSING CHOICE VOUCHER MOBILITY DEMONSTRATION ACT OF 2018

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the vote on the 
motion to suspend the rules and pass the bill (H.R. 5793) to authorize 
the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to carry out a housing 
choice voucher mobility demonstration to encourage families receiving 
such voucher assistance to move to lower-poverty areas and expand 
access to opportunity areas, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Michigan (Mr. Huizenga) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 368, 
nays 19, not voting 40, as follows:
```
