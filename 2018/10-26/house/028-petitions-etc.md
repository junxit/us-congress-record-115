# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       123. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Gregory D. Watson, 
     a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to requesting that Congress 
     enact legislation that would uniformly mandate the immediate 
     termination of the national security clearance of any person 
     who has fully and completely ceased to occupy a position 
     within the Federal Government -- or within a corporation 
     conducting business with the Federal Government or within a 
     State or lower level of American government, or within the 
     government of a foreign nation -- that requires the holding 
     of such a security clearance; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.
```
