# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6954

**Measures:** H.R. 6954

```
           By Mr. NEAL:
       H.R. 6954.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is in 
     the power of Congress to make all laws necessary and proper 
     for carrying into Execution of foregoing Powers as enumerated 
     in Article I, Section 8.
```
