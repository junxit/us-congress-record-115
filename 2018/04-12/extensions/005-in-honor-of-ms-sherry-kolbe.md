# IN HONOR OF MS. SHERRY KOLBE

**Speaking:** Blum, Rod (B001294) [R-IA]

```
                      IN HONOR OF MS. SHERRY KOLBE

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ROD BLUM

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 12, 2018

  Mr. BLUM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a remarkable woman and 
native Iowan from Marshalltown, Iowa. Ms. Sherry Kolbe has dedicated 
her career to the National Association of Private Special Education 
Centers. NAPSEC advocates for the needs of children with severe 
disabilities. Having served as the Executive Director for 28 years, Ms. 
Kolbe has impacted the lives of hundreds of children.
   We congratulate Ms. Kolbe as she prepares for retirement. After 
years of hard work, she deserves the utmost respect for her 
distinguished career and should be assured that new leadership at 
NAPSEC will benefit from her outstanding example.
```
