# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 113, H.Con.Res. 115, H.R. 3288, H.R. 4177, H.R. 4311, H.R. 4423, H.R. 4631, H.R. 4673, H.R. 5294, H.R. 5305, H.R. 5319, H.R. 5326

Committee Meetings
APPROPRIATIONS--SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Interior, Environment, and 
Related Agencies held a budget hearing on the Smithsonian Institution. 
Testimony was heard from David Skorton, Secretary, Smithsonian 
Institution.

Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human 
Services, Education, and Related Agencies held a budget hearing

Testimony was heard from Tom Morris, Associate Administrator for Rural 
Health Policy, Health Resources and Services Administration; and Luis 
Padilla, M.D., Associate Administrator for Health Workforce, Health 
Resources and Services Administration.

Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Commerce, Justice,

Administration.

Lieutenant General Charles D. Luckey, Chief of Army Reserve; Vice 
Admiral Luke M. McCollum, Chief of Navy Reserve; Lieutenant General Rex 
C. McMillian, Commander, Marine Forces Reserve; and Lieutenant General 
Maryanne Miller, Chief of Air Force Reserve.
