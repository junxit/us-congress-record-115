     on the state of the Union.

       Mr. BRADY (TX): Committee on Ways and Means. H.R. 6199. A

     to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the 
     Union.
       Mr. BRADY (TX): Committee on Ways and Means. H.R. 6309. A

     amendment (Rept. 115-851). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. BRADY (TX): Committee on Ways and Means. H.R. 6317. A
