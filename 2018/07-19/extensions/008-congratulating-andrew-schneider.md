# CONGRATULATING ANDREW SCHNEIDER

**Speaking:** Blum, Rod (B001294) [R-IA]

```
                    CONGRATULATING ANDREW SCHNEIDER

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ROD BLUM

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 19, 2018

  Mr. BLUM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Andrew Schneider 
who attained the Eagle Scout Award. Becoming an Eagle Scout, the 
highest rank in the Boy Scouts, has only been accomplished by 
approximately four percent of all Boy Scouts. The process requires 
earning at least 21 merit badges which takes years to fulfill.
  Receiving his Eagle Scout rank demonstrates that he is a strong, 
dedicated, and proven leader. With this accomplishment, it is my hope 
he will continue to serve as a role model for younger boy scouts. Thank 
you to Mr. Schneider for making a positive impact in northeast Iowa.

                          ____________________
```
