# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 5515

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:02 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 5515. An act to authorize appropriations for fiscal 
     year 2019 for military activities of the Department of 
     Defense, for military construction, and for defense 
     activities of the Department of Energy, to prescribe military 
     personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for other 
     purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to section 50802 of the 
Bipartisan Budget Act of 2018 (Public Law 115-123), the Minority Leader 
appoints the following individual to serve as a Commissioner to the 
Commission on Social Impact Partnerships: Ms. Anne Wilson of San 
Rafael, California.

                          ____________________
```
