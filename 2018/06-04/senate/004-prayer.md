# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we thank You that although we plan our course, You 
determine the success of our journey. We praise You for the many 
amazing gifts You provide for our pilgrimage.
  Lord, inspire our lawmakers to commit their plans to You, trusting 
the unfolding of Your loving providence. May they not depend only on 
persistent self-reliance, but draw upon the supernatural strength and 
wisdom You desire to give them. Today, fill this Chamber with Your 
presence and power.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
