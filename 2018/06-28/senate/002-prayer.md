# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Sovereign God, thank You that Your mercies endure forever. Show us 
Your ways and teach us Your paths as You lead us with Your truth.
  Today, set the hearts of our lawmakers on Heaven's way. In all of 
their actions, may they seek Your celestial approval. Remind them that 
You are the only constituent they absolutely must please. May our 
Senators stand on Your promises and lean on Your grace.
  Lord, thank You for Your mercy. You lift the lowly, satisfy the 
thirsty, and fill the hungry with good things.
  And, Lord, thank You for the faithfulness of our summer pages.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
