# REINSTATE AND EXTEND THE DEADLINE FOR COMMENCEMENT OF CONSTRUCTION OF A HYDROELECTRIC PROJECT INVOLVING JENNINGS RANDOLPH DAM

**Measures:** H.R. 2122

```
REINSTATE AND EXTEND THE DEADLINE FOR COMMENCEMENT OF CONSTRUCTION OF A 
         HYDROELECTRIC PROJECT INVOLVING JENNINGS RANDOLPH DAM

  The bill (H.R. 2122) to reinstate and extend the deadline for 
commencement of construction of a hydroelectric project involving 
Jennings Randolph Dam, was considered, was ordered to a third reading, 
was read the third time, and passed.
```
