# BLOOMINGDALE VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 4574

```
          BLOOMINGDALE VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4574) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 108 West Schick Road in Bloomingdale, 
Illinois, as the ``Bloomingdale Veterans Memorial Post Office 
Building,'' was ordered to a third reading, was read the third time,
```
