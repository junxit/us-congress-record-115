# TRIBUTE TO LOGAN AUKES

```
                         TRIBUTE TO LOGAN AUKES

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Logan Aukes, an 
intern in my Sioux Falls, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Logan is a graduate of Sioux Falls Christian High School in Sioux 
Falls, SD. Currently, he is attending Dordt College, where he is 
majoring in business administration. He is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Logan for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
