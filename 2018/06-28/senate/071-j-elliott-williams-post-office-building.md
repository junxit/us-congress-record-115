# J. ELLIOTT WILLIAMS POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                J. ELLIOTT WILLIAMS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4301) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 201 Tom Hall Street in Fort Mill, South 
Carolina, as the ``J. Elliott Williams Post Office Building,'' was 
ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
