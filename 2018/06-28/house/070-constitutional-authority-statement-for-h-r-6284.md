# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6284

**Measures:** H.R. 6284

```
           By Mrs. LAWRENCE:
       H.R. 6284.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article I, Section 8, Clause 18, Congress shall have 
     Power to make all Laws which shall be necessary and proper 
     for carrying into Execution theforegoing Powers, and all 
     other Powers vested by this Constitution in the Government of 
     hte United States, or in any Department or Officer thereof.
```
