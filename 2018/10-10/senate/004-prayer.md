# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Lord God, You are our hiding place. You alone are our mighty 
rock and fortress. Thank You for directing our steps.
  Today, provide our lawmakers with Your peace, infusing them with the 
wisdom that strives for faithfulness. Lord, let that faithfulness so 
energize them that harmony will triumph over discord. May the effects 
of this unity be felt in our Nation and world.
  Teach us all to serve You with all our hearts, souls, minds, and 
strength.
  We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
