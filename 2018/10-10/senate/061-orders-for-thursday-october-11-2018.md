# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 11, 2018

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 11, 2018

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, October 11; further, that following the prayer and pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and morning business be closed; finally, 
following leader remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session and 
resume consideration of the Clark nomination as under the previous 
order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
