# SUMMER INTERN JANSON REQUIST

```
                             SUMMER INTERN 
                             JANSON REQUIST

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. KEN BUCK

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 24, 2018

  Mr. BUCK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Janson Requist for 
his hard work and dedication to the people of Colorado's Fourth 
District as an intern in my Washington, D.C. office for the summer of 
2018.

[[Page 13515]]

  The work of this young man has been exemplary, and I know he has a 
bright future. He served as a tour guide, interacted with constituents, 
and learned a great deal about our nation's legislative process. I was 
glad to be able to offer this educational opportunity, and look forward 
to seeing him build his career in public service.
  Janson plans to continue pursuing his degree at Colorado Christian 
University and working as a public servant after this internship. I 
wish him the best as he pursues his career path. Mr. Speaker, it is an 
honor to recognize Janson Requist for his service the last several 
months to the people of Colorado's 4th District.

                          ____________________
```
