**Speaking:** Massie, Thomas (M001184) [R-KY]

                     STIGLER ACT AMENDMENTS OF 2018

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and concurring in the Senate amendments to the 
bill (H.R. 2606) to amend the Act of August 4, 1947 (commonly known as 
the Stigler Act), with respect to restrictions applicable to Indians of 
the Five Civilized Tribes of Oklahoma, and for other purposes.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from California (Mr. McClintock) that the House suspend the 
rules and concur in the Senate amendments.
  The question was taken.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. In the opinion of the Chair, two-thirds 
being in the affirmative, the ayes have it.
