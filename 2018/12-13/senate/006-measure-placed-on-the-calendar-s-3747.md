# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3747

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 3747

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3747

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk due a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the title of 
the bill for the second time.
  The legislative clerk read the title of the bill as follows:

       A bill (S. 3747) to provide for programs to help reduce the 
     risk that prisoners will recidivate upon release from prison, 
     and for other purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. In order to place the bill on the calendar, under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I object to further proceedings.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The objection having been heard, 
the bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
