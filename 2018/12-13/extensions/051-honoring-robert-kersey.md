# HONORING ROBERT KERSEY

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                         HONORING ROBERT KERSEY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 13, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Robert Kersey for his 
contribution to our state.
  Bob is the President of American Lawn Mower Company, which has been 
dedicated to creating jobs in Indiana for years. His company is the 
only domestic manufacturer of reel mowers in the United States and 
commands a considerable share of the market. With a manufacturing 
facility in Shelbyville and an office in Muncie, American Lawn Mower 
Company is critical to the economy of the 6th Congressional District.
  I have had the pleasure of knowing Bob for nearly two decades. He is 
a loyal friend, trusted advisor and longtime supporter of my work in 
Congress. Most importantly, Bob is a man of faith who I greatly admire. 
I want to thank Bob for his friendship and loyalty to me over all these 
years. I wish him continued success in all that God has planned for his 
family.

                          ____________________
```
