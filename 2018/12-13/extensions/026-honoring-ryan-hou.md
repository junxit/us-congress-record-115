# HONORING RYAN HOU

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                           HONORING RYAN HOU

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 13, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Ryan Hou for his 
contribution to the City of Columbus and our state.
  Ryan is a hard worker and has had an incredible career as a job 
creator in Indiana. He started LHP Engineering Solutions in 2001, which 
provides engineering and technology integration services for major U.S. 
corporations. The company has offices all over the world and employs 
more than 100 Hoosiers in Columbus. Ryan is also very engaged in the 
community and has volunteered his time and resources for countless 
community efforts.
  On a personal note, Ryan is a loyal friend, trusted advisor and 
longtime supporter of my work in Congress. I will always appreciate 
Ryan's positive spirit and wise counsel. If you are having a tough 
time, you can count on Ryan to remind you of the extraordinary 
opportunity each day brings.
  I want to thank Ryan for his friendship and loyalty to me over all 
these years. I wish him continued success in all that God has planned 
for his family.

                          ____________________
```
