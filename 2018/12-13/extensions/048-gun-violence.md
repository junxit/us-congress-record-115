# GUN VIOLENCE

**Speaking:** Kelly, Robin L. (K000385) [D-IL]

```
                              GUN VIOLENCE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBIN L. KELLY

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 13, 2018

  Ms. KELLY of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise today because 2017 marked 
the greatest number of gun violence deaths in two decades.
  Last year, we lost 39,773 Americans to gun violence--that's more than 
109 people killed each and every day.
  Despite these shocking and horrific statistics, despite the fact that 
more Americans are dying from gun violence, despite the fact that 
mothers are burying their children far too soon, this House--this 
Republican majority--has done nothing to stop the violence.
  Mr. Speaker, you and your team's response to 39,773 dead Americans 
was to do nothing, zero, zilch?
  Why? What is the root of your inaction?
  Could it be the large numbers in those NRA checks that flood your 
campaign account and leadership PAC?
  Could it be the more than 30 million dollars that the NRA spent to 
elect President Donald J. Trump?
  Mr. Speaker, I have one question: why do those millions of dollars 
matter more than 39,773 American lives lost in one year?
  Why does any amount of money matter more to you and your caucus than 
the life of an American?
  Mr. Speaker, these are the names of more Americans that this Congress 
has failed--Americans killed or hurt by gun violence that has become an 
epidemic in our nation because of Republican inaction.
```
