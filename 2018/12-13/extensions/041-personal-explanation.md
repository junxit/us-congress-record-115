# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 88, H.Res. 1160, H.R. 6901, H.R. 7187

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DANNY K. DAVIS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 13, 2018

  Mr. DANNY K. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I was unable to cast 
votes on the following legislative measures. If I were present for roll 
call votes, I would have voted ``No'' for the following vote:
  Roll No. 423, November 29, 2018: On Agreeing to the Resolution, H. 
Res. 1160, Providing for consideration of the Senate amendment to the 
bill (H.R. 88) to modify the boundary of the Shiloh National Military 
Park located in Tennessee and Mississippi, to establish Parker's 
Crossroads Battlefield as an affiliated area of the National Park 
System, and for other purposes.
  If I were present for roll call votes, I would have voted ``Aye'' for 
the following votes:
  Roll No. 424, November 29, 2018: On Motion to Suspend the Rules and 
Pass H.R. 7187, the National Flood Insurance Program Further Extension 
Act; and
  Roll No. 425, November 30, 2018: On Motion to Suspend the Rules and 
Pass, as Amended, H.R. 6901, the Federal CIO Authorization Act of 2018.
```
