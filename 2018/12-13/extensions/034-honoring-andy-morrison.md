# HONORING ANDY MORRISON

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                         HONORING ANDY MORRISON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 13, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Andy Morrison for his 
contribution to our state.
  After a distinguished career as a high-ranking executive at 
Wellpoint, Andy now serves as the CEO of Fulcrum Sports, which produces 
high quality performance nutrition solutions for athletes. Outside of 
work, Andy has been dedicated to improving the community in which he 
lives. It has been an honor to work with him in advancing our shared 
Republican principles.
  I have had the pleasure of knowing Andy for nearly a decade. He has 
been a good friend, wise advisor and loyal supporter during my entire 
tenure in Congress.
  I want to thank Andy for his friendship and loyalty to me over all 
these years. I wish him continued success in all that God has planned 
for his family.

                          ____________________
```
