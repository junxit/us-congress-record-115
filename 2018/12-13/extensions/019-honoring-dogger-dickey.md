# HONORING DOGGER DICKEY

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                         HONORING DOGGER DICKEY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 13, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Dogger Dickey for his 
contribution to our state and our District.
  Dogger works for Hillenbrand Capital Partners and has been making the 
trains run on time for years. He has always been involved in Indiana 
politics and has continuously volunteered his time and resources toward 
improving the quality of life for his fellow Hoosiers. It has been an 
honor to work with him.
  On a personal note, Dogger was among my earliest and most vocal 
supporters. He is a loyal confidant and wise adviser. I want to thank 
him for his friendship and support of my work in Congress. I wish 
Dogger continued success in all that God has planned for him and his 
family.

                          ____________________
```
