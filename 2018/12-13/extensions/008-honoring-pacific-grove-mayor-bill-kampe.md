# HONORING PACIFIC GROVE MAYOR BILL KAMPE

**Speaking:** Panetta, Jimmy (P000613) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING PACIFIC GROVE MAYOR BILL KAMPE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JIMMY PANETTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 13, 2018

  Mr. PANETTA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the retiring Mayor of 
Pacific Grove, Bill Kampe for his career in public service and his 
commitment to the people of the central coast of California. His 
professional accomplishments and leadership in the community have 
affected the lives of many.
   Throughout his ten years in public office, Mayor Kampe served the 
City of Pacific Grove as a dynamic and effective leader. Mayor Kampe 
endeavored in his three terms as Mayor and two terms as a Councilmember 
to improve the city's infrastructure, allowing visitors and residents 
to continue enjoying the breathtaking vistas and charming storefronts. 
Mayor Kampe has been a strong advocate for ocean protection, including 
procuring the Lovers Point Watershed project to help preserve our 
National Marine Sanctuary. Mayor Kampe also updated the city's Historic 
Preservation Ordinance to preserve city resources and maintain the 
city's historic properties.
   In addition to his distinguished service as Mayor and Councilmember, 
Mayor Kampe holds several regional leadership roles that greatly 
benefit our community on the Central Coast. As President of the 
Monterey Peninsula Regional Water Authority, he has worked to help 
remediate water shortages on the Monterey Peninsula. Additionally, 
Mayor Kampe has dedicated his time to representing Pacific Grove on the 
Fort Ord Reuse Authority, an entity overseeing the development of the 
former military base Fort Ord. As a Navy veteran, Mayor Kampe has 
ensured that local resources remain accessible for local veterans and 
active-duty members of the military.
   Throughout his career, Mayor Kampe prioritized the importance of 
giving back to our community's youth by hosting mock City Council 
meetings. Through this program, students are inspired to engage with 
current issues in Pacific Grove and learn the value of civic 
engagement. This spirit of service is evident in Mayor Kampe's service 
as a member of the Pacific Grove Beautification Committee and the 
Pacific Grove Rotary Club, and his volunteer contributions at countless 
town functions and events.
   I thank Mayor Kampe for his service to his country, community, and 
the City of Pacific Grove. As he celebrates his retirement, I am proud 
to commend him on his invaluable work as a public servant for the past 
decade. Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues join me in recognizing 
former Mayor of Pacific Grove, Bill Kampe, on his career of service and 
congratulate him on a well-earned retirement.
```
