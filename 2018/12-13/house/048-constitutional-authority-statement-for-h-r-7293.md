# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7293

**Measures:** H.R. 7293

```
            By Mr. MORELLE:
        H.R. 7293.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Post Offices (e.g., naming post offices, creating honorary 
     stamps)--Article I, Section 8, clause 7 provides Congress 
     with the power to establish post offices and post roads.
```
