# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7306

**Measures:** H.R. 7306

```
           By Mr. KHANNA:
       H.R. 7306.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution, under the General 
     Welfare Clause; and Article I, Section 4 of the Constitution, 
     which give Congress power to make laws governing the time, 
     place, and manner of Federal elections.
```
