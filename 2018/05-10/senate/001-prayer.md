# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  God of hope and love, through all the length of changing years, Your 
goodness never fails. Help us to know that to embrace Your counsel is 
the way to find the road to abundant life. Sustain our Senators. 
Empower them to walk blamelessly and honor You by doing what is right.
  Lord, prosper the works of their hands, and use them to help our 
Nation and world reflect the greatness of Your Kingdom. May their 
mouths speak wisdom and their hearts possess a knowledge of Your 
holiness, as You sanctify them through Your truth.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
