# TRIBUTE TO LINDSEY BORCHERDING

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO LINDSEY BORCHERDING

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Lindsey 
Borcherding, the Okeechobee County Teacher of the Year from Yearling 
Middle School in Okeechobee, FL.
  Lindsey teaches seventh grade math at Yearling Middle School and said 
her students were really excited and supportive of her nomination. 
While accepting the award, Lindsey thanked the people who have 
supported and helped her throughout her teaching career, especially her 
students. Lindsey stated that none of this would be possible without 
the help of countless others, and she is grateful to be a part of the 
lives of her students and colleagues. She said it was an exciting and 
emotional moment and was incredibly honored to be named the winner.
  I would like to congratulate Lindsey for her commitment to teaching 
her students throughout the years. I extend my best wishes to her and 
look forward to hearing of her continued success.●

                          ____________________
```
