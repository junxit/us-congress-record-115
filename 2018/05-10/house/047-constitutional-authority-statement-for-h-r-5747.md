# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5747

**Measures:** H.R. 5747

```
            By Mr. HUDSON:
        H.R. 5747.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Amendment.l6. ``The Congress shall have power to lay and 
     collect taxes on incomes, from whatever source derived, 
     without apportionment among the several States, and without 
     regard to any census or enumeration''
```
