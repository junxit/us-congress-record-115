# PERMISSION FOR THE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL 6 P.M. ON FRIDAY, MAY 11, 2018, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORTS

**Speaking:** Dent, Charles W. (D000604) [R-PA]

```
PERMISSION FOR THE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO HAVE UNTIL 6 P.M. ON 
            FRIDAY, MAY 11, 2018, TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORTS

  Mr. DENT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee on 
Appropriations have until 6 p.m. on Friday, May 11, 2018, to file 
privileged reports to accompany measures making appropriations for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
