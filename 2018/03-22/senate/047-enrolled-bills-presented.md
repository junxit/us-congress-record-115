# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, March 22, 2018, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 2030. An act to deem the compliance date for amended 
     energy conservation standards for ceiling fan light kits to 
     be January 21, 2020, and for other purposes.
       S. 2040. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 621 Kansas Avenue in 
     Atchison, Kansas, as the ``Amelia Earhart Post Office 
     Building''.
```
