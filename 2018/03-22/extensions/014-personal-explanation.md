  Roll Call No. 117 NO--On Ordering the Previous Question;
  Roll Call No. 118 NO--Providing for consideration of H.R. 4566, the 
Alleviating Stress Test Burdens to Help Investors Act; Providing for 
consideration of H.R. 5247, the Trickett Wendler, Frank Mongiello, 
Jordan McLinn, and Matthew Bellina Right to Try Act of 2018; and for 
other purposes; and
  Roll Call No. 119 YES--Alleviating Stress Test Burdens to Help
