# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6475

**Measures:** H.R. 6475

```
            By Mr. GALLAGHER:
        H.R. 6475.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 of the Constitution, which 
     enumerates the power to ``To regulate commerce with foreign 
     nations.''
```
