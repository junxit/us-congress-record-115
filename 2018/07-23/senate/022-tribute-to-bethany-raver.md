# TRIBUTE TO BETHANY RAVER

```
                        TRIBUTE TO BETHANY RAVER

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Bethany for her hard work as 
an intern at the Senate Republican Policy Committee. I recognize her 
efforts and contributions to my office, as well as to the State of 
Wyoming.
  Bethany is a native of Casper. She is a student at Casper College, 
where she is studying business administration. She has demonstrated a 
strong work ethic, which has made her an invaluable asset to our 
office. The quality of her work is reflected in her great efforts over 
the last several months.
  I want to thank Bethany for the dedication she has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of 
our team. I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her next journey.●
```
