# Congressional Record (Bound Edition), Volume 164 for May 31, 2018

```
[[Page 8352]]

                     SENATE--Thursday, May 31, 2018

  The Senate met at 11:30 and 6 seconds a.m. and was called to order by 
the Honorable Mike Lee, a Senator from the State of Utah.

                          ____________________



              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Hatch).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                     Washington, DC, May 31, 2018.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Mike Lee, a Senator from the State of Utah, to perform the 
     duties of the Chair.
                                                   Orrin G. Hatch,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. LEE thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________



           ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JUNE 4, 2018, AT 3 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until 3 p.m. on Monday, June 4, 2018.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11:30 and 36 seconds a.m., adjourned until 
Monday, June 4, 2018, at 3 p.m.
```
