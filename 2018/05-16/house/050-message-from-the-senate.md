# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 52

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Lasky, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a Joint Resolution of the following title in 
which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S.J. Res. 52. Joint Resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the Federal Communications 
     Commission relating to ``Restoring Internet Freedom''.
  The CHAIR. The Committee will resume its sitting.
```
