# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2850

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2850

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2850

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.

       A bill (S. 2850) to amend the White Mountain Apache Tribe 
     Water Rights Quantification Act of 2010 to clarify the use of 
     amounts in the WMAT Settlement Fund.

calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I object to further 
proceedings.

placed on the calendar.
```
