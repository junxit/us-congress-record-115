# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       111. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson, a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to urging Congress 
     to propose an amendment to the United States Constitution, 
     pursuant to Article V, that would prohibit a President of the 
     United States from pardoning himself or herself for any high 
     crime or misdemeanor that he or she might have committed; 
     which was referred to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
