# IN HONOR OF CHIC AND BILLIE RAWLINGS' 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Cheney, Liz (C001109) [R-WY]

```
     IN HONOR OF CHIC AND BILLIE RAWLINGS' 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LIZ CHENEY

                               of wyoming

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 5, 2018

  Ms. CHENEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my congratulations to 
Chic and Billie Rawlings on the celebration of their 50th Wedding 
Anniversary.
   This significant benchmark is a symbol of their commitment to each 
other and to their family. I am happy to join their friends and family 
in extending my best to them on this special occasion.
   Again, Mr. Speaker, I would like to extend my congratulations to 
Chic and Billie on the celebration of their 50th Wedding Anniversary.
```
