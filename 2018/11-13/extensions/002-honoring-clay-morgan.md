# HONORING CLAY MORGAN

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                          HONORING CLAY MORGAN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 13, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Clay Morgan for his 
contribution to our state and Henry County.
  As a member of the 6th District Faith Based Advisory Committee, Clay 
regularly met with my team to discuss important faith and family issues 
affecting Hoosiers. Through his ministry and community service, Clay 
has made New Castle a better place to live.
  On a personal note, Clay is a brother in Christ, and I appreciate his 
prayers and encouragement over the years. Clay is a wise adviser and 
among my closest friends in politics. I want to thank him for his 
incredible support of my work in Congress. I wish him continued success 
in all that God has planned for him and his family.

                          ____________________
```
