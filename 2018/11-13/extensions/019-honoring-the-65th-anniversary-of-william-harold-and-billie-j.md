# HONORING THE 65TH ANNIVERSARY OF WILLIAM HAROLD AND BILLIE JO BROWN GUTHRIE

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
  HONORING THE 65TH ANNIVERSARY OF WILLIAM HAROLD AND BILLIE JO BROWN 
                                GUTHRIE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 13, 2018

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 65th 
wedding anniversary of William Harold and Billie Jo Brown Guthrie of 
Oxford, Alabama.
  They were married on November 1, 1953 at First Baptist Church of 
Oxford. Harold was in the Navy and came home on leave for the wedding 
and returned immediately to San Diego, California.
  Harold retired from the Navy Reserve as Lieutenant Commander after 25 
years and from Anniston Army Depot after 30 years.
  Billie Jo was a house wife and she and Harold raised three daughters. 
They've been blessed with one grandson and five great-grandchildren.
  The Guthries have lived in Oxford their entire 65 years of marriage 
and have been faithful members of Meadowbrook Baptist Church for over 
50 years.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the 65th wedding 
anniversary of Harold and Billie Jo.

                          ____________________
```
