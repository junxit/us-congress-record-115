# APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO NATIONAL SECURITY COMMISSION ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**Speaking:** Nunes, Devin (N000181) [R-CA]

```
APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO NATIONAL SECURITY COMMISSION ON ARTIFICIAL 
                              INTELLIGENCE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DEVIN NUNES

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 13, 2018

  Mr. NUNES. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to Section 1051 of the John S. 
McCain National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2019 (Public

National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence.
```
