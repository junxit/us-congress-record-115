# SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 2377, S. 3414, S. 3442

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  The Speaker announced his signature to enrolled bills of the Senate 
of the following titles:

       S. 2377. An act to designate the Federal building and 
     United States courthouse located at 200 West 2nd Street in 
     Dayton, Ohio, as the ``Walter H. Rice Federal Building and 
     United States Courthouse''.
       S. 3414. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 20 Ferry Road in 
     Saunderstown, Rhode Island, as the ``Captain Matthew J. 
     August Post Office''.
       S. 3442. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 105 Duff Street in Macon, 
     Missouri, as the ``Arla W. Harrell Post Office''.

                          ____________________
```
