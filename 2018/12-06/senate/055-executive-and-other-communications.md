                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

with accompanying papers, reports, and documents, and were referred as 
indicated:

        EC-7374. A communication from the Assistant Secretary of 
     Defense (Homeland Defense and Global Security), transmitting,
