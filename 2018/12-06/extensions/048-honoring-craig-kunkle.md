# HONORING CRAIG KUNKLE

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                         HONORING CRAIG KUNKLE

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 6, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize a former member of my 
staff, Craig Kunkle, for his work in my office and his service to our 
state.
  I've known Craig for more than two decades and during that time, he 
has become a close friend and trusted adviser. Craig has proven himself 
to be one of our state's top political minds. There's no doubt that I 
wouldn't be where I am today without Craig's counsel and help every 
step of the way.
  On a personal note, Craig is a talented leader and loyal friend. He 
has already impacted our state in extraordinary ways. But, I am certain 
that his biggest impact is yet to come. I want to thank Craig for his 
friendship and dedication to our work. I wish him continued success in 
all that God has planned for his family.

                          ____________________
```
