# RECOGNIZING HUILIANG LIU

**Speaking:** Coffman, Mike (C001077) [R-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING HUILIANG LIU

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 6, 2018

  Mr. COFFMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the President and 
co-founder of the Denver Chinese School, Huiliang Liu. President 
Huiliang Liu has overseen the growth of the school from a single room 
class in 1998 to its current size of three different campuses in the 
Denver metropolitan area with over four hundred students. Huiliang Liu 
is truly an icon in our community and it is my honor to recognize him 
in the United States House of Representatives.

[[Page E1610]]

  President Huiliang Liu's work has made the difference in the lives of 
countless Chinese-American students as they gain a greater appreciation 
and understanding of their ancestor's historical homeland, and thereby 
contributing to the cultural fabric of our community.
  The Denver Chinese School provides courses and programs that serve to 
enlighten the community with educational and cultural enrichment. The 
Denver Chinese School organizes the largest Colorado Chinese New Year 
Celebration held in the State of Colorado.
  Throughout his 20 years of dedicated service, President Huiliang Liu 
has guided the Denver Chinese School to be the excellent organization 
and valuable asset to the State of Colorado that it is today. I look 
forward to and wish Huiliang Liu and the Denver Chinese School nothing 
but continued success in its future.

                          ____________________
```
