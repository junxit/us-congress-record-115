# HONORING MOLLY GILLASPIE

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                        HONORING MOLLY GILLASPIE

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 6, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize a longtime member of my 
staff, Molly Gillaspie, for her work in my office and her service to 
our state.
  Molly served as my Communications Director for more than a year in my 
Washington, D.C. office and continued working in Indiana as part of our 
team throughout the past year. Molly was responsible for all my 
official communications and media relations, which included writing 
press releases, speeches, opinion editorials and more. Molly is an 
outstanding member of our staff who always came to work with a smile on 
her face. I am truly grateful for everything she has done for our team.
  On a personal note, I admire Molly's commitment to her family. Molly 
works very hard and delivers great results. But, you always have the 
sense that Molly's priorities are in the right order.
  I want to thank Molly for her friendship and dedication to our work. 
I wish her and her husband, Adam, continued success in all that God has 
planned for their family. I have no doubt Molly's biggest impact on our 
state and nation are yet to come. I look forward to cheering Molly on 
in those efforts.
```
