# HONORING JOHN HATTER

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                          HONORING JOHN HATTER

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 6, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize a longtime member of my 
staff, John Hatter, for his work in my office and his service to our 
state.
  John has been on our team from the very beginning, serving as our 
Director of Constituent Services. In this role, John is responsible for 
managing our constituent services program, coordinating the 6th 
Congressional District Veterans Advisory Committee, and leading the 6th 
Congressional District Military Service Academy Nomination Board, which 
nominates young Hoosiers to serve at the United States Military Service 
Academies.
  On a personal note, I admire John's loyalty, intellect and work 
ethic. He has been a valuable member of our team, and I have no doubt 
John's future is very bright. I want to thank John for his friendship 
and his hard work in our office. I wish him, Katia, and the kids 
continued success in all that God has planned for their family.

                          ____________________
```
