# HONORING CHASEN BULLOCK

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                        HONORING CHASEN BULLOCK

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 6, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize a former member of my 
staff, Chasen Bullock, for his work in my office and his service to our 
state.
  Chasen worked in my Indiana office for nearly a year, serving as the 
head of our political operations. Chasen is a rock star and was a 
tremendous leader and asset to our team. He brought a wealth of 
experience and knowledge and was someone who became a trusted adviser, 
but more importantly, a great friend.
  On a personal note, Chasen is one of the rarest beasts you can find 
in Washington--a talented leader with humility and integrity. I wish 
Chasen continued success in all that God has planned for him and his 
family. I have no doubt Chasen's biggest impact on our nation are yet 
to come. I look forward to cheering Chasen on in those efforts.

                          ____________________
```
