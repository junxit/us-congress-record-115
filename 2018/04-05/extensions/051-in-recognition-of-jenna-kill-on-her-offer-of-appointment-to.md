# IN RECOGNITION OF JENNA KILL ON HER OFFER OF APPOINTMENT TO ATTEND THE UNITED STATES AIR FORCE ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
IN RECOGNITION OF JENNA KILL ON HER OFFER OF APPOINTMENT TO ATTEND THE 
                    UNITED STATES AIR FORCE ACADEMY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 5, 2018

  Mr. LATTA. Mr. Speaker, it is my great pleasure to pay special 
tribute to an outstanding student from Ohio's Fifth Congressional 
District. I am pleased to announce that Jenna Kill of Sylvania, Ohio 
has been offered an appointment to the United States Air Force Academy 
in Colorado Springs, Colorado.
  Jenna's offer of appointment permits her to attend the United States 
Air Force Academy this fall with the incoming Class of 2022. Attending 
one of our nation's military academies not only offers the opportunity 
to serve our country, but also guarantees a world-class education while 
undertaking one of the most challenging and rewarding experiences of a 
lifetime.
  Jenna brings a tremendous amount of leadership, service, and 
dedication to the incoming Class of 2022. While attending Northview 
High School in Sylvania, Ohio, Jenna was a member of the National Honor 
Society and was ranked in the top five percent of her class.
  Throughout high school, Jenna participated in soccer, and was captain 
for both the cross country and track and field teams, earning varsity 
letters in all three sports. Jenna also served as a co-leader for the 
NASA Student Launch Team, was involved in leadership academy, and 
participated in competitions such as Team America Rocketry Contest, and 
S4 Satellite Rocketry Team. I am confident that Jenna will carry the 
lessons of her student and athletic leadership to the Air Force 
Academy.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Jenna 
Kill on her offer of appointment to the United States Air Force 
Academy. Our service academies offer the finest military training and 
education available. I am positive that Jenna will excel during her 
career at the Air Force Academy, and I ask my colleagues to join me in 
extending their best wishes to her as she begins her service to our 
Nation.

                          ____________________
```
