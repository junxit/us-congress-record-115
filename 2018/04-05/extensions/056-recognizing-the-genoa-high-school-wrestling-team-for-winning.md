# RECOGNIZING THE GENOA HIGH SCHOOL WRESTLING TEAM FOR WINNING THE INDIVIDUAL TOURNAMENT TEAM STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

```
   RECOGNIZING THE GENOA HIGH SCHOOL WRESTLING TEAM FOR WINNING THE 
             INDIVIDUAL TOURNAMENT TEAM STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 5, 2018

  Mr. LATTA. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the Genoa High School 
Wrestling Team for winning the Ohio School Athletic Association 
Division III Individual Tournament Team State Championship. The title 
completes the double as Genoa won the Dual Team Championship this 
season as well.
  The Comets posted a scored of 113.5 led by Junior Dylan D'Emilio who 
won an individual state title and finished the season 
52-0.
  Wrestling tests the mettle of its competitors, and it takes grit, 
determination, and toughness to compete at a high level. These 
competitors deserve our recognition. The Genoa community should be 
proud of the team's accomplishments.
  Once again, congratulations to Coach Bob Bergman and the Comets on 
the state championship. Great job Genoa wrestling.
```
