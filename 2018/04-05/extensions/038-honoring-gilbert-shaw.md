# HONORING GILBERT SHAW

```
                         HONORING GILBERT SHAW

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JACKY ROSEN

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 5, 2018

  Ms. ROSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a longtime member of 
the Las Vegas community on the occasion of his 90th birthday, which he 
will celebrate on April 10, 2018. I am proud to stand today and let 
Congress and our country know what an amazing man he is.
  Gilbert Shaw is a founding member of Congregation Ner Tamid, and 
served in the Navy and Coast Guard for twenty-seven years. He is an 
officer of the Jewish War Veterans of the USA, serving as the Senior 
Vice Commander of Sgt. Manny Peven Post 65. To this day, Mr. Shaw still 
teaches photography to fellow senior citizens at a Life-Long Learning 
Institute.
  I want to congratulate Gilbert on reaching this incredible milestone, 
and thank him for his years of dedicated service. He has lived a long, 
impressive, and successful life. Today, I join Gilbert's family, 
friends and the entire Southern Nevada community to wish him a very 
happy 90th birthday and for many more joyful memories in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
