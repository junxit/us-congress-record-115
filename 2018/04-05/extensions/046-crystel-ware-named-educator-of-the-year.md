# CRYSTEL WARE NAMED EDUCATOR OF THE YEAR

```
                CRYSTEL WARE NAMED EDUCATOR OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 5, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Crystel Ware for 
being awarded the 2018 Texas Alliance of Black School Educators (TABSE) 
Educator of the Year.
  Crystel is the math and science facilitator at Williams Elementary 
School in Rosenberg, TX. The TABSE Educator of the Year award is gifted 
to teachers who truly make a difference with their students and 
demonstrate an unparalleled commitment to enhancing the quality of 
classroom instruction by using innovative teaching techniques. Ware 
also previously received the Educator of the Year award from the 
Houston Area Association of Black School Educators in 2017. Her 
achievements were recognized on February 25th at the TABSE'S annual 
conference in Houston.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Crystel on receiving this honor. We thank her 
for her dedication to her students and look forward to seeing what she 
accomplishes in the future.

                          ____________________
```
