# IN RECOGNITION OF DAVID MULLIGAN ON HIS OFFER OF APPOINTMENT TO ATTEND THE UNITED STATES AIR FORCE ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Latta, Robert E. (L000566) [R-OH]

```
IN RECOGNITION OF DAVID MULLIGAN ON HIS OFFER OF APPOINTMENT TO ATTEND 
                  THE UNITED STATES AIR FORCE ACADEMY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 5, 2018

  Mr. LATTA. Mr. Speaker, it is my great pleasure to pay special 
tribute to an outstanding student from Ohio's Fifth Congressional 
District. I am pleased to announce that David Mulligan of Sylvania, 
Ohio has been offered an appointment to the United States Air Force 
Academy, in Colorado Springs, Colorado.
  David's offer of appointment permits him to attend the United States 
Air Force Academy this fall with the incoming Class of 2022. Attending 
one of our nation's military academies not only offers the opportunity 
to serve our country, but also guarantees a world-class education while 
undertaking one of the most challenging and rewarding experiences of a 
lifetime.
  David brings a tremendous amount of leadership, service, and 
dedication to the incoming Class of 2022. While attending Northview 
High School in Sylvania, Ohio, David served as secretary for the 
National Honors Society, and was active in leadership academy and LINK 
Crew.
  Throughout high school, David participated in recreational 
basketball, and was senior captain for both varsity cross country and 
varsity baseball. David is also involved with church youth group and 
the NASA Student Launch Team. I am confident that David will carry the 
lessons of his student and athletic leadership to the Air Force 
Academy.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating David 
Mulligan on his offer of appointment to the United States Air Force 
Academy. Our service academies offer the finest military training and 
education available. I am positive that David will excel during his 
career at the Air Force Academy, and I ask my colleagues to join me in 
extending their best wishes to him as he begins his service to our 
Nation.

                          ____________________
```
