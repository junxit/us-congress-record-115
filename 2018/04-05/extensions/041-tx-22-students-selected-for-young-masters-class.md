# TX-22 STUDENTS SELECTED FOR YOUNG MASTERS CLASS

```
            TX-22 STUDENTS SELECTED FOR YOUNG MASTERS CLASS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 5, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Lauren Mathews, 
a freshman at Seven Lakes High school and Lauren Yang, a junior at 
Dulles High School for being selected for Young Masters Class.
  Texas Young Masters recognizes students from grades 8-11 who excel in 
dance, theater, film, music, media, and literary and visual arts. More 
than 300 students from across Texas applied, but only the most talented 
fifteen students received the title of ``Young Master.'' These students 
were awarded with $10,000 multi-year scholarships to enhance their 
studies and provide them access to enhanced opportunities prior to 
college. Lauren Mathews plays the cello and Lauren Yang was given the 
award for her dedication to visual arts. These two outstanding students 
will be recognized during the 2018 class of Young Masters during an 
awards ceremony and concert on April 16th at Austin's ZACH Theater.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Lauren Mathews and Lauren Yang for achieving 
this great artistic accomplishment. I am proud and confident of their 
continued success.

                          ____________________
```
