# TRIBUTE TO SHEILA KUNZE

```
                        TRIBUTE TO SHEILA KUNZE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 5, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Sheila Kunze of Lewis, Iowa on her retirement as the Cass 
County Clerk of Court. Sheila began her duties in the Clerk's office in 
1979, and has served the citizens of Cass County for almost 39 years.
  Appointed as the Cass County Clerk of Court in 2001, Sheila said when 
she joined the Clerk's office she was hired as an office clerk. During 
her 39 years of service she has witnessed many changes in the office 
and the way records are kept. Today, almost everything is 
electronically filed, but the office still handles some paper forms. 
Sheila said the greatest part of her job was ``working with all the 
people, I've met a lot of people over the years, and made a lot of 
friends.''
  Mr. Speaker, Sheila has made a difference in her community by helping 
and serving others. I am honored to recognize her today and I ask that 
my colleagues in the United States House of Representatives join me in 
applauding her accomplishments and in wishing her nothing but the best 
in her retirement.

                          ____________________
```
