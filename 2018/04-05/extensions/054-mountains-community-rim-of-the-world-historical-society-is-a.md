# MOUNTAINS COMMUNITY RIM OF THE WORLD HISTORICAL SOCIETY IS AWARDED NON- PROFIT ORGANIZATION OF THE YEAR

```
MOUNTAINS COMMUNITY RIM OF THE WORLD HISTORICAL SOCIETY IS AWARDED NON-
                    PROFIT ORGANIZATION OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. PAUL COOK

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 5, 2018

  Mr. COOK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Rim of the World 
Historical Society, which has been selected by the Lake Arrowhead 
Communities Chamber of Commerce as its Non-Profit of the Year. The 
Society is dedicated to collecting, preserving and sharing the history 
of the Western San Bernardino Mountains communities.
  The Rim of the World Historical Society was founded in 1986 as the 
Crestline Historical Society. The early organization provided temporary 
history exhibits and programs. Over time, the name changed to the Rim 
of the World Historical Society the focus shifted to being a stronger 
and more visible part of mountaintop communities, from Green Valley 
Lake to Cedar Pines Park. Its volunteers have designed and installed 
exhibits within the Mountain History Museum that are open to the 
public. Through the work of countless volunteers and community members, 
the Mountain History Museum has provided a window into the history of 
our beautiful mountain communities.
  On behalf of the U.S. House of Representatives, I would like to thank 
Rim of the World Historical Society for their hard work, dedication, 
and passion for culture and history of our mountain communities.
```
