# TRIBUTE TO PAT AND HOWARD ELLIOTT

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                   TRIBUTE TO PAT AND HOWARD ELLIOTT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 5, 2018

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Pat and Howard Elliott of Farragut, Iowa on the very special occasion 
of their 65th wedding anniversary.
  Pat and Howard's lifelong commitment to each other and their family 
truly embodies Iowa values. As they reflect on their 65th anniversary, 
may their commitment grow even stronger, as they continue to love, 
cherish, and honor one another for many years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend this great couple on their 65th year together 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
