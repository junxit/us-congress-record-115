# COMMENDING JAY SORENSEN

```
                        COMMENDING JAY SORENSEN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 15, 2018

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and commend Jay 
Sorensen upon his induction into the California Outdoors Hall of Fame. 
This honor is a testament to his dedication in working to promote 
conservation. As a lifelong fishing lover, his tireless advocacy for 
the Delta has not only inspired thousands of others to take part in the 
great outdoors, but has also helped preserve the Delta's waters and 
wildlife for future generations to appreciate. This incredible 
commitment to our region and to conservation merits our highest 
distinction.
  On behalf of the people of California's 3rd Congressional District, I 
offer my sincere congratulations as well as my best wishes for the 
years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
