# SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 46--DIRECTING THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES TO MAKE A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 5895

**Measures:** S. 46, S.Con.Res. 46, H.R. 5895

```
 SENATE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 46--DIRECTING THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE OF 
  REPRESENTATIVES TO MAKE A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 5895

  Mr. SHELBY submitted the following concurrent resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                            S. Con. Res. 46

       Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives 
     concurring), That, in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 5895, 
     the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the 
     following correction to the title so as to read: ``Making 
     consolidated appropriations for Energy and Water Development, 
     the Legislative Branch, Military Construction, Veterans 
     Affairs, and Related Agencies for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2019, and for other purposes.''.

                          ____________________
```
