# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord of our being, on yesterday, as we remembered September 11, we 
also felt gratitude for Your sovereignty over the affairs of humanity. 
May our lawmakers trust in Your unfolding providence to continue to 
sustain this land we love and guide our world.
  Lord, disentangle our Senators from life's turmoil, inspiring them to 
focus on accomplishing Your purposes. Lord, give them the wisdom to 
find time to read Sacred Scriptures. Remind them that they belong to 
You, as You draw them daily nearer to Yourself.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
