# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6790

**Measures:** H.R. 6790

```
           By Mr. MOONEY of West Virginia:
       H.R. 6790.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority for the Monetary Metals Tax 
     Neutrality Act of 2018 is found in Article I, Section 8, 
     which gives Congress the power to lay and collect taxes.
```
