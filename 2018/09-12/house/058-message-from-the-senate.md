# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 46, H.R. 5895

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate agrees to the report of the committee of conference on 
the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment of the Senate 
to the bill (H.R. 5895) ``An Act making appropriations for energy and 
water development and related agencies for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2019, and for other purposes.''.
  The message also announced that the Senate agreed to a concurrent 
resolution of the following title in which the concurrence of the House 
is requested:

       S. Con. Res. 46. Concurrent Resolution directing the Clerk 
     of the House of Representatives to make a correction in the 
     enrollment of H.R. 5895.

                          ____________________
```
