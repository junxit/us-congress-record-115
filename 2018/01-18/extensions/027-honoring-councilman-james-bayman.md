# HONORING COUNCILMAN JAMES BAYMAN

**Speaking:** Banks, Jim (B001299) [R-IN]

```
                    HONORING COUNCILMAN JAMES BAYMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM BANKS

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 18, 2018

  Mr. BANKS of Indiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Whitley 
County Councilman James Bayman. For 36 years, Councilman Bayman has 
represented the townships of Washington, Cleveland, Jefferson and the 
South precinct of my home town, Columbia City. I had the honor of 
serving with Councilman Bayman from 2008 through 2010, and I saw 
firsthand the positive influence he has had on the people of District 
4. Councilman Bayman is hard-working, honest and a caring public 
servant. I wish him the best as he begins the next chapter of his life. 
Whitley County will miss his leadership and commitment to public 
service.

                          ____________________
```
