# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Portman, Rob (P000449) [R-OH]

```

  Mr. PORTMAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
be in a period of morning business, with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 10 minutes each until 7 p.m., with the time equally 
divided, and that all quorum calls during that time also be equally 
divided.
```
