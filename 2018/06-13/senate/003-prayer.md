# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Loving and Ever-Present God, we thank You for being our helper in the 
present and our hope for the future. We trust You to direct our steps 
with Your providential power. Forgive our slowness to understand and 
our haste to question Your purposes.
  Lord, guide our lawmakers. Where there is perplexity, provide 
clarity. Where there is sickness, bring healing. Where there is doubt, 
give faith. Where there is despair, bestow hope. Hasten the day when 
the Earth will be filled with Your glory as the waters cover the sea.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
