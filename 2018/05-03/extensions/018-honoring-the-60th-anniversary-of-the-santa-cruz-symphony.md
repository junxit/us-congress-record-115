# HONORING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SANTA CRUZ SYMPHONY

```
        HONORING THE 60TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SANTA CRUZ SYMPHONY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JIMMY PANETTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 3, 2018

  Mr. PANETTA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of an organization 
that displays the unique artistic virtue of my district. The Santa Cruz 
Symphony has served my district on the central coast of California by 
promoting, fostering, and furthering musical cultural enrichment, 
appreciation, and innovation. This year the Santa Cruz Symphony is 
celebrating its sixtieth anniversary. Since its founding in 1958, the 
Santa Cruz Symphony has fostered the musical arts by organizing 
thousands of concerts, community events and productions over the years.
  The Santa Cruz Symphony has helped children in Santa Cruz County with 
their youth education program. Since 1996, this program has engaged 
elementary school students throughout the county by coordinating year-
long classical music listening programs, providing musicians who attend 
classrooms to discuss and demonstrate their instruments, and hosting 
free youth concerts for over 4,000 4th and 5th graders. With a deep 
commitment to education, The Santa Cruz Symphony has also established 
the Mueller Scholarship Fund which provides financial assistance to 
aspiring young musicians.
  The Santa Cruz Symphony has a greatly talented array of musicians and 
composers and has hosted many notable concerts. The Symphony takes 
great pride in working with and being conducted by Maestro Daniel 
Stewart's remarkable musical genius and artistic talent. During their 
60th anniversary season, the symphony's most recent production, 
Celebration, has generated much acclaim. By featuring renowned soloists 
like Yuja Wang and promoting the works of living composers, The Santa 
Cruz Symphony continues to add to the musical richness of our community 
by delivering outstanding performances.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my pleasure to recognize the dedication and 
cultural enrichment the Santa Cruz Symphony has provided to the Central 
Coast, Santa Cruz, and the entire State of California. I ask my 
distinguished colleagues to join me in recognizing the Santa Cruz 
Symphony as an indispensable cultural asset of our community.
```
