                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ROBERT B. ADERHOLT

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 22, 2018

  Mr. ADERHOLT. Mr. Speaker, I would like to go on the record as fully 
supporting H.R. 2, the Agriculture and Nutrition Act of 2018. I was 
detained off the floor and missed the opportunity to cast my vote on a 
bill that indeed supports our farmers and rural America. I would like 
to reflect that I voted in favor of this bill, H.R. 2, the first time 
it came to the floor on May 18, 2018. I commend Chairman Conaway on 
passage of the bill on the House floor. This new Farm Bill will benefit 
consumers and producers, and all those who make our food supply chain 
abundant and secure.
