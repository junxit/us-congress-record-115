# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Res. 714, S.Res. 715

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. GRAHAM (for himself, Mrs. Feinstein, Mr. Rubio, 
             Mr. Markey, Mr. Young, and Mr. Coons):
       S. Res. 714. A resolution expressing the sense of the 
     Senate that Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman bin Abd al Aziz 
     Al Saud of Saudi Arabia be held accountable for contributing

     dissidents and activists inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, 
     the use of force to intimidate rivals, and the abhorrent and 
     unjustified murder of journalist Jamal Khashoggi; to the
```
