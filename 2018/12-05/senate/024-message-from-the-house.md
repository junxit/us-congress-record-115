# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1207

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  The President pro tempore (Mr. Hatch) announced that on today, 
December 5, 2018, he had signed the following enrolled bill, which was 
previously signed by the Speaker of the House:

       H.R. 1207. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 306 River Street in Tilden, 
     Texas, as the ``Tilden Veterans Post Office''.
```
