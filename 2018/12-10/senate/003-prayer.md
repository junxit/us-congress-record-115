# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Eternal God, stay close to us, and we will have no need to fear. 
Enable us to feel the joy of Your saving presence. You have been our 
refuge in ages past. You continue to be our hope for the seasons to 
come.
  Inspire our Senators to live with a sense of accountability to You. 
Remind them that You are the only constituent they absolutely must 
please. Lord, help them to emulate the depths of Your caring in their 
relationships and responsibilities, ever seeking to glorify Your Name.
  We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
