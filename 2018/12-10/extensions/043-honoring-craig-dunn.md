# HONORING CRAIG DUNN

**Speaking:** Messer, Luke (M001189) [R-IN]

```
                          HONORING CRAIG DUNN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 10, 2018

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, on behalf of the entire 6th 
Congressional District of Indiana, to recognize Craig Dunn for his 
contribution to our state.
  I have had the pleasure of knowing Craig for more than a decade. 
Craig served Howard County as the GOP Chairman for nearly my entire 
tenure in Congress. Although Howard County is not in my district, we 
have grown to become close friends and he remains a trusted advisor to 
me. Craig served the County with honor and distinction for years, and I 
have no doubt that our state is better off today due to his 
extraordinary leadership and service.
  I want to thank Craig for his friendship and loyalty to me over all 
these years. I wish him continued success in all that God has planned 
for his family.

                          ____________________
```
