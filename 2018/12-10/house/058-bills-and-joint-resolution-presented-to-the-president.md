# BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 143, H.R. 754, H.R. 1207

```
         BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on December 6, 2018, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills and joint resolution:

       H.J. Res. 143. Making further continuing appropriations for 
     fiscal year 2019, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 754. To award the Congressional Gold Medal to Anwar 
     Sadat in recognition of his heroic achievements and 
     courageous contributions to peace in the Middle East.
       H.R. 1207. To designate the facility of the United States 
     Postal Service located at 306 River Street in Tilden, Texas, 
     as the ``Tilden Veterans Post Office''.

                          ____________________
```
