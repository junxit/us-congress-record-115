# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 2018-02-08

                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR THURSDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)

nominations of Paul C. Ney, Jr., of Tennessee, to be General

Columbia, to be Chairman and Member of the Privacy and Civil 
Liberties Oversight Board, and John C. Anderson, to be United States

United States Attorney for the Middle District of Louisiana, Joseph 
P. Kelly, to be United States Attorney for the District of Nebraska,

  Select Committee on Intelligence: to receive a closed briefing
