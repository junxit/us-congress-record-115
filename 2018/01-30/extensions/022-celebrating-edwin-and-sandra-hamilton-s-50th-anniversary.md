# CELEBRATING EDWIN AND SANDRA HAMILTON'S 50TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Loudermilk, Barry (L000583) [R-GA]

```
        CELEBRATING EDWIN AND SANDRA HAMILTON'S 50TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BARRY LOUDERMILK

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 30, 2018

  Mr. LOUDERMILK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 50th 
wedding anniversary of Mr. Edwin Richard Hamilton and Mrs. Sandra Elain 
(Thompson) Hamilton.
  Edwin and Sandra were married on February 3, 1968 in Cartersville, 
Georgia by the Reverend Charles McPherson.
  Reaching the wonderful milestone of a golden anniversary shows such a 
great commitment to your spouse and family. This marriage stands as a 
blessing and an encouragement to me and all those who know this couple.
  Please join me in wishing Mr. and Mrs. Hamilton a very happy and 
blessed anniversary. May God bless them with many more years of 
happiness and health.

                          ____________________
```
