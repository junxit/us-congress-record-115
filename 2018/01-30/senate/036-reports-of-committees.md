                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. JOHNSON, from the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs, with an amendment in the nature of a 
     substitute:
       S. 1847. A bill to amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 
     to ensure that the needs of children are considered in 
     homeland security, trafficking, and disaster recovery 
     planning, and for other purposes (Rept. No. 115-202).


 =========================== NOTE ===========================

following appears: S. 1847. A bill to amend the Homeland Security 
Act of 2002 to ensure that the needs of children are considered in 
homeland security, trafficking, and disaster recovery planning, 
and for other purposes (Rept. No. 115-201).

amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to ensure that the needs 
of children are considered in homeland security, trafficking, and 
disaster recovery planning, and for other purposes (Rept. No. 115-
202).
