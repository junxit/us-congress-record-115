# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 2553

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, October 4, 2018, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bill:

       S. 2553. An act to amend title XVIII of the Social Security 
     Act to prohibit Medicare part D plans from restricting 
     pharmacies from informing individuals regarding the prices 
     for certain drugs and biologicals.
```
