# PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF S. 2553

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 49, S. 2553

```
        PROVIDING FOR A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF S. 2553

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Chair lays before the 
House the following concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 49) providing 
for a correction in the enrollment of S. 2553.
  The Clerk read the title of the concurrent resolution.
  There was no objection.
  The text of the concurrent resolution is as follows:

                            S. Con. Res. 49

       Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives 
     concurring), That in the enrollment of the bill S. 2553, the 
     Secretary of the Senate shall make the following correction:
       Amend the title so as to read: ``A bill to amend title 
     XVIII of the Social Security Act to prohibit Medicare part D 
     plans from restricting pharmacies from informing individuals 
     regarding the prices for certain drugs and biologicals.''.

  The concurrent resolution was concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
