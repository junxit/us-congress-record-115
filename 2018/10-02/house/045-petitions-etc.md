# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       122. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Gregory D. Watson, 
     a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to requesting the enactment 
     of legislation to levy a 10 percent tax upon remittances made 
     by aliens within the United States to recipients in foreign 
     nations--excluding payment for the lawful purchase of goods 
     or services--and to utilize the proceeds of such tax for 
     construction and maintenance of breach-resistant physical 
     barriers between the United States and nations with which the 
     United States shares land borders.; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Ways and Means.
```
