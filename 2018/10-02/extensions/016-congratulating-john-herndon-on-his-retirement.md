# CONGRATULATING JOHN HERNDON ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
             CONGRATULATING JOHN HERNDON ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 2, 2018

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, as a former Naval aviator, I rise today to 
congratulate a Navy veteran and longtime federal employee, John 
Herndon, on his retirement.
  John is retiring from his position as a FG-14 Program Manager in the 
Department of Transportation Federal Aviation Administration. He served 
a total of 37 years, four months, and seventeen days in the federal 
workforce. After serving in the Navy for 20 years, six months and six 
days and deploying worldwide in support of reconnaissance squadrons, 
John retired as Maintenance Chief. His work serving our country both in 
and out of uniform deserves our utmost respect and gratitude.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Mr. John Herndon. May he have fair winds and 
following seas in his retirement.

                          ____________________
```
