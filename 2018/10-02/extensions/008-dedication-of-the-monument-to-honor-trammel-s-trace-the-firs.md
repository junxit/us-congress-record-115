# DEDICATION OF THE MONUMENT TO HONOR TRAMMEL'S TRACE, THE FIRST ROAD TO TEXAS

**Speaking:** Gohmert, Louie (G000552) [R-TX]

```
DEDICATION OF THE MONUMENT TO HONOR TRAMMEL'S TRACE, THE FIRST ROAD TO 
                                 TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LOUIE GOHMERT

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 2, 2018

  Mr. GOHMERT. Mr. Speaker, the following certificate of congressional 
recognition is presented to the Stone Fort Chapter, Daughters of the 
Republic of Texas October 2, 2018:

       Upon the dedication of the monument to honor Trammel's 
     Trace, the First Road to Texas, upon whose path, such notable 
     Texans as Sam Houston, Jim Bowie and Davy Crockett traveled 
     into Nacogdoches and on to the battles of the Revolution; and 
     in further recognition of its historic significance as a 
     route which brought many to settle this great and untamed 
     territory along the Red and Sabine Rivers, all while 
     promoting trade and commerce along its well-travelled path, 
     thereby paving the way for the establishment of the Great 
     Republic of Texas.
       Presented on this 2nd day of October in the year of our 
     Lord 2018.

                          ____________________
```
