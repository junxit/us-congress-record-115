# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 3, 2018

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 302

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 3, 2018

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until 10 a.m., 
Wednesday, October 3; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings and the Executive Journal be approved to date, and the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; 
further, that notwithstanding rule XXII, all time postcloture on the 
House message to accompany H.R. 302 be considered expired at 12 noon.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
