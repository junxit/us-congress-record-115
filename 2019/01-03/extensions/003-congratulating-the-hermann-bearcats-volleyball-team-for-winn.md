# CONGRATULATING THE HERMANN BEARCATS VOLLEYBALL TEAM FOR WINNING THE 2018 MISSOURI CLASS 2 STATE VOLLEYBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
  CONGRATULATING THE HERMANN BEARCATS VOLLEYBALL TEAM FOR WINNING THE 
          2018 MISSOURI CLASS 2 STATE VOLLEYBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 3, 2019

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Hermann Bearcats Volleyball team for 
winning the 2018 Missouri Class 2 State Volleyball Championship.
   This team and Coach Linda Lampkin should be commended for all of 
their hard work throughout this past year and for bringing home the 
state championship to their school and community.
   I ask you to join me in recognizing the Hermann Bearcats Volleyball 
team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
