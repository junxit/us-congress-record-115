# TRIBUTE TO LIEUTENANT COLONEL VINCENT B. MYERS

**Speaking:** Wenstrup, Brad R. (W000815) [R-OH]

```
             TRIBUTE TO LIEUTENANT COLONEL VINCENT B. MYERS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRAD R. WENSTRUP

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 27, 2017

  Mr. WENSTRUP. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in gratitude for the hard 
work and commitment of Lieutenant Colonel Vincent B. Myers, who has 
served as the Medical Army Legislative Liaison to my office for the 
past two years.
  A Lieutenant Colonel in the United States Army and an experienced 
health care provider, Vince has been essential to my office and the 
House Armed Services Committee, as we work toward the goal of enhancing 
medical readiness and providing top care for our service members.
  On May 11, Vince will continue his committed service to the United 
States as he takes command of McDonald Army Health Center at Fort 
Eustis, Virginia. I know that in this role, Vince will serve with 
conviction and honor.
  I congratulate Vince, and thank him for his work and friendship.
```
