**Speaking:** Sullivan, Dan (S001198) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S.Res. 132

```
   CONGRATULATING THE ASHLAND UNIVERSITY WOMEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM FOR 
 WINNING THE 2017 NATIONAL COLLEGIATE ATHLETIC ASSOCIATION DIVISION II 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

Commerce Committee be discharged from further consideration of and the 
Senate now proceed to the consideration of S. Res. 132.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the resolution by title.

       A resolution (S. Res. 132) congratulating the Ashland 
     University women's basketball team for winning the 2017

  The resolution (S. Res. 132) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
```
