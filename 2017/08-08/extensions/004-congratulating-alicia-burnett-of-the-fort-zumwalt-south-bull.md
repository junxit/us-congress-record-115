# CONGRATULATING ALICIA BURNETT OF THE FORT ZUMWALT SOUTH BULLDOGS

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
    CONGRATULATING ALICIA BURNETT OF THE FORT ZUMWALT SOUTH BULLDOGS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 8, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Alicia Burnett of the Fort Zumwalt South 
Bulldogs for her first place win in the 100, 200, and 400 meter dash at 
the 2017 Missouri Class 4 Girls Track and Field State Championship.
   Alicia and her coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for bringing home the state championship 
to their school and community.
   I ask you to join me in recognizing Alicia Burnett on a job well 
done.

                          ____________________
```
