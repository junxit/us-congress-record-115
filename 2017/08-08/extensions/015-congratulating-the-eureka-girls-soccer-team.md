# CONGRATULATING THE EUREKA GIRLS SOCCER TEAM

```
              CONGRATULATING THE EUREKA GIRLS SOCCER TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                  in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 8, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Eureka Girls Soccer Team on their first 
place win in the 2017 Missouri Class 4 Girls Soccer State Championship.
  This team should be commended for all of their hard work throughout 
this past year and for bringing home the state championship to their 
school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing the Eureka Girls Soccer Team for 
a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
