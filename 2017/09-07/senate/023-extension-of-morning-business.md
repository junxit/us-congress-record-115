# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Perdue, David (P000612) [R-GA], Donnelly, Joe (D000607) [D-IN], Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. PERDUE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that morning 
business, for debate only, be extended until 12 noon.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Perdue). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. DONNELLY. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
