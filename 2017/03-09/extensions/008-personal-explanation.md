# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Jenkins, Lynn (J000290) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.Res. 174, H.R. 725, H.R. 1301

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. LYNN JENKINS

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 9, 2017

  Ms. JENKINS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I was absent for roll call votes 
129 through 132 on the evening of March 8, 2017. I would have voted in 
favor of both votes which would provide for consideration of H.R. 725, 
the Innocent Party Protection Act. I would have also voted in favor of 
H. Res. 174 on ordering the previous question and providing for 
consideration of H.R. 1301, the Department of Defense Appropriations 
Act of 2017. Lastly, I would have voted against the motion to adjourn.
  Had I been present, I would have voted: YEA on Roll Call No. 129, YEA 
on Roll Call No. 130, YEA on Roll Call No. 131, and NAY on Roll Call 
No. 132.
```
