# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

```
                  ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, March 30, 2017, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled joint resolution:

       S.J. Res 34. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the Federal Communications 
     Commission relating to ``Protecting the Privacy of Customers 
     of Broadband and Other Telecommunications Services''.
```
