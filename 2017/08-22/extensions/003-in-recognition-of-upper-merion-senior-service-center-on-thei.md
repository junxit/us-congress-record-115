# IN RECOGNITION OF UPPER MERION SENIOR SERVICE CENTER ON THEIR 20TH ANNIVERSARY

```
  IN RECOGNITION OF UPPER MERION SENIOR SERVICE CENTER ON THEIR 20TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. PATRICK MEEHAN

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 22, 2017

  Mr. MEEHAN. Mr. Speaker, today I recognize the Upper Merion Senior 
Service Center of King of Prussia, PA for its twentieth anniversary. 
Caring for our senior citizens is a challenging yet immensely rewarding 
experience to which those at UMSSC have dedicated their lives.
  The Center enables seniors in the Seventh District to live life to 
the fullest. They facilitate activities that allow seniors to engage in 
physical, educational, and recreational activities. This social 
environment and steadfast care helps seniors enjoy their retirement and 
stay healthy.
  I hope UMSSC continues to provide their services for many years to 
come. The senior citizens of our district deserve the best care we can 
provide and UMSSC is a treasured place that does just that.
  I congratulate UMSSC for their twenty years of service to our 
community, and I wish them well in their efforts to provide top quality 
care for Upper Merion area seniors.

                          ____________________
```
