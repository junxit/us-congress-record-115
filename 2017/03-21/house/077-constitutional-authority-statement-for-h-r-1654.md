# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1654

**Measures:** H.R. 1654

```
           By Mr. McCLINTOCK:
       H.R. 1654.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 4, Section 3, Clause 2 of the U.S. Constitution, 
     which confers on Congress the power to make all needful Rules 
     and Regulations respecting the property belonging to the 
     United States.
```
