# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1659

**Measures:** H.R. 1659

```
           By Mr. ROSKAM:
       H.R. 1659.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 states that, ``The Congress should 
     have Power To lay and collect Taxes,'' and Article I, Section 
     7 states ``All Bills for raising Revenue shall originate in 
     the House of Represenatives.''
```
