**Speaking:** Portman, Rob (P000449) [R-OH]

                         RESOLUTIONS DISCHARGED

  Mr. PORTMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
applicable committees be discharged and the Senate proceed to the 
immediate consideration of the following resolutions, en bloc: S. Res. 
203, S. Res. 194, S. Res. 214, S. Res. 215, S. Res. 231, S. Res. 213, 
S. Res. 233 and S. Res. 221.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

resolutions en bloc.

resolutions be agreed to, the preambles be agreed to, and the motions 
to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table, all en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
