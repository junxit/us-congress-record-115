# New York City Terror Attack (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Hassan, Margaret Wood (H001076) [D-NH], Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA]

```
                      New York City Terror Attack

  Ms. HASSAN. Mr. President, I would like to start my remarks this 
morning by offering my thoughts to the victims of yesterday's 
horrendous act of terror in New York City, my thanks to the first 
responders, and I would note the resiliency of the people of New York. 
Once again, they are going forward with their lives today, showing the 
world and anyone who would do us harm that Americans stand together and 
that we move forward regardless of what our foes may try to do.
```
