# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4214

**Measures:** H.R. 4214

```
           By Mr. McCLINTOCK:
       H.R. 4214.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 2 of the United States 
     Constitution, ``The Borrowing Clause,'' which confers on 
     Congress the power to borrow money on the credit of the 
     United States.
```
