# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 622

**Speaking:** Stewart, Chris (S001192) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.R. 622

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 622

  Mr. STEWART. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 622, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Chaffetz of Utah, for the 
purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Utah?
  There was no objection.
```
