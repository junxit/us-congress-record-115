# RECOGNIZING ALBERT NAPOLI

```
                       RECOGNIZING ALBERT NAPOLI

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIMI WALTERS

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 21, 2017

  Mrs. MIMI WALTERS of California. Mr. Speaker, today, I recognize Mr. 
Albert Napoli's contributions to Orange County, California during his 
tenure as Chairman of the Greater Irvine Chamber of Commerce. Over the 
course of the last year, Albert successfully represented the 15,000 
businesses and industries that call Irvine, California home.
  Local Chambers of Commerce support small businesses and encourage 
economic growth and vitality. A thriving local economy helps companies 
of all sizes grow and invest in their operations, which strengthens our 
communities.
  I thank Albert for his work to strengthen and expand Irvine's 
business community. I wish him all the best in his future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
