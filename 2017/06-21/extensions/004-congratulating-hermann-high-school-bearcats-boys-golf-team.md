# CONGRATULATING HERMANN HIGH SCHOOL BEARCATS BOYS GOLF TEAM

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
       CONGRATULATING HERMANN HIGH SCHOOL BEARCATS BOYS GOLF TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 21, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Hermann High School Bearcats Boys Golf 
Team on their first place win in the 2017 Class 2 Missouri State Golf 
Tournament.
   This team includes Andrew Budnik, Justin Grosse, Matthew Heidger, 
Thomas Henson, Ross Henson, and their coach, Jeremy Hosick. They should 
be commended for all of their hard work throughout this past year and 
for bringing home the state championship to their school and community.
   I ask you to join me in recognizing the Hermann High School Bearcats 
Boys Golf Team on a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
