# CONGRATULATING GANNON WITHERS OF THE NEW BLOOMFIELD WILDCATS

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
      CONGRATULATING GANNON WITHERS OF THE NEW BLOOMFIELD WILDCATS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 21, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Gannon Withers of the New Bloomfield Wildcats 
for his second place finish in the 2017 Missouri Class 1 Boys 
Individual Golf State Championship.
   Gannon and his coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for bringing home the team state 
championship to their school and community.
   I ask you to join me in recognizing Gannon Withers of the New 
Bloomfield Wildcats for a job well done.
```
