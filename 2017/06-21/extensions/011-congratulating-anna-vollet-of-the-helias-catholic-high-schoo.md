# CONGRATULATING ANNA VOLLET OF THE HELIAS CATHOLIC HIGH SCHOOL LADY CRUSADERS

```
  CONGRATULATING ANNA VOLLET OF THE HELIAS CATHOLIC HIGH SCHOOL LADY 
                               CRUSADERS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 21, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Anna Vollet of the Helias Catholic High 
School Lady Crusaders on her second place finish at the 2017 Class 4 
Missouri Track and Field State Championship in the 200-meter sprint.
  Anna and her coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for Anna's success at the State 
Championship.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing Anna Vollet of the Helias 
Catholic High School Lady Crusaders for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
