# CONGRATULATING BRIANNA HALLER OF THE FATIMA COMETS CROSS COUNTRY TEAM

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
 CONGRATULATING BRIANNA HALLER OF THE FATIMA COMETS CROSS COUNTRY TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 13, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Brianna Haller of the Fatima Comets Cross 
Country team on winning the Class 2 Girls Individual Cross Country 
State Championship.
   Brianna Haller and her coach should be commended for all of their 
hard work throughout this past year and for bringing home the state 
championship to their school and community.
   I ask you to join me in recognizing Brianna Haller on a job well 
done.

                          ____________________
```
