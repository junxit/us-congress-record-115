# CONGRATULATING THE HELIAS CRUSADERS VOLLEYBALL TEAM

```
          CONGRATULATING THE HELIAS CRUSADERS VOLLEYBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 13, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Helias Crusaders Volleyball team on 
winning the Class 3 Volleyball State Championship.
  This team and their coach should be commended for all of their hard 
work throughout this past year and for bringing home the state 
championship to their school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing the Helias Crusaders Volleyball 
team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
