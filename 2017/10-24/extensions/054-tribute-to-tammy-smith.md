# TRIBUTE TO TAMMY SMITH

```
                         TRIBUTE TO TAMMY SMITH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 24, 2017

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Tammy Smith as the 2017 Taco John's International Manager 
of the Year.
  Tammy's leadership has helped the team at the Creston Taco John's 
achieve record-setting sales levels by providing great service and a 
friendly smile. Tammy was quoted in the Creston News Advertiser as 
saying ``It isn't just me. It's everybody in this store that makes 
something like that happen. You have to be doing everything right. 
There's no way I could come in and do it myself.''
  Mr. Speaker, it is an honor to represent leaders like Tammy in the 
United States Congress and it is with great pride that I recognize and 
applaud her for utilizing her talents to better both her community and 
the great State of Iowa. I ask that my colleagues in the United States 
House of Representatives join me in congratulating her for earning this 
outstanding award and in wishing her nothing but continued success.

                          ____________________
```
