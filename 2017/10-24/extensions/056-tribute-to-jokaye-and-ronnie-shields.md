# TRIBUTE TO JOKAYE AND RONNIE SHIELDS

```
                  TRIBUTE TO JOKAYE AND RONNIE SHIELDS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 24, 2017

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate JoKaye and Ronnie Shields on the very special occasion of 
their 60th wedding anniversary. The couple married on September 22, 
1957 and resides in Mount Ayr, Iowa.
  JoKaye and Ronnie's lifelong commitment to each other and their 
family truly embodies Iowa's values. As they reflect on their 60th 
anniversary, may their commitment grow even stronger, as they continue 
to love, cherish, and honor one another for many years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend JoKaye and Ronnie on their 60 years together 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
