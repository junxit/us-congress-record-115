# EMILY BROWNE WINGED FOOT AWARD

**Speaking:** Rooney, Francis (R000607) [R-FL]

```
                     EMILY BROWNE WINGED FOOT AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. FRANCIS ROONEY

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 7, 2017

  Mr. FRANCIS ROONEY of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Emily Browne on winning the Winged Foot Scholar-Athlete 
Award.
   Due to her hard work and dedication, Ms. Browne managed to be the 
star athlete of both her basketball and lacrosse teams at Barron 
Collier High School, all while maintaining a stellar grade point 
average. Her successful balancing of athletic and academic activities 
shows maturity and discipline beyond her years.
   I am proud of Emily's achievements on and off the field. The award 
is well deserved.

                          ____________________
```
