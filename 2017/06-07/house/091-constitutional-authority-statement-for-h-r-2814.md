# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2814

**Measures:** H.R. 2814

```
            By Mr. KHANNA:
        H.R. 2814.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        ``Amendment XVI to the Constitution'': The Congress shall 
     have power to lay and collect taxes on incomes, from whatever 
     source derived, without apportionment among the several 
     States, and without regard to any census or enumeration.
```
