# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2795

**Measures:** H.R. 2795

```
            By Ms. BASS:
        H.R. 2795.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8, clause 18 allows Congress to make 
     all laws ``which shall be necessary and proper for carrying 
     into execution'' any of Congress' enumerated powers.
```
