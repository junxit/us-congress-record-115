# IN HONOR OF ARLENE SHERMAN'S 100TH BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION

**Speaking:** Cheney, Liz (C001109) [R-WY]

```
        IN HONOR OF ARLENE SHERMAN'S 100TH BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LIZ CHENEY

                               of wyoming

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 19, 2017

  Ms. CHENEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my congratulations to 
Arlene Sherman on her 100th birthday.
   I join her friends and family in extending my best to her on this

hope she uses this momentous day to do the same.

Arlene on her birthday. May her year be filled with happiness and 
blessings.
```
