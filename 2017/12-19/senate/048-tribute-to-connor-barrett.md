# TRIBUTE TO CONNOR BARRETT

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO CONNOR BARRETT

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize the hard work of 
my Commerce, Science, and Transportation Committee intern Connor 
Barrett. Connor hails from Bend, OR, and is an aspiring law student.

was committed to getting the most out of his internship. I extend my

did for the committee and wish him continued success in the years to
```
