# RULES AND REPORTS SUBMITTED PURSUANT TO THE CONGRESSIONAL REVIEW ACT

```
  RULES AND REPORTS SUBMITTED PURSUANT TO THE CONGRESSIONAL REVIEW ACT

  Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 801(d), executive communications [final rules] 
submitted to the House pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 801(a)(1) during the period 
of June 13, 2016, through January 3, 2017, shall be treated as though 
received on January 30, 2017. Original dates of transmittal, 
numberings, and referrals

to committee of those executive communications remain as indicated in 
the Executive Communication section of the relevant Congressional 
Record.
```
