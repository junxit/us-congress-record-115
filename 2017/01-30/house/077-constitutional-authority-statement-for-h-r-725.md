# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 725

**Measures:** H.R. 725

```
            By Mr. BUCK:
        H.R. 725.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional Authority on which this legislation is 
     based is found in Article I, Section 8, Clause 9; Article 
     III, Section 1, Clause 1, and Article III, Section 2, Clause 
     2 of the Constitution, which grants Congress authority over 
     the federal courts.
```
