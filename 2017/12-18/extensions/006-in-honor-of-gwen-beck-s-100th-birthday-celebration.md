# IN HONOR OF GWEN BECK'S 100TH BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION

```
           IN HONOR OF GWEN BECK'S 100TH BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LIZ CHENEY

                               of wyoming

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 18, 2017

  Ms. CHENEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my congratulations to

  I join her friends and family in extending my best to her on this

state. I hope she uses this momentous day to do the same.

on her birthday. May her year be filled with happiness and blessings.
```
