# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3900

**Measures:** H.R. 3900

```
            By Mr. FLORES:
        H.R. 3900.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8, Clause 1, 3--The Congress shall have 
     the power to provide for the general Welfare of the United 
     States . . . and to regulate commerce with foreign Nations, 
     and among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
```
