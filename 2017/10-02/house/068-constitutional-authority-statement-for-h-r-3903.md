# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3903

**Measures:** H.R. 3903

```
            By Mr. BUDD:
        H.R. 3903.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8, Clause 3, providing the power to 
     ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among the 
     several states.''
```
