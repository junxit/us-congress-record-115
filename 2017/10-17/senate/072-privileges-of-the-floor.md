**Speaking:** Enzi, Michael B. (E000285) [R-WY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 71

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. ENZI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the following 
staff members from my staff and from Senator Sanders' staff be given
```
