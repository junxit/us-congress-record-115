# CONGRATULATING CAMERON RUDY OF FORT ZUMWALT SOUTH HIGH SCHOOL ON WINNING THE MISSOURI CLASS 3 WRESTLING STATE CHAMPIONSHIP IN THE 126 POUND WEIGHT CLASS

```
   CONGRATULATING CAMERON RUDY OF FORT ZUMWALT SOUTH HIGH SCHOOL ON 
 WINNING THE MISSOURI CLASS 3 WRESTLING STATE CHAMPIONSHIP IN THE 126 
                           POUND WEIGHT CLASS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 20, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Cameron Rudy of the Fort Zumwalt South High 
School Bulldogs on winning the Class 3 Wrestling State Championship in 
the 126 pound weight class.
  Cameron and his coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for bringing home the state championship 
to their school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing Cameron Rudy for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
