# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2767

**Measures:** H.R. 2767

```
           By Mrs. MURPHY of Florida:
       H.R. 2767.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 grants Congress the power to regulate 
     commerce among the several states, and to make all laws which 
     shall be necessary and proper for carrying into execution 
     this power.
```
