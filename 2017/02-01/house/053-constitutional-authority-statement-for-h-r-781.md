# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 781

**Measures:** H.R. 781

```
            By Mr. SCALISE:
        H.R. 781.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The First Amendment guarantees both free speech and the 
     free exercise of religion.
       The Free Speech Fairness Act restores these fundamental 
     liberties to churches and nonprofits.
```
