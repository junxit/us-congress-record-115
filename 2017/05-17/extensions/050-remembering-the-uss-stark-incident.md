# REMEMBERING THE USS ``STARK'' INCIDENT

```
                 REMEMBERING THE USS ``STARK'' INCIDENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 17, 2017

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, today marks the 30th 
Anniversary of the tragic attack on the USS Stark during the Iran-Iraq 
War. On May 17, 1987, the USS Stark was on patrol in the Arabian Gulf 
when an Iraqi aircraft fired two missiles at the frigate without 
warning. The attack and resulting fire killed 37 military personnel and 
wounded 21 others. The brave crew fought the fire throughout the night 
and managed to save the ship.
  I would like to recognize the heroism of the service members who 
served on the USS Stark, including Mr. Luther James Padgett of Gaston, 
South Carolina, a member of the HSL-32 Squadron. A proud Navy man, he 
served his country from 1977 to 1995. I am grateful for his admirable 
service.

                          ____________________
```
