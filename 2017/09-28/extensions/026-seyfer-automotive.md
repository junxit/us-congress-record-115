# SEYFER AUTOMOTIVE

```
                           SEYFER AUTOMOTIVE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 28, 2017

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Seyfer Automotive for receiving the City of Wheat Ridge's Business of 
the Year Award for 2017.
  The Business of the Year Award recognizes local businesses who 
demonstrate a commitment to their community, strong management 
practices and are a positive reflection of the City of Wheat Ridge's 
values.
  As noted in their mission and vision statement, ``For over 50 years, 
Seyfer Automotive has provided expert, quality auto repair. Wheat Ridge 
drivers have come to know them for their reputation of fixing problems 
that no other shop can fix. Their mission is to provide their customer 
and their vehicle with the quality service they expect and deserve. The 
Wheat Ridge auto repair team takes pride in their ability to outperform 
local dealerships.''
  I applaud Seyfer Automotive for being the recipient of this well-
deserved honor by the City of Wheat Ridge, and I congratulate them on 
their success.
```
