# CONGRATULATIONS TO KRYSTAL CAYLOR

```
                   CONGRATULATIONS TO KRYSTAL CAYLOR

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILLY LONG

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 28, 2017

  Mr. LONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Krystal Caylor on 
her 2017 gold medal in the 200-yard individual medley race.
  Competing in the Missouri State High School State Activities 
Association swimming championships, Ms. Caylor proudly represented the 
7th district's Kickapoo High School located in Springfield, Missouri. 
Ms. Caylor went on to take the gold and win the 200-yard individual 
medley with a time of 2:03.48.
  Ms. Caylor has now finished her Kickapoo swimming career and intends 
to continue her swimming career at the collegiate level at the 
University of Indianapolis.
  I am honored to recognize Ms. Caylor for her outstanding achievement 
and wish her the best of luck in her future endeavors. On behalf of 
Missouri's 7th Congressional District, I ask all of my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Ms. Caylor on her outstanding performance.

                          ____________________
```
