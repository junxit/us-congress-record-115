# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW, AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 29, 2017, TO MONDAY, OCTOBER 2, 2017

```
HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW, AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 29, 
                    2017, TO MONDAY, OCTOBER 2, 2017

  Mr. McCARTHY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 11:30 a.m. tomorrow; and
```
