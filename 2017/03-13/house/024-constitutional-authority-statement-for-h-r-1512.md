# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1512

**Measures:** H.R. 1512

```
            By Mr. MARCHANT:
        H.R. 1512.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8, Clause 1, related to 
     providing for the general welfare.
       AND;
       Enacted under the authority provided in Article I, Section 
     8, related to Congress' ability to ``(carry) into execution 
     the foregoing powers.''
```
