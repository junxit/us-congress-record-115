# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I move that the Senate proceed to 
executive session to consider Calendar No. 23, Daniel Coats to be 
Director of National Intelligence.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the nomination.
  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Daniel Coats, of 
Indiana, to be Director of National Intelligence.
```
