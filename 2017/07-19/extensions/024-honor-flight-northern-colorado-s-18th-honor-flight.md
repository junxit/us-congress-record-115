# HONOR FLIGHT NORTHERN COLORADO'S 18TH HONOR FLIGHT

**Speaking:** Buck, Ken (B001297) [R-CO]

```

                             HON. KEN BUCK

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 19, 2017

  Mr. BUCK. Mr. Speaker, in honor of America's heroic veterans, the 
Honor Flight Network conducts two annual Honor Flight ceremonies to 
Washington, D.C. to give our nation's heroes a day to visit and reflect 
at their war memorials. On May 7, 2017, Honor Flight Northern Colorado 
held its 18th Honor Flight that gave many of our courageous veterans 
this extraordinary opportunity. I am pleased to recognize the Honor 
Flight on May 7, 2017, honoring World War II, Korean War, and Vietnam 
War veterans of Northern Colorado.
```
