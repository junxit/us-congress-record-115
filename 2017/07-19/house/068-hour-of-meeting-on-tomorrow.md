# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                      HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW

  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 9 a.m. 
tomorrow.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  July 19, 2017, on page H6030, the following appeared: adjourns 
to meet at 9 a.m. tomorrow.
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: adjourn to meet 
at 9 a.m. tomorrow.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Dunn). Is there objection to the request 
of the gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
```
