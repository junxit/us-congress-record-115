# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4444

**Measures:** H.R. 4444

```
            By Mr. NEAL:
        H.R. 4444.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     in the power of Congress to lay and collect taxes as 
     enumerated in Article I, Section 8.
```
