**Measures:** H.R. 4446

```

       H.R. 4446.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Under Article IV Section 3, Congress shall have Power to 
     dispose and make all needful Rules and Regulations respecting 
     the Territory or other Property belonging to the United 
     States.
```
