# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4436

**Measures:** H.R. 4436

```
           By Miss GONZALEZ-COLON of Puerto Rico:
       H.R. 4436.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. I, Sec. 1 of the Constitution of the United States of 
     America All legislative Powers herein granted shall be vested 
     in a Congress of the United States, which shall consist of a 
     Senate and House of Representatives.
```
