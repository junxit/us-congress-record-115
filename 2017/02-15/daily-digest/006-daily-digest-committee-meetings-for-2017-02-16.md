# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 2017-02-16

                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR THURSDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)

  Committee on Armed Services: to hold hearings to examine reshaping
