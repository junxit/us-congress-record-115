# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Beatty, Joyce (B001281) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.Res. 99, H.Res. 116, H.R. 428

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOYCE BEATTY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 15, 2017

  Mrs. BEATTY. Mr. Speaker, on February 7, 2017, I did not cast my vote 
on roll call votes No. 81 and No. 82. Had I been present, I would have 
voted ``NAY'' on both votes.
  On February 14, 2017, I was unavoidably absent due to illness and 
unable to cast my votes on roll call votes Nos. 88 through 92. Had I 
been present, I would have voted as follows: Roll call vote 88 on 
ordering the previous question; NAY; Roll call vote 89 on agreeing to 
the Resolution, H. Res. 99; NAY; Roll call vote 90 on ordering the 
previous question: NAY; Roll call vote 91 on agreeing to the 
Resolution, H. Res. 116: NAY; Roll call vote 92 on final passage of 
H.R. 428, the Red River Gradient Boundary Survey Act; NAY.
```
