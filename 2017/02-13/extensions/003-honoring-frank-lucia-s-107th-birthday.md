# HONORING FRANK LUCIA'S 107TH BIRTHDAY

```
                 HONORING FRANK LUCIA'S 107TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOSH GOTTHEIMER

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, February 13, 2017

  Mr. GOTTHEIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to wish a very happy 
birthday to Frank Lucia, a treasured member of our North Jersey 
community. Frank recently celebrated his 107th birthday, a tremendous 
milestone in a very special life. Frank is an outstanding father and a 
true blessing to our Demarest community. I am sure he and his family 
had a wonderful time celebrating, dancing, and singing in Italian. Mr. 
Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Frank and wishing 
him many more years of good health and happiness.

                          ____________________
```
