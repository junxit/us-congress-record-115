# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S. 84

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, the Speaker 
signed the following enrolled bill on Thursday, January 19, 2017:
  S. 84, to provide for an exception to a limitation against
```
