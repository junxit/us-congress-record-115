# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4311

**Measures:** H.R. 4311

```
            By Mr. PITTENGER:
        H.R. 4311.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests the 
     power of Congress to lay and collect duties and to regulate 
     Commerce with foreign Nations, as enumerated in Article I, 
     Section 8.
```
