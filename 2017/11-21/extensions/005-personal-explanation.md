**Speaking:** Cuellar, Henry (C001063) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2201, H.R. 3043

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 21, 2017

  Mr. CUELLAR. Mr. Speaker, on Wednesday, November 8, 2017, I requested 
and was granted a leave of absence for the rest of the week to visit 
the community of Sutherland Springs following the recent shooting.
  For the information of our colleagues and my constituents, below is 
how I would have voted on the following votes I missed during this time 
period.

  NAY on Roll Call 617, on rule providing for consideration of H.R. 
2201, Micro Offering Safe Harbor Act;
  YEA on Roll Call 618, on passage of the Veterans Crisis Line Study 
Act of 2017;
  YEA on Roll Call 619, on the passage of the Rush Amendment;
  YEA on Roll Call 620, on passage of H.R. 3043, Hydropower Policy 
Modernization Act of 2017;
  YEA on Roll Call 621, on passage of Veterans Fair Debt Notice Act of 
2016; and
  NAY on Roll Call 622, on passage of H.R. 2201, Micro Offering Safe 
Harbor Act.
```
