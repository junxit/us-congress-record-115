# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4494

**Measures:** H.R. 4494

```
            By Mr. DESANTIS:
        H.R. 4494.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 5, Clause 2 (``Each House may determine 
     the Rules of its Proceedings, punish its Members for 
     disorderly Behaviour, and, with the Concurrence of two 
     thirds, expel a Member.'').
```
