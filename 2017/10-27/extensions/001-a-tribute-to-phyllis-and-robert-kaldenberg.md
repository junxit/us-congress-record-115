# A TRIBUTE TO PHYLLIS AND ROBERT KALDENBERG

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
               A TRIBUTE TO PHYLLIS AND ROBERT KALDENBERG

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 27, 2017

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Phyllis and Robert Kaldenberg, of Winterset, Iowa for 
receiving Madison County Chamber of Commerce's Lifetime Achievement 
Award.
  Phyllis has been a popular daycare provider in the city of Winterset, 
and is active with the local United Methodist Church. She worked to get 
the Iowa Quilt Museum off the ground and is a member of the Covered 
Bridge Quilters. Robert, a member of the Winterset Rotary, participates 
in community volunteering opportunities as much as he can, including 
City Park hedge maze, the Bluebird house projects, and construction of 
the community bocce ball court. Robert is also active in the local 
Methodist Church and has been a big help in the construction of the 
Winterset Area Skate Park.
  Mr. Speaker, I'm proud to represent community leaders like Phyllis 
and Robert in the United States Congress and it is with great pride 
that I recognize them today. I ask that all of my colleagues in the 
United States House of Representatives join me in congratulating 
Phyllis and Robert for their outstanding achievements and in wishing 
them both nothing but continued success.

                          ____________________
```
