# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 1, 2017

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                     ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 1, 2017

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
May 1; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour 
be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and morning business be closed; finally, that following leader remarks, 
the Senate proceed to executive session and resume consideration of the 
Clayton nomination as under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
