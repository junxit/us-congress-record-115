# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2242

**Measures:** H.R. 2242

```
           By Mr. HUFFMAN:
       H.R. 2242.

[[Page H2973]]

       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 4, Section 3, Clause 2: The Congress shall have 
     Power to dispose of and make all needful Rules and 
     Regulations respecting the Territory or other Property 
     belonging to the United States; and nothing in this 
     Constitution shall be so construed as to Prejudice any Claims 
     of the United States, or of any particular State. [Page H775]
```
