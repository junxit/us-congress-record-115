# JIM NATHAN OF LEE HEALTH

```
                        JIM NATHAN OF LEE HEALTH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. FRANCIS ROONEY

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 28, 2017

  Mr. FRANCIS ROONEY of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of 
Jim Nathan, a leader in Southwest Florida who has helped define our 
community-centered health system.
  Forty-two years ago Jim Nathan interviewed at Lee Memorial Hospital. 
He expected to complete a one-year administrative residency and then 
return back to Cincinnati. However, after more than four decades later, 
he is still in Fort Myers serving the healthcare needs of our 
community.
  I thank Jim Nathan for his decades of service and success to 
Southwest Florida, and I wish him well in his endeavors. I know Lee 
Health will continue to be caring people, inspiring health.

                          ____________________
```
