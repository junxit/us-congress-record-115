# RECOGNIZING THREE TEXANS ON VETERANS DAY

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

```
                RECOGNIZING THREE TEXANS ON VETERANS DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE SESSIONS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 31, 2017

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, in honor of Veterans Day, I rise today to 
recognize three World War II veterans who have honorably and faithfully 
served their country.
   Milledge A. ``Mitch'' Hart, III, graduate of the US Naval Academy 
Class of 1956, served for 5 years in the U.S. Marine Corps. His final 
rank was 1st Lieutenant, selected for Captain, USMC.
   William ``Bill'' James Sloan, graduate of SMU Class of 1949, served 
in the U.S. Navy from October 1942 to May 1946.
   Raymond Earl Sloan, graduate of SMU Class of 1949, served in the US 
Army from 1944 to 1946.
   These gentlemen served with honor and distinction and I thank them 
for their service.

                          ____________________
```
