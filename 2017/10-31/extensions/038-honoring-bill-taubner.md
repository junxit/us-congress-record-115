# HONORING BILL TAUBNER

**Speaking:** Engel, Eliot L. (E000179) [D-NY]

```
                         HONORING BILL TAUBNER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ELIOT L. ENGEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 31, 2017

  Mr. ENGEL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a pillar of Pelham 
Manor, Bill Taubner, who this year is being honored by the Pelham Civic 
Association at their annual Dinner Dance Gala for his dedication to the 
community.
  A lifelong resident of Pelham Manor and a member of the Pelham Civic 
Association, Bill is an exemplary member of the community. He is 
extremely altruistic and benevolent to charities, individuals, and 
families in need. Bill has a reputation for being very dependable, 
especially when it matters most.
  An Executive Vice President of Ball Chain Manufacturing, he has been 
a member of the Pelham Manor Planning Board, has served as Chair of the 
board, Director and Coach of Pelham Little League, Director and Coach 
of Pelham Ice Hockey, and as a Pelham Soccer Coach. Bill has also been 
integral in the Fundraising Committees of Siwanoy Elementary School, 
Pelham Middle School, and Pelham High School, demonstrating his 
commitment to education and improving the lives of students in the 
neighborhood.
  The Pelham Civic Association has made a fine choice this year in 
honoring Bill at their annual Dinner Dance Gala. I too would like to 
honor, and congratulate him on this well-deserved recognition, and 
thank him for all of his contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
