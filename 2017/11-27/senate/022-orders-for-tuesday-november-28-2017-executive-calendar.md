# Orders for Tuesday, November 28, 2017 (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                 Orders for Tuesday, November 28, 2017

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 12 noon, Tuesday, 
November 28; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; finally, that following leader 
remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session and resume 
consideration of the Katsas nomination, then subsequently recess until 
4 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
