# TRIBUTE TO JACOB HENRY

```
                         TRIBUTE TO JACOB HENRY

● Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Jacob Henry for his hard work 
as an intern in my Casper office. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Jacob is a native of Casper, WY, and is a second-year student at 
Casper College. He served in the Marine Corps and has received two 
Purple Hearts. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made 
him an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is 
reflected in his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Jacob for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
