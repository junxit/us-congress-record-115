# SWEARING IN OF MEMBER-ELECT

```
                      SWEARING IN OF MEMBER-ELECT

  The SPEAKER. Will the Representative-elect please present himself in 
the well.
  Mr. Schrader of Oregon appeared at the bar of the House and took the 
oath of office, as follows:

       Do you solemnly swear that you will support and defend the 
     Constitution of the United States against all enemies, 
     foreign and domestic; that you will bear true faith and 
     allegiance to the same; that you take

[[Page 512]]

     this obligation freely, without any mental reservation or 
     purpose of evasion; and that you will well and faithfully 
     discharge the duties of the office on which you are about to 
     enter, so help you God.

  The SPEAKER. Congratulations. You are now a Member of the 115th 
Congress.

                          ____________________
```
