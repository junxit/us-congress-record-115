# ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 23, 2017, TO MONDAY, JUNE 26, 2017

**Speaking:** Curbelo, Carlos (C001107) [R-FL]

```
    ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 23, 2017, TO MONDAY, JUNE 26, 2017

  Mr. CURBELO of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet on Monday, June 26, 
2017, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour debate and 2 p.m. 
for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```
