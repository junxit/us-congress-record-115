# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 13, 2017

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2810

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 13, 2017

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Wednesday, September 13; further, that following the prayer and pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and morning business be closed; finally, that 
following leader remarks, and notwithstanding the provisions of rule 
XXII, the Senate resume consideration of the motion to proceed to H.R. 
2810 with no postcloture time remaining.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
