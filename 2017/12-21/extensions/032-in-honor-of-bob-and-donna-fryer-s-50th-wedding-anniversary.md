# IN HONOR OF BOB AND DONNA FRYER'S 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

```
       IN HONOR OF BOB AND DONNA FRYER'S 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LIZ CHENEY

                               of wyoming

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 21, 2017

  Ms. CHENEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my congratulations to 
Bob and Donna Fryer on their 50th Wedding Anniversary.

in extending my best to them on this special occasion.
```
