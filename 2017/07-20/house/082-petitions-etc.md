# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 14

```

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       59. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Lieutenant Governor 
     Byron Mallott, Alaska, relative to House Joint Resolution 14,

     convention of the states to propose amendments to the 
     Constitution of the United States; which was referred to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
```
