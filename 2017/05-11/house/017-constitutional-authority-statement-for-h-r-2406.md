# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2406

**Measures:** H.R. 2406

```
           By Mr. GOODLATTE:
       H.R. 2406.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 4 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution--The 
     Congress shall have Power to establish a uniform Rule of 
     Naturalization, and uniform Laws on the subject of 
     Bankruptcies throughout the United States.
```
