# SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE KAREN C. HANDEL, OF GEORGIA, AS A MEMBER OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Lewis, John (L000287) [D-GA]

```
 SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE KAREN C. HANDEL, OF GEORGIA, AS A MEMBER 
                              OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. LEWIS of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
gentlewoman from Georgia, the Honorable Karen C. Handel, be permitted
```
