```
                        HURRICANE IRMA RECOVERY

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, due to ongoing efforts from 
Hurricane Irma, which devastated many parts of Florida, I am staying in 
my state to assess the damage and help marshal the full capacity of 
recovery resources available to us. Today I met with U.S. Department of 
Agriculture Secretary Sonny Perdue and personally showed him various 
parts of Florida's agriculture lands that were damaged by Hurricane 
Irma.●
```
