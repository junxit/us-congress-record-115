# WISHING A HAPPY 100TH BIRTHDAY TO MS. PEARL GENEVIEVE MOORE

**Speaking:** Reichert, David G. (R000578) [R-WA]

```
      WISHING A HAPPY 100TH BIRTHDAY TO MS. PEARL GENEVIEVE MOORE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. REICHERT

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 18, 2017

  Mr. REICHERT. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Ms. Pearl Genevieve 
Moore of Preston, WA on her 100th birthday today.
  Pearl was born in Washington State on September 18, 1917 to parents 
Hulda and Olaf Carlson, who immigrated to Preston, Washington from 
Varmland, Sweden. Pearl graduated from Broadway High School in 1935.
  In 1941, Pearl married the love of her life, Mr. Marshall Moore. They 
were married for 56 beautiful years.
  Pearl credits her wonderful life to God's grace, her ancestors, and a 
busy social life. She is actively involved in her church community and 
many have been blessed by her hospitality and care over the years. 
Pearl also loves to garden and enjoys eating lots of vegetables, 
peanuts, and chocolate.
  Through her big heart and dedication to her community, Pearl has 
continued to make Preston a better place throughout her lifetime. I 
join with her family, friends, and the Eighth District in 
congratulating her on this special occasion and wishing her a wonderful 
birthday

                          ____________________
```
