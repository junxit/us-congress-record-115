# BERT HACK

```
                               BERT HACK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIMI WALTERS

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 18, 2017

  Mrs. MIMI WALTERS of California. Mr. Speaker, on March 24, 1999, 
Laguna Woods, California officially became Orange County's 32nd city.
  A great deal has changed in the last 18 years, but Laguna Woods 
Councilman Bert Hack has remained a constant in our community.
  Since taking office in 1999, Bert served as Mayor five times and sat 
on numerous boards throughout Southern California.
  Bert's deep knowledge of local history and passion for service to 
others has made him a beloved fixture in Laguna Woods.
  Bert's presence on the City Council will surely be missed, but he 
will undoubtedly continue to serve as an inspiration for all who follow 
in his footsteps.
  I wish Bert well in retirement, and thank him for his many years of 
service to Laguna Woods and Orange County.

                          ____________________
```
