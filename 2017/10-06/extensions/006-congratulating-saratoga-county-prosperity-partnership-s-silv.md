# CONGRATULATING SARATOGA COUNTY PROSPERITY PARTNERSHIP'S SILVER EXCELLENCE IN ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AWARD

```
    CONGRATULATING SARATOGA COUNTY PROSPERITY PARTNERSHIP'S SILVER 
                EXCELLENCE IN ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 6, 2017

  Ms. STEFANIK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Saratoga 
County Prosperity Partnership for receiving a Silver Excellence in 
Economic Development award for its 8@8 e-Newsletter.
  Saratoga County Prosperity Partnership is the leading agency in 
advancing economic development in Saratoga County. The Saratoga 
Partnership sends out a biweekly e-Newsletter featuring the top eight 
news items affecting the Saratoga County economy. 8@8 provides 
important information to stakeholders in the community as a way of 
creating opportunities that benefit business in Saratoga County. To 
recognize this important service, the International Economic 
Development Council presented the Silver Excellence award to the 
Saratoga Partnership at its annual conference held in late September. 
This award reflects the Saratoga Partnership's commitment to building a 
business environment where all companies can succeed.
  On behalf of New York's 21st District, I want to congratulate the 
Saratoga County Prosperity Partnership on its well-deserved award. We 
are grateful that the Saratoga Partnership can provide this informative 

e-Newsletter to the people of Saratoga County.

                          ____________________
```
