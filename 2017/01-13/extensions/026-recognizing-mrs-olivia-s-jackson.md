# RECOGNIZING MRS. OLIVIA S. JACKSON

**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

```
                   RECOGNIZING MRS. OLIVIA S. JACKSON

                                  _____

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 13, 2017

2016. Mrs. Jackson celebrated her birthday on January 7th in Chicago
```
