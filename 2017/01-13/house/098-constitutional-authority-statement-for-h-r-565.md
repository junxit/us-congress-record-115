# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 565

**Measures:** H.R. 565

```
            By Mr. ROHRABACHER:
        H.R. 565.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 4 of the United States 
     Constitution, which gives Congress the power ``To establish 
     an uniform Rule of Naturalization, and uniform Laws on the 
     subject of Bankruptcies throughout the United States.''
```
