# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 545

**Measures:** H.R. 545

```
            By Mr. CRAMER:
        H.R. 545.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign 
     Nations in Article I, Section 8 of the United States 
     Constitution, specifically Clause 3.
```
