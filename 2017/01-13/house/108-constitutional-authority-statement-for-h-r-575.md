**Measures:** H.R. 575

```

        H.R. 575.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3:
       Congress shall have the Power to regulate Commerce with 
     foreign Nations, and among several States, and with the 
     Indian Tribes.
```
