# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3821

**Measures:** H.R. 3821

```
            By Mr. COLLINS of Georgia:
        H.R. 3821.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the United States 
     Constitution, Congress is empowered ``To establish Post 
     Offices and post roads''.
```
