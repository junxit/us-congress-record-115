# TAX REFORM BILL

```
                            TAX REFORM BILL

  Mr. ISAKSON. Mr. President, it is a historic day for our country, for 
the Senate, and for the Congress.
  As we speak, the President of the United States is about to sign the 
bill that we passed on the floor of the Senate last night, which was 
the agreement on the conference report--the largest tax reform in the 
history of our

[[Page 20328]]

country or, certainly, the largest since 1986. It is historic in many 
other ways because we are fighting wars overseas, we are dealing with 
terrorism, and we are looking at the economic climate for the future 
and trying to inspire our country to be better and be everything that 
it can be. We are talking about all of those types of things, and we 
are getting ready for Christmas.
```
