# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2461

**Measures:** H.R. 2461

```
            By Mr. LANCE:
        H.R. 2461.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 of the Constitution, which 
     states that the Congress has the power to regulate Commerce 
     with foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with 
     the Indian Tribes.
```
