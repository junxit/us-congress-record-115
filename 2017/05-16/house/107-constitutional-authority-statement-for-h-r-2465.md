# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2465

**Measures:** H.R. 2465

```
            By Mrs. McMORRIS RODGERS:
        H.R. 2465.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The power of the Congress to regulate Commerce as 
     enumerated by Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 as applied to 
     the Social Security Act.
```
