# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 28, 2017

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 28, 2017

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 12 noon, 
Wednesday, June 28; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time

[[Page 9936]]

for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and 
morning business be closed; finally, that following leader remarks, the 
Senate proceed to executive session and resume consideration of the Rao 
nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
