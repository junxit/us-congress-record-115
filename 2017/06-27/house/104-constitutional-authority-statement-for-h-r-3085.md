# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3085

**Measures:** H.R. 3085

```
            By Mr. PEARCE:
        H.R. 3085.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article 1, clause 8, section 6, Congress in empowered 
     ``to coin Money, regulate the Value therof, and of foriegn 
     Coin, and fix the Standard or Wieghts and Measures''
```
