# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1228

**Measures:** H.R. 1228

```
           By Mr. HARPER:
       H.R. 1228.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution, which grants 
     Congress the authority to make laws governing the commerce 
     among several states, including employment discrimination 
     laws.
```
