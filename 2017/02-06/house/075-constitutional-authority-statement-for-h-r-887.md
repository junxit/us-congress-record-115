# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 887

**Measures:** H.R. 887

```
            By Mr. JONES:
        H.R. 887.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8 of the United States Constitution 
     gives Congress the authority to ``establish an uniform rule 
     of naturalization'' and to ``make rules for the government 
     and regulation of the land and naval forces''.
```
