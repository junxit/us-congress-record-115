# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 890

**Measures:** H.R. 890

```
            By Mr. MARINO:
        H.R. 890.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 8: To promote the Progress of 
     Science and useful Arts, by security for limited Times to 
     Authors and Inventors the exclusive Right to their respective 
     Writings and Discoveries.
```
