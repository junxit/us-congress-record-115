# ANNOUNCEMENT BY COMMITTEE ON RULES REGARDING AMENDMENT PROCESS FOR H.R.

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 806, H.R. 2997

```
ANNOUNCEMENT BY COMMITTEE ON RULES REGARDING AMENDMENT PROCESS FOR H.R. 
 806, OZONE STANDARDS IMPLEMENTATION ACT OF 2017, AND H.R. 2997, 21ST 
      CENTURY AVIATION INNOVATION, REFORM, AND REAUTHORIZATION ACT

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, this morning the Rules Committee issued 
announcements outlining the amendment processes for two measures that 
will likely be before the Rules Committee next week.
  An amendment deadline has been set for Monday, July 17, at 10 a.m., 
for H.R. 806, the Ozone Standards Implementation Act of 2017; and 
Monday, July 17, at noon, for H.R. 2997, the 21st Century AIRR Act.
  The text of these measures is presently available on the Rules 
Committee website.
  Feel free to contact me or my staff if we may provide any additional 
information.

                          ____________________
```
