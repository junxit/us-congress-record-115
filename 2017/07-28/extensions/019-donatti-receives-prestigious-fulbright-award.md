# DONATTI RECEIVES PRESTIGIOUS FULBRIGHT AWARD

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
              DONATTI RECEIVES PRESTIGIOUS FULBRIGHT AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 28, 2017

  Mr. OLSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Michael Donatti 
of Pearland, TX, for receiving a Fulbright award to work on his Master 
of Science degree at the University of Bristol in the UK.
  Michael is a Rice University student studying environmental policy 
and management. His environmental policy program will cover corporate 
environmental sustainability, legal and economic frameworks and climate 
science, and will require him to write a thesis. Each year the 
Fulbright Program grants students the opportunity to study, research or 
teach English abroad in an effort to internationalize communities and 
campuses around the world. Fulbright scholars focus on the conditions 
and challenges differing regions face, as well as building valuable 
U.S. relationships.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, 
congratulations again to Michael for receiving this Fulbright award. 
Keep up the great work.

                          ____________________
```
