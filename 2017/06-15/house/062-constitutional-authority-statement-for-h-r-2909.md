# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2909

**Measures:** H.R. 2909

```
            By Mr. MASSIE:
        H.R. 2909.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Second Amendment to the United States Constitution, and 
     Article 1, Section 8, which gives Congress the authority to 
     legislate for the District of Columbia.
```
