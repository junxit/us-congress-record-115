          HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES--Tuesday, December 26, 2017

  The House met at 4:30 p.m. and was called to order by the Speaker pro 
tempore (Mr. McHenry).

                 DESIGNATION OF THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                December 26, 2017.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Patrick T. McHenry to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore on this day.
                                                     Paul D. Ryan,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives.

                          ____________________



                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Paul D. Holland, S.J., Jesuit Conference, Washington, D.C., 
offered the following prayer:
  Gracious God, we acknowledge You as Lord of all nations, creator of 
land, and sea and sky, source of the wonders of nature and of all 
goodness.
  You have entrusted creation to our faithful stewardship, yet You 
alone sustain all Your creation through the life-giving breath of Your 
spirit.
  In Your wisdom, You arranged the changing of times and seasons so 
that we might marvel at Your love, reflected in the vastness of the 
universe and the bounty of this Earth.
  As we near the end of this year, we pray Your blessings upon the 
Members of this Congress throughout the coming new year, and ask You to 
keep them always in Your ways of truth and faithful service.
  We pray this in Your Holy Name.
  Amen.

                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 4 of rule I, the 
following enrolled bills were signed by Speaker pro tempore Upton on 
Friday, December 22, 2017:
  H.R. 267, to redesignate the Martin Luther King, Junior, National 
Historic Site in the State of Georgia, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 381, to designate a mountain in the John Muir Wilderness of the 
Sierra National Forest as ``Sky Point'';
  H.R. 518, to amend the Energy Policy and Conservation Act to exclude 
power supply circuits, drivers, and devices designed to be connected 
to, and power, light-emitting diodes or organic light-emitting diodes 
providing illumination from energy conservation standards for external 
power supplies, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 560, to amend Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area 
Improvement Act to provide access to certain vehicles serving residents 
of municipalities adjacent to the Delaware Water Gap National 
Recreation Area, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 699, to amend the Omnibus Public Land Management Act of 2009 to 
modify provisions relating to certain land exchanges in the Mt. Hood 
Wilderness in the State of Oregon;
  H.R. 863, to facilitate the addition of park administration at the 
Coltsville National Historical Park, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 954, to remove the use restrictions on certain land transferred 
to Rockingham County, Virginia, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 1242, to establish the 400 Years of African-American History 
Commission, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 1306, to provide for the conveyance of certain Federal land in 
the State of Oregon, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 1927, to amend title 54, United States Code, to establish within 
the National Park Service the African American Civil Rights Network, 
and for other purposes;
  H.R. 2142, to improve the ability of U.S. Customs and Border 
Protection to interdict fentanyl, other synthetic opioids, and other 
narcotics and psychoactive substances that are illegally imported into 
the United States, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 2228, to provide support for law enforcement agency efforts to 
protect mental health and well-being of law enforcement officers, and 
for other purposes;
  H.R. 2331, to require a new or updated Federal website that is 
intended for use by the public to be mobile friendly, and for other 
purposes;
  H.R. 2611, to modify the boundary of the Little Rock Central High 
School National Historic Site, and for other purposes;
  H.R. 4661, to reauthorize the United States Fire Administration, the 
Assistance to Firefighters Grants program, the Fire Prevention and 
Safety Grants program, and the Staffing for Adequate Fire Emergency 
Response grant program, and for other purposes;
  S. 1393, to streamline the process by which active duty military, 
reservists, and veterans receive commercial driver's licenses;
  S. 1532, to disqualify from operating a commercial motor vehicle for 
life an individual who uses a commercial motor vehicle in committing a 
felony involving human trafficking;
  S. 1766, to reauthorize the SAFER Act of 2013, and for other 
purposes.
