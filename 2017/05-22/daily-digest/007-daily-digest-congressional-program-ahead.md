                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                  Week of May 23 through May 26, 2017

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will resume consideration of the nomination of 
John J. Sullivan, of Maryland, to be Deputy Secretary of State, and 
vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination at approximately 
11 a.m.
  Upon disposition of the nomination of John J. Sullivan, of Maryland, 
to be Deputy Secretary of State, Senate will vote on the motion to 
invoke cloture on the nomination of Amul R. Thapar, of Kentucky, to be 
United States Circuit Judge for the Sixth Circuit.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
