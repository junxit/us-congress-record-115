# HONORING DR. L. FRANCES P. LIDDELL

```
                   HONORING DR. L. FRANCES P. LIDDELL

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 24, 2017

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. 
L. Frances P. Liddell's dedicated commitment of service to her 
community and her services to the conference of Minority Public 
Administrators (COMPA) and the National Planning Committee.
  Dr. L. Frances P. Liddell was awarded by the renaming of the American 
Society for Public Administration (ASPA), in her honor, to the Dr. L. 
Frances P. Liddell Student Policy Debate.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Dr. L. 
Frances P. Liddell.

                          ____________________
```
