# HONORING NORTHEASTERN HIGH SCHOOL KNIGHTS BOYS VARSITY BASKETBALL TEAM

```
 HONORING NORTHEASTERN HIGH SCHOOL KNIGHTS BOYS VARSITY BASKETBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. LUKE MESSER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 7, 2017

  Mr. MESSER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Northeastern High 
School on its 2017 IHSAA Class 2A Sectional 41 championship in boys' 
basketball.
   The Knights faced off against the Union County Patriots, defeating 
them 54-41.
   I am proud of these young men for not only their remarkable win, but 
also for the Hoosier sportsmanship that they displayed throughout this 
exciting season. I want to commend Coach Brent Ross as well as all of 
the assistant coaches who led these young men to victory.
   Congrats, Knights.

                          ____________________
```
