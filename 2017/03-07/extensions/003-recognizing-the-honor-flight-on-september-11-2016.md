# RECOGNIZING THE HONOR FLIGHT ON SEPTEMBER 11, 2016

**Speaking:** Buck, Ken (B001297) [R-CO]

```
           RECOGNIZING THE HONOR FLIGHT ON SEPTEMBER 11, 2016

                             HON. KEN BUCK

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 7, 2017

  Mr. BUCK. Mr. Speaker, in honor of America's heroic veterans, the 
Honor Flight Network conducts two annual Honor Flight ceremonies to 
Washington, D.C. to give our nation's heroes a day to visit and reflect 
at their war memorials. On September 11th of last year, Honor Flight 
Northern Colorado held its 17th Honor Flight that gave many of our 
courageous veterans this extraordinary opportunity. I am pleased to 
recognize the Honor Flight on September 11, 2016, honoring World War 
II, Korean War, and Vietnam War veterans of Northern Colorado.
```
