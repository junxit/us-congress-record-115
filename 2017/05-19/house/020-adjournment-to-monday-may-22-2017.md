# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 22, 2017

**Speaking:** McCarthy, Kevin (M001165) [R-CA]

```
                  ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, MAY 22, 2017

House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday next and 
that the order of the House of January 3, 2017, regarding morning-hour 
debate not apply on that day.
```
