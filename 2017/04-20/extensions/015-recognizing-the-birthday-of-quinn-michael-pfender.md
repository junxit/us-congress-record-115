# RECOGNIZING THE BIRTHDAY OF QUINN MICHAEL PFENDER

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
           RECOGNIZING THE BIRTHDAY OF QUINN MICHAEL PFENDER

                                  _____
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 20, 2017

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Quinn Michael 
Pfender, on the day of his Twenty-Second birthday. Quinn is a shining 
example of a young man, whose characteristics of hard work, 
determination, and faith exemplify everything we hope to see in our 
future generations. It is my privilege to recognize him on the Floor of 
the House of Representatives in our nation's capital.
```
