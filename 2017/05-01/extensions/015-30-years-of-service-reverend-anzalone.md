# 30 YEARS OF SERVICE--REVEREND ANZALONE

**Speaking:** Hartzler, Vicky (H001053) [R-MO]

```
                 30 YEARS OF SERVICE--REVEREND ANZALONE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. VICKY HARTZLER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 1, 2017

  Mrs. HARTZLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Reverend Alan 
Anzalone, born in Kansas City and raised in Deepwater, Missouri. Alan 
has dedicated 30 years of service to Brownington Baptist Church.
  From an early age, Alan committed himself to bettering his community, 
helping those in need and setting an example for his peers through 
excelling in academics, athletics and school spirit. At age 17, Alan 
answered his calling from God and became Pastor of his church. Not only 
has he led followers to the Faith but he has also guided the renovation 
of the Church over the years to better suit the community's needs. I am 
honored to share my gratitude and respect for Reverend Alan Anzalone, 
and his generosity and service are an inspiration to us all.

                          ____________________
```
