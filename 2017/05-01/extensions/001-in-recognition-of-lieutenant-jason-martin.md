# IN RECOGNITION OF LIEUTENANT JASON MARTIN

**Speaking:** Meehan, Patrick (M001181) [R-PA]

```
               IN RECOGNITION OF LIEUTENANT JASON MARTIN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. PATRICK MEEHAN

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 1, 2017

  Mr. MEEHAN. Mr. Speaker, today I honor Lieutenant Jason Martin of the 
Springfield Township Ambulance Corps. Lieutenant Martin grew up around 
the ambulance corps--often acting as a patient in training exercises 
when he was young. He has demonstrated his devotion for protecting his 
community through his work.
   As a pillar of Springfield Township, Lieutenant Martin has rightly 
been nominated as EMT of the Year as part of the 25th Annual 
Springfield Loyalty Day.
   I thank Lieutenant Martin for his service.

                          ____________________
```
