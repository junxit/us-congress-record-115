# RECOGNIZING THE MAHOMET-SEYMOUR BOYS CROSS COUNTRY TEAM

**Speaking:** Shimkus, John (S000364) [R-IL]

```
        RECOGNIZING THE MAHOMET-SEYMOUR BOYS CROSS COUNTRY TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN SHIMKUS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 28, 2017

  Mr. SHIMKUS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the Mahomet-Seymour 
High School Boys Cross Country Team, who are the 2017 Class 2A Illinois 
team cross country champions.
  Mahomet-Seymour won the meet with 91 points, easily outdistancing its 
nearest competitor, as its three top-ten finishers, Mathias Powell 
(4th), Riley Fortune (6th), and Ryan Hodge (9th), powered the team to 
victory. But this was a team effort and a team victory as the 
outstanding efforts by Bryson Keeble, Evan Burge, Kaelan Davis, and 
Nate Douglas also contributed to the win.
  I would like to congratulate the entire Mahomet-Seymour Boys Cross 
Country Team on their victory: Evan Burge, Joe Churm, Riley Fortune, 
Ryan Hodge, Kyle Sheehy, Garrett Williams, Kaelan Davis, Nate Douglas, 
Bryson Keeble, Keil Ledin, Caleb Mason, Jack McHale, Mathias Powell, 
David Wilcoski,

[[Page E1603]]

Gaven Williams, Caleb Dowers, and Joe Taylor, as well as Head Coach 
Neal Garrison, on a superb end to a great season.
  Mr. Speaker, it is an honor for me to acknowledge the hard work and 
dedication of the Mahomet-Seymour High School Boys Cross Country Team 
in winning the 2017 state cross country title, and I wish the team and 
their coach all the best in the future.

                          ____________________
```
