# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 443

**Measures:** H.R. 443

```
            By Mr. DesJARLAIS:
        H.R. 443.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 2 of Section 3 of Article IV of the U.S. 
     Constitution: The Congress shall have the power to dispose of 
     and make all needful rules and regulations respecting the 
     territory or other property belonging to the United States
```
