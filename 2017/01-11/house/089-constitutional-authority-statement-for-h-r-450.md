# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 450

**Measures:** H.R. 450

```
            By Mr. JONES:
        H.R. 450.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution 
     (clauses 12, 13, 14, and 16), which grats Congress the power 
     to raise and support an Army; to provide and maintain a Navy; 
     to to make rules for the government and regulation of the 
     land and naval forces; and to provide for organizing, arming, 
     and disciplining the militia.
```
