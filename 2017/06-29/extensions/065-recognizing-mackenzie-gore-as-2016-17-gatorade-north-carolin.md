# RECOGNIZING MACKENZIE GORE AS 2016-17 GATORADE NORTH CAROLINA BASEBALL PLAYER OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** Rouzer, David (R000603) [R-NC]

```
RECOGNIZING MACKENZIE GORE AS 2016-17 GATORADE NORTH CAROLINA BASEBALL 
                           PLAYER OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DAVID ROUZER

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 29, 2017

  Mr. ROUZER. Mr. Speaker, I'm proud to say that North Carolina is home 
to many great student athletes that serve as community role models.
  One prime example is MacKenzie Gore who graduated from Whiteville 
High School this month and has been named the 2017 Gatorade North 
Carolina Baseball Player of the Year. And, not only that, he was just 
recently selected 3rd overall in the Major League Baseball draft by the 
San Diego Padres!
  MacKenzie not only demonstrates athletic excellence, but also 
exemplary character and work ethic--the two primary traits necessary 
for great success. Very few have achieved as much as early in life, and 
it's the culmination of years of hard work and commitment.
  As with all who earn success, MacKenzie is blessed to have a big 
decision to make: go pro and play for the Padres or head to East 
Carolina University to play for the Pirates. Whatever MacKenzie 
decides, we are all proud of him and wish him the very best.

                          ____________________
```
