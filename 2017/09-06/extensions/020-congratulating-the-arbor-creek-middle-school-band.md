# CONGRATULATING THE ARBOR CREEK MIDDLE SCHOOL BAND

**Speaking:** Marchant, Kenny (M001158) [R-TX]

```
           CONGRATULATING THE ARBOR CREEK MIDDLE SCHOOL BAND

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. KENNY MARCHANT

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 6, 2017

  Mr. MARCHANT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Arbor 
Creek Middle School Band of Lewisville Independent School District on 
being selected to perform in the 71st Annual Midwest Clinic 
International Band and Orchestra Conference in Chicago, Illinois.
  The Arbor Creek Band, directed by Mr. Rylon Guidry, has been 
recognized as one of the premiere middle school band programs in the 
state of Texas. Among their numerous honors, Arbor Creek's band has won 
the Foundation for Music Education's Mark of Excellence: National Wind 
Band Honors Contest on three occasions, was first runner-up in the 
2016-2017 Texas Music Educators Association's Honor Band State Contest, 
and receives the University Interscholastic League Sweepstakes Award on 
a yearly basis.
  This December, the Arbor Creek Band will perform at the Midwest 
Clinic in Chicago as one of only four middle school bands across the 
world chosen as an official performance group. This incredible honor is 
a testament to the hard work and dedication of these students.
  The following musicians will represent Arbor Creek Middle School this
```
