# IN RECOGNITION OF BROCK LIVELY

**Speaking:** Schweikert, David (S001183) [R-AZ]

```
                     IN RECOGNITION OF BROCK LIVELY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID SCHWEIKERT

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 6, 2017

  Mr. SCHWEIKERT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge Brock Lively 
who served as an intern in my office from May 16 to July 29, 2016. I 
recently learned of Brock's interest to explore military service after 
graduating from Loyola University in May 2018 with honors in history.
   I speak to Brock's character with enthusiasm for his commitment to 
service. In addition to accomplishing the leadership of an Eagle Scout, 
Brock has given of himself on many faith-based and humanitarian 
missions around the globe. He carried my appreciation for the time he 
takes to give back to his community and country.

                          ____________________
```
