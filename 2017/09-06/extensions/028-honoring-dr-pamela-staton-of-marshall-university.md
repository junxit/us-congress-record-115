# HONORING DR. PAMELA STATON OF MARSHALL UNIVERSITY

**Speaking:** Jenkins, Evan H. (J000297) [R-WV]

```
           HONORING DR. PAMELA STATON OF MARSHALL UNIVERSITY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. EVAN H. JENKINS

                            of west virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 6, 2017

  Mr. JENKINS of West Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
hard work of Dr. Pamela Staton of Marshall University in Huntington, 
West Virginia. Dr. Staton has played a major role in the success of 
Marshall University's Forensic Science Center, which has repeatedly 
been named the nation's best Forensic Science Program.
  Dr. Pamela Staton joined the Forensic Science Program in 2002, and 
leads the Bacterial Source Tracking Research Project at the Robert C. 
Byrd Biotechnology Center. Her invaluable experience as the Forensic 
Science Center's program administrator and chief academic advisor for 
its graduate students has contributed greatly to the program's 
reputation and plays a central role in its success. Dr. Staton's 
contributions to the Huntington community and West Virginia are 
invaluable, and I wish her the best of luck on her retirement. I know 
she will always be a part of the Marshall University family and look 
forward to seeing all the great things she achieves in the future.

                          ____________________
```
