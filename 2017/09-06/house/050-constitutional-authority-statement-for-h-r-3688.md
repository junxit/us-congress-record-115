# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3688

**Measures:** H.R. 3688

```
            By Mr. KING of Iowa:
        H.R. 3688.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution provides Congress 
     with the authority to regulate commerce with the Indian 
     Tribes in the United States.
```
