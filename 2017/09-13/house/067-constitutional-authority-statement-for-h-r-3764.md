# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3764

**Measures:** H.R. 3764

```
           By Mr. GIANFORTE:
       H.R. 3764.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 Article I, section 8 of the 
     Constitution of the United States that states ``The Congress 
     shall have Power to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, 
     and among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
```
