# HONORING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF VICKIE HALE

```
               HONORING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF VICKIE HALE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRAD R. WENSTRUP

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 13, 2017

  Mr. WENSTRUP. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to wish a happy 100th 
birthday to Vickie Hale of New Richmond, OH.
  Through her life, Vickie has been an incredible member of our 
community here in Ohio's Second District. It's people like her who make 
the world a lot nicer.
  A retired school teacher and member of the New Richmond Historical 
Society, Vickie has spent her life dedicated to the life and 
improvement of her community and those around her.
  Her public service and example to other is admired.
  VIckie, as well as her husband John, a World War II veteran, have 
made New Richmond a better place throughout their lifetime, through 
their kindness and dedication to all.
  Happy Birthday Vickie.

                          ____________________
```
