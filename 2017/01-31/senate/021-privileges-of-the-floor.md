# PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

```
                        PRIVILEGES OF THE FLOOR

  Mr. NELSON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Commander Dan 
Hurd, U.S. Coast Guard, a fellow in my office, be granted floor 
privileges for the remainder of the week.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that all time 
during recess or adjournment of the Senate count post-cloture on the 
Tillerson nomination; further, that following the prayer and pledge, 
the remaining post-cloture time be equally divided between the two 
leaders or their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
