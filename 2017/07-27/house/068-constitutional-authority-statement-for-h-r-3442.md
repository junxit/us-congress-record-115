# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3442

**Measures:** H.R. 3442

```
            By Mr. BUDD:
       H.R. 3442.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3, granting authority to 
     regulate interstate commerce, along with Article I, Section 
     8, Clause 18, granting authority to make all laws that are 
     necessary and proper for executing the forgoing.
```
