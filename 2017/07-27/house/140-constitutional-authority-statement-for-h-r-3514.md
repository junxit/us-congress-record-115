# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3514

**Measures:** H.R. 3514

```
            By Mr. SUOZZI:
        H.R. 3514.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution, Congress 
     has the power ``to make all Laws which shall be necessary and 
     proper for carrying into Execution the foregoing Powers, and 
     all other Powers vested by this Consitution in the Govenrment 
     of the United Sates, or any Department of Officer thereof''
```
