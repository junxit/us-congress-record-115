# DISAPPROVING A RULE SUBMITTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF LABOR--MOTION TO PROCEED

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 67

```
  DISAPPROVING A RULE SUBMITTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF LABOR--MOTION TO 
                                PROCEED

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I move to proceed to H.J. Res. 67.
```
