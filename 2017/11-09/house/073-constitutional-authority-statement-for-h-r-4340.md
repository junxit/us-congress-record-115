# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4340

**Measures:** H.R. 4340

```
            By Mr. BRAT:
        H.R. 4340.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        American immigration law stems from Congress' power to 
     ``establish a uniform Rule of Naturalization'' (Article 1, 
     Section 8, Clause 4).
```
