# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 3043, H.R. 3705, H.R. 4173

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 3043. An act to modernize hydropower policy, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources.
       H.R. 3705. An act to direct the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs to require the use of certified mail and plain 
     language in certain debt collection activities; to the 
     Committee on Veterans' Affairs.

     Affairs to conduct a study on the Veterans Crisis Line; to 
     the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
```
