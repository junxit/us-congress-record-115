# TRIBUTE TO SHARON AND HARRY SACKETT

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                  TRIBUTE TO SHARON AND HARRY SACKETT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 19, 2017

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Sharon and Harry Sackett on the very special occasion of 
their 60th wedding anniversary. The couple married on October 12, 1957, 
and resides in Dexter, Iowa.
  Sharon and Harry's lifelong commitment to each other, their two 
children, six grandchildren, twenty great-grandchildren, and two great-
great-grandchildren, truly embodies Iowa's values. As they reflect on 
their 60th anniversary, may their commitment grow even stronger, as 
they continue to love, cherish, and honor one another for many years to 
come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend Sharon and Harry on their 60 years together 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
