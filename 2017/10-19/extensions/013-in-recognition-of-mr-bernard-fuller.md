# IN RECOGNITION OF MR. BERNARD FULLER

```
                  IN RECOGNITION OF MR. BERNARD FULLER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MARK WALKER

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 19, 2017

  Mr. WALKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
constituent--Mr. Bernard Fuller. I am honored to congratulate Mr. 
Bernard Fuller, a teacher at Graham Middle School in Alamance County, 
North Carolina. Mr. Bernard Fuller is a 2016-2017 recipient of the 
Outstanding Special Education Teacher of the Year Award by the National 
Association of Special Education Teachers. This award was presented to 
16 teachers across the nation and Mr. Fuller is the only teacher to 
earn the honor from North Carolina. Mr. Fuller has demonstrated a 
passionate commitment by developing special skills and going the extra 
mile for his remarkable students. He is an exceptional example for his 
colleagues in Graham and teachers everywhere. I join with his friends, 
family, and the Sixth District in congratulating Bernard Fuller on this 
excellent accomplishment.
```
