# CONGRATULATING DR. PAUL COREIL

```
                     CONGRATULATING DR. PAUL COREIL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. GARRET GRAVES

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 19, 2017

  Mr. GRAVES of Louisiana. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
Dr. Paul Coreil. Dr. Paul Coreil, born and raised in Ville Platte, 
Louisiana, has enjoyed a distinguished career with the 4H Program--the 
most recent achievement being his induction into the 4H National Hall 
of Fame. Honored as one of only 16 inducted across the nation, Dr. 
Coreil has played a major role in the program for more than 34 years 
and currently serves as the Director and Vice Chancellor Emeritus for 
the Louisiana State University Agricultural Center. His work on the 
Marsh Maneuvers and the Louisiana 4H Foundation Squirrel Hunt has 
helped raise thousands of dollars to benefit Louisiana 4H programs. Dr. 
Coreil has received numerous awards and accolades for his contributions 
4H and to the great state of Louisiana. I applaud Dr. Coreil for his 
work and commend him on this well-deserved honor.

                          ____________________
```
