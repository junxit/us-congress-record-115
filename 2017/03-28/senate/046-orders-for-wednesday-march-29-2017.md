**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 29, 2017

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Wednesday, March 29; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the
```
