# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3025

**Measures:** H.R. 3025

```
            By Mr. SMITH of Washington:
        H.R. 3025.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        The constitutional authorities on which this bill rests 
     are the powers of Congress to: ``provide for the common 
     Defence'', ``raise and support Armies'', ``provide and 
     maintain a Navy'' and ``make Rules for the Government and 
     Regulation of the land and naval Forces'' as enumerated in 
     Article I, section 8 of the United States Constitution.
```
