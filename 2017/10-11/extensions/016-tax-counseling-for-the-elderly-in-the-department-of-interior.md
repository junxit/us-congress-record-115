# TAX COUNSELING FOR THE ELDERLY IN THE DEPARTMENT OF INTERIOR, ENVIRONMENT, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2018

```
     TAX COUNSELING FOR THE ELDERLY IN THE DEPARTMENT OF INTERIOR, 
       ENVIRONMENT, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2018

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 11, 2017

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Soto amendment, Soto number 27 to Division D, 
the Department of Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies 
Appropriations Act, 2018, increased funding to the Tax Counseling for 
the Elderly Program by $1,000,000. It did so by making an additional 
carveout of money from the overall Taxpayer Services Account.
  I support increased funding for the Tax Counseling for the Elderly 
Program and thank Chairman Graves and my colleagues for their support 
of my amendment.
```
