     farmers to grow more fruits and vegetables--thereby altering 
     the supply of crops, and therefore food prices) (Cf. Wickard 
     v. Filburn).

     Power . . . To make all Laws which shall be necessary and

     (This bill promotes the general welfare of the United States 
     by expanding access to fresh produce in underserved 
     communities, while promoting crop diversity and therefore 
     farmer income).
