# HONORING MARTHA MOORE

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

```
                         HONORING MARTHA MOORE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MARSHA BLACKBURN

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 29, 2017

  Mrs. BLACKBURN. Mr. Speaker, today we honor Martha Moore, multi-
market media force, Nashville PR queen, and go-to expert on independent 
public relations.
  Born in Washington, DC and transplanted to the mid-South via 
University of Tennessee-Knoxville, Ms. Moore kicked off her Nashville 
career in 1976 with WLAC Radio before transitioning to press and artist 
relations with MCA/ABC Records and Mercury/Polygram.
  In 1988, she marshalled her impeccable industry pedigree and vast 
connections, and launched her own independent, boutique PR firm. Known 
for her strategic planning and attention to detail, she wasted no time 
in crashing through the industry glass ceiling by signing Johnny Cash 
as her first client. Her charisma and personal motivation propelled 
invaluable connections with the foreign press. Her creativity and 
dependability fostered strategic partnerships with powerhouse firms. 
Her professional relationships with songwriters and performers generate 
an evolving and eclectic client roster, and are a testament to her 
passion for success in a demanding, but rewarding career.
  We honor Martha Moore with a SOURCE Award, recognizing her vital 
contribution to the success of Music Row and the Nashville 
entertainment industry.

                          ____________________
```
