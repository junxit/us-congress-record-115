# CONGRATULATING CHIEF MARILYN DENISE HENDERSON

```
             CONGRATULATING CHIEF MARILYN DENISE HENDERSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. AL LAWSON, JR.

                               of florida

                  in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 29, 2017

  Mr. LAWSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
Chief Marilyn Denise Henderson on her retirement from the Florida 
Department of Corrections Office of Inspector General as the Assistant 
Chief of Investigation. I want to thank Chief Henderson for giving over 
30 years of public service in law enforcement to the State of Florida. 
With her knowledge, integrity, and leadership she has been able to 
service the people of this great State. I know that the staff at the 
Florida Department of Corrections will miss her immensely.
  Chief Henderson has been an asset to the community, not only in 
public service, but in her commitment to charity fundraisers and the 
faith based community. It is an honor to have people such as her in our 
community working diligently to make it better.
  I want to welcome Chief Henderson to her new role as a retired 
professional. I hope she can enjoy the new freedoms that this position 
affords, such as cycling, attending Miami Dolphin games, and spending 
time with friends and family.
  Once more, Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Chief Henderson on her 
retirement and wish her a life full of happiness.

                          ____________________
```
