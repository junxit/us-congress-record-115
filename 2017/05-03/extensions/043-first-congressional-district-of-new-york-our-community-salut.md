# FIRST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT OF NEW YORK OUR COMMUNITY SALUTES HONOREE LIST

**Speaking:** Zeldin, Lee M. (Z000017) [R-NY]

```

  Mr. ZELDIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay a special tribute to 42 
exemplary young men and women from the First Congressional District of 
New York who have chosen to serve our country in various branches of 
the Armed Forces of the United States. These courageous individuals 
will be dedicating at least their immediate future to the security and 
welfare of their fellow citizens. I admire their strong sense of 
patriotism and desire to protect our values and make certain we remain 
the great nation we are today.
```
