# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 4, 2017

```
                    ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 4, 2017

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Thursday, May 4; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time

[[Page 6428]]

for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and 
morning business be closed; finally, that following leader remarks, the 
Senate resume consideration of the House message to accompany H.R. 244; 
finally, that at 10:30 a.m., Senator Young be recognized for up to 20 
minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
