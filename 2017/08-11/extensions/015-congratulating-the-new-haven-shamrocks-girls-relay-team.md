# CONGRATULATING THE NEW HAVEN SHAMROCKS GIRLS RELAY TEAM

```
        CONGRATULATING THE NEW HAVEN SHAMROCKS GIRLS RELAY TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 11, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the New Haven Shamrocks Girls Relay Team on 
their first place win in the Class 1 4x400 meter relay at the 2017 
Missouri Track and Field State Championship.
  This team and their coach should be commended for all of their hard 
work throughout this past year and for bringing home the state 
championship to their school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing the New Haven Shamrocks Girls 
Relay Team on a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
