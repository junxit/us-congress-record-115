# CONGRATULATING MIKAYLA REED OF THE WASHINGTON BLUE JAYS

```
        CONGRATULATING MIKAYLA REED OF THE WASHINGTON BLUE JAYS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 11, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Mikayla Reed of the Washington Blue Jays for 
her first place win in the 1600 and 3200 meter run at the 2017 Missouri 
Class 4 Girls Track and Field State Championship.
  Mikayla and her coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for bringing home the state championship 
to their school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing Mikayla Reed on a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
