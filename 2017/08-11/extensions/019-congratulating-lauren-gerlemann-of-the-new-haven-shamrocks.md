# CONGRATULATING LAUREN GERLEMANN OF THE NEW HAVEN SHAMROCKS

```
       CONGRATULATING LAUREN GERLEMANN OF THE NEW HAVEN SHAMROCKS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 11, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Lauren Gerlemann of the New Haven Shamrocks 
for her first place win in the long jump at the 2017 Missouri Class 1 
Girls Track and Field State Championship.
  Lauren and her coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for bringing home the state championship 
to their school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing Lauren Gerlemann on a job well 
done.
```
