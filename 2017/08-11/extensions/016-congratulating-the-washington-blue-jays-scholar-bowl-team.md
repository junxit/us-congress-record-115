# CONGRATULATING THE WASHINGTON BLUE JAYS SCHOLAR BOWL TEAM

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
       CONGRATULATING THE WASHINGTON BLUE JAYS SCHOLAR BOWL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 11, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Washington Blue Jays Scholar Bowl Team 
for their first place win in the 2017 Missouri Class 4 Scholar Bowl 
State Championship.
   This team and their coach should be commended for all of their hard 
work throughout this past year and for bringing home the state 
championship to their school and community.
   I ask you to join me in recognizing the Washington Blue Jays Scholar 
Bowl team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
