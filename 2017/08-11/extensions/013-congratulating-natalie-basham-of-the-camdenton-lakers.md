# CONGRATULATING NATALIE BASHAM OF THE CAMDENTON LAKERS

```
         CONGRATULATING NATALIE BASHAM OF THE CAMDENTON LAKERS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 11, 2017

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Natalie Basham of the Camdenton Lakers for 
her first place win in the 100 Meter Hurdles at the 2017 Missouri Class 
4 Girls Track and Field State Championship.
  Natalie and her coach should be commended for all of their hard work 
throughout this past year and for bringing home the state championship 
to their school and community.
  I ask you to join me in recognizing Natalie Basham on a job well 
done.

                          ____________________
```
