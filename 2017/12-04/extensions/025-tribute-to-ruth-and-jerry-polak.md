# TRIBUTE TO RUTH AND JERRY POLAK

**Speaking:** Young, David (Y000066) [R-IA]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO RUTH AND JERRY POLAK

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID YOUNG

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, December 4, 2017

  Mr. YOUNG of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Ruth and Jerry Polak of Council Bluffs, Iowa on the very 
special occasion of their 70th wedding anniversary. They were married 
on September 29, 1947 in Minnesota.
  Ruth and Jerry's lifelong commitment to each other and their family 
truly embodies Iowa values. As they reflect on their 70th anniversary, 
may their commitment grow even stronger, as they continue to love, 
cherish, and honor one another for many years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend this great couple on their 70th year together, 
and I wish them many more. I ask that my colleagues in the United 
States House of Representatives join me in congratulating them on this 
momentous occasion and in wishing them nothing but the best.

                          ____________________
```
