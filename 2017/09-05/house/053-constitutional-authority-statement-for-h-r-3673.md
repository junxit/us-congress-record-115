# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3673

**Measures:** H.R. 3673

```
           By Mr. COFFMAN:
       H.R. 3673.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 states that ``The Congress 
     shall have the power to regulate commerce with foreign 
     Nations, and among several States, and with the Indian 
     Tribes.''
```
