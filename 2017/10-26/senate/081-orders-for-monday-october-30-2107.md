# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 30, 2107

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 30, 2107

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
October 30; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; finally, that following leader 
remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session and resume 
consideration of the McFadden nomination under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
