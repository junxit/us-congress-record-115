# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4151

**Measures:** H.R. 4151

```
            By Mrs. COMSTOCK:
        H.R. 4151.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (Interstate Commerce 
     Clause). The United States Congress shall have the power ``To 
     regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the several 
     States, and with the Indian Tribes.''
```
