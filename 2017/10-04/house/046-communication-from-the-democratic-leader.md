# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                                  October 4, 2017.
     Hon. Paul Ryan,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives, U.S. Capitol, 
         Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Ryan: Pursuant to section 3 of the Alyce 
     Spotted Bear and Walter Soboleff Commission on Native 
     Children Act (Pub. L. 114-244), I am pleased to appoint Dr. 
     Dolores Subia BigFoot of Norman, Oklahoma to the Alyce 
     Spotted Bear and Walter Soboleff Commission on Native 
     Children Commission.

           Sincerely,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
```
