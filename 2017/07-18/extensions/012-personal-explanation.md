                         HON. YVETTE D. CLARKE

  Ms. CLARKE of New York. Mr. Speaker, on July 17, 2017, I was

been present,

Regulation Compact, I would have voted YEA; and
  on Roll Call No. 381, H.J. Res. 76, Granting the consent and approval 
of Congress for the Commonwealth of Virginia, the State of Maryland, 
and the District of Columbia to enter into a compact relating to the 
establishment of the Washington Metrorail Safety Commission, I would 
have voted YEA.
