# GEORGE TAKACS

**Speaking:** Rooney, Francis (R000607) [R-FL]

```
                             GEORGE TAKACS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. FRANCIS ROONEY

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 13, 2017

  Mr. FRANCIS ROONEY of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in 
recognition of George Takacs, a student at Gulf Coast High School in 
Naples, who recently committed to play football at the University of 
Notre Dame. By signing his commitment, George fulfilled a lifelong 
dream to attend the University he has admired throughout his life.
   Selected as an Army All-American, George has proven his dedication 
to the sport and achieving his goals. He will be on the field with some 
of the finest athletes in the country, while pursuing his academic 
goals with some of the finest scholars in the world.
   I congratulate George on his perseverance and look forward to seeing 
what he will accomplish in South Bend over the course of his collegiate 
career.

                          ____________________
```
